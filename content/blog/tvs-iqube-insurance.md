## Best Add-ons for TVS iQube Insurance

### 1. Zero Depreciation Cover ⭐ Most Important

TVS iQube mein fiber body panels, LED headlamp, digital dashboard — sab depreciation ke subject hain.

- **Cost**: ₹800-₹1,400 per year
- **Depreciation without zero dep**: Plastic 50%, Fiber 30%, Rubber 50%, Glass 0%
- **With zero dep**: Full claim amount — no depreciation deduction
- **Must-have for**: All iQube owners, especially new scooters

### 2. Battery Cover Add-on

iQube ki battery warranty 3 years/50,000 km hai. Uske baad:

- **Cost**: ₹1,000-₹1,800 per year
- **Coverage**: Battery capacity below 70%, accidental battery damage
- **Battery replacement cost**: ₹35,000-₹50,000
- **Best for**: Scooters older than 2 years

### 3. Personal Accident Cover

- **Cost**: ₹300-₹500 per year
- **Coverage**: ₹15 Lakh for death/disability
- **IRDAI Mandatory**: Yes, for all two-wheeler owners

### 4. Return to Invoice

- **Cost**: ₹400-₹700 per year
- **Coverage**: Full invoice value in case of theft (not depreciated IDV)
- **Best for**: New iQube owners (first 2 years)

### 5. Charging Damage Protection

- **Cost**: ₹250-₹500 per year
- **Coverage**: Fire, electrical surge, short-circuit during charging
- **Important**: TVS home charger installation ke liye ye cover recommended hai

### 6. Engine Protector (Motor Cover)

- **Cost**: ₹600-₹1,000 per year
- **Coverage**: Electric motor burnout, controller failure, water damage to motor
- **Note**: EVs mein engine protector = motor + controller + inverter cover

## TVS iQube Claim Process

### Step-by-Step Cashless Claim

1. **Immediate Action**: Accident ke baad insurer ko 24-48 hours mein inform karein
2. **TVS Service Center**: Nearest TVS iQube authorized center par scooter le jaayein
3. **Documentation**: RC, DL, policy copy, damage photos, FIR (if applicable)
4. **Surveyor Visit**: Insurer surveyor service center par aayega inspect karne
5. **Battery Assessment**: iQube ke connected app se battery health data extract karein
6. **Repair Authorization**: Surveyor approval ke baad repair start
7. **Cashless Settlement**: Insurer directly service center ko pay karega
8. **Deductibles**: Compulsory + voluntary deductible aapko pay karna hoga

### Reimbursement Claim Process

Agar aap non-network garage mein repair karwa rahe hain:

1. Insurer ko inform karein
2. Photos aur documents submit karein
3. Surveyor approval le bina repair start mat karein
4. Repair ke baad bills, payment proof, aur job card submit karein
5. 7-15 days mein reimbursement milega

### iQube-Specific Claim Tips

- **TVS service network** India mein bahut vast hai — cashless claim easier hai
- **Connected app data** (ride history, battery status) claim evidence mein useful
- **Flood-prone areas** mein water damage claim ke liye immediate action zaroori
- **Dashboard/screen damage** bahut common hai — zero dep ke bina heavy depreciation
- **TVS warranty claims** aur insurance claims separately file karein — dono possible hain

## TVS iQube Insurance Renewal Guide

### Renewal Checklist

- [ ] Current policy document ready

- [ ] Battery health check report
- [ ] Compare 3-4 insurers online
- [ ] Verify IDV — ensure it's not too low

## FAQs

### 1. TVS iQube ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

TVS iQube ka third-party premium ₹1,414 fixed hai (IRDAI EV discount included). Comprehensive insurance ₹3,000-₹5,800 per year hota hai variant aur add-ons ke hisaab se. Zero dep ke saath total ₹3,800-₹5,800 tak jata hai. ST variant ka premium Std se thoda zyada hoga kyunki IDV zyada hai.

### 2. Kya TVS iQube ki battery warranty ke baad insurance cover karega?

Haan, agar aapke paas **Battery Cover Add-on** hai toh warranty ke baad bhi battery degradation cover hoga. Battery cover add-on tabhi kaam aata hai jab warranty expire ho jati hai aur battery capacity 70% se neeche girti hai. Ye add-on ₹1,000-₹1,800 per year mein milta hai.

### 3. iQube ke liye zero dep add-on zaroori hai?

Haan, strongly recommended! TVS iQube mein fiber body panels, LED lights, aur digital dashboard sab depreciation ke subject hain. Zero dep ke bina claim settlement mein 30-50% deduction hota hai plastic aur fiber parts par. ₹800-₹1,400 ka annual investment claim mein ₹8,000-₹15,000 bacha sakta hai.

### 4. TVS iQube chori ho jaaye toh kya mileagega?

Comprehensive insurance ke under theft cover hai — aapko IDV mileagegi. Agar **Return to Invoice** add-on hai toh full ex-showroom price mileagegi. Bina is add-on ke depreciated IDV milegi jo significantly kam ho sakti hai purane scooter mein. Ye add-on especially useful hai first 2 years ke liye.

### 5. Kya TVS service center par cashless claim hota hai?

Haan, most major insurance companies TVS authorized service centers ke saath cashless tie-up rakhti hain. Cashless claim mein aapko sirf deductible amount pay karna hota hai, baaki insurer directly service center ko pay karta hai. TVS ki vast service network ke karan cashless claim easy hota hai.

### 6. iQube insurance renewal ke time kya documents chahiye?

Renewal ke liye: existing policy document, RC copy, aur previous year NCB certificate (if applicable). Online renewal mein sirf registration number aur mobile number se ho jata hai. Agar insurer change kar rahe hain toh NCB certificate previous insurer se zaroor lein — 50% tak discount transfer hota hai.
