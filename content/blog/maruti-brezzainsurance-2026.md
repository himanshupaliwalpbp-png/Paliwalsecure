## FAQ

### Q: How much does Maruti Brezza insurance cost?
**A:** Comprehensive: ₹6,000-12,000/year (TP ₹2,094 + OD ₹4,000-10,000). Zero-dep add-on: ₹2,000-3,500/year. Premium varies by variant, city, NCB, add-ons.

### Q: What is the IDV for Maruti Brezza?
**A:** New: ₹7.5-12L (depending on variant — LXi to ZXi+). 3-year-old: ₹5.2-8.4L. 5-year-old: ₹3.7-6L. IDV = max payout if stolen/totalled.

### Q: Is zero-depreciation worth it for Brezza?
**A:** Yes. Brezza parts (LED DRLs, infotainment, sensors) are expensive. Zero-dep costs ₹2,000-3,500/year but saves ₹5,000-15,000 per major claim. Essential for first 5 years.

### Q: Where to buy Brezza insurance?
**A:** Buy online (insurer website or POSP like Paliwal Secure) for 10-20% discount. Maruti dealer insurance is typically 15-25% more expensive (dealer commission). You can refuse dealer insurance — it's your legal right.

### Q: Can I switch insurers at Brezza renewal?
**A:** Yes. At renewal, compare quotes from 3+ insurers. Your NCB (up to 50%) transfers. Use POSP for free comparison + claim assistance.
