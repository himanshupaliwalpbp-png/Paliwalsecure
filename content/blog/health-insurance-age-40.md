## Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) — The Most Important Factor at 40

40 saal tak aapke paas koi na koi medical condition ho sakti hai. PED declaration **sabse important step** hai insurance lene mein.

### What Counts as PED?

- Diabetes (Type 1 ya Type 2)
- High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)
- Thyroid disorders (Hypo/Hyper)
- Asthma / Respiratory issues
- Heart conditions
- Any surgery in past
- PCOD/PCOS in women
- Kidney stones (recurring)

## FAQs

### 1. 40 ki age mein kitna health insurance cover chahiye?

Minimum ₹15 lakh, ideally ₹25 lakh ka cover lein. 40 ke baad serious illness ka risk badh jaata hai — cancer treatment ₹15-25 lakh, heart surgery ₹8-15 lakh tak lag jaata hai. ₹25 lakh + super top-up ka combo best strategy hai.

### 2. Agar mujhe diabetes/BP hai toh insurance milega?

Haan, milega! PED (Pre-existing Disease) declare karein — 3-4 saal ki waiting period ke baad cover hoga. Kuch specialized plans jaise Star Diabetes Safe, Care Heart mein PED ka better coverage milta hai. Kabhi disease chhupana nahi — claim reject ho jaayega.

### 3. 40 ki age mein premium kitna hoga?

₹15 lakh cover ke liye ₹1,000-1,600/month (without PED), ₹1,200-2,000/month (with PED). Yeh 30 ki age se 40-60% zyada hai, lekin 50 ki age se 30-40% kam hai — toh abhi buy karein!

### 4. Health check-up benefit kya hota hai?

Achhe health insurance plans mein har saal free health check-up milta hai — blood tests, ECG, thyroid profile, etc. Yeh ₹3,000-5,000 ka value hai aur early detection mein help karta hai. Claim se koi effect nahi padta.

### 5. Kya critical illness cover health insurance se alag lena chahiye?

Haan, zaroor lein! Health insurance hospital bills cover karta hai, lekin critical illness cover diagnosis pe lump sum deta hai — chahe treatment ho ya na ho. Yeh income replacement ke liye hai — recovery period mein earning stop ho jaati hai, tab yeh amount kaam aata hai.

### 6. Super top-up kya hai aur 40 mein kyun chahiye?

Super top-up ek extra cover hai jo ₹5-10 lakh deductible ke baad activate hota hai. Premium bahut kam hota hai — ₹25 lakh super top-up sirf ₹300-500/month mein mil jaata hai. Base plan + super top-up = ₹40-50 lakh total coverage at affordable price.
