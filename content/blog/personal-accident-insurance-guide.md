## Key Takeaway

Personal Accident (PA) insurance ek aisi policy hai jo specifically accidents se hone wale financial loss ko cover karti hai — accidental death, permanent disability, temporary disability, aur medical expenses. Yeh life insurance aur health insurance se ALAG hai! Life insurance death pe paisa deta hai (karan nahi dekhta), health insurance hospital bills deta hai, lekin PA insurance specifically accident-related losses cover karta hai — disability monthly income, partial disability lump sum, accident medical expenses, etc. Premium sirf ₹500-₹3,000/year for ₹25-50 Lakh cover! India mein har saal 5 lakh+ accidents hote hain — PA insurance ek chhote premium mein bada protection deta hai.

## TL;DR — Key Points

- **PA insurance covers accidental death, disability, and medical expenses**
- **Premium: ₹500-₹3,000/year** for ₹25-50 Lakh cover — cheapest insurance!
- **Covers disability** — which life and health insurance DON'T adequately cover
- **No medical test required** up to age 65-70
- **24/7 worldwide coverage** — accidents anywhere, anytime
- **NOT the same as life insurance** — PA is accident-specific
- **NOT the same as health insurance** — PA covers income loss from disability

## What is Personal Accident Insurance?

Personal Accident Insurance is a policy that provides financial compensation in case of accidental death, permanent total disability, permanent partial disability, or temporary total disability. It also covers medical expenses arising from accidents.

### PA Insurance Kya Hai? (Hindi)

Personal Accident insurance woh policy hai jo accident se hone wale financial loss ko cover karti hai. Agar accident mein death ho jaaye toh lump sum payment, permanent disability ho toh lump sum, temporary disability ho toh weekly allowance, aur medical expenses bhi cover hote hain. Sabse bada fayda: yeh disability cover karta hai — jo life insurance nahi karta (life insurance sirf death pe deta hai).

## Types of PA Insurance Coverage

### 1. Accidental Death Cover
Full sum insured paid to nominee if death occurs due to accident within policy period.

**Example:** ₹50 Lakh PA policy → Accident death → Nominee receives ₹50 Lakh

### 2. Permanent Total Disability (PTD)
Full sum insured paid if accident causes complete and permanent disability.

| Condition | Payout |
|-----------|--------|
| Loss of both limbs | 100% of SI |
| Loss of both eyes | 100% of SI |
| Loss of one limb + one eye | 100% of SI |
| Permanent paralysis | 100% of SI |
| Loss of hearing (both ears) | 100% of SI |

### 3. Permanent Partial Disability (PPD)
Percentage of sum insured paid based on extent of disability.

| Condition | Payout (% of SI) |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Loss of one limb | 50% |
| Loss of one eye | 50% |
| Loss of one ear | 25% |
| Loss of thumb | 20% |
| Loss of index finger | 10% |
| Loss of hearing (one ear) | 25% |
| Loss of toe | 5% |

### 4. Temporary Total Disability (TTD)
Weekly allowance paid if accident temporarily prevents you from working.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Weekly benefit | 1% of SI per week (typically) |
| Maximum duration | 100-104 weeks |
| Maximum payout | 100% of SI |
| Example | ₹50L SI → ₹5,000/week for up to 100 weeks |

### 5. Accident Medical Expenses
Covers medical treatment cost for accident injuries.

| Plan | Medical Expense Cover |
|------|----------------------|
| Basic | Not included |
| Standard | Up to ₹1,00,000 |
| Comprehensive | Up to ₹5,00,000 |
| Premium | Up to ₹10,00,000 |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. PA insurance kya hota hai?
Personal Accident insurance accident se hone wale financial loss ko cover karta hai — accidental death, disability (permanent/temporary), aur medical expenses. Yeh life insurance se alag hai kyunki yeh disability bhi cover karta hai, aur health insurance se alag hai kyunki yeh income loss bhi cover karta hai. (Durbhagya beema durghatna se nuksan cover karta hai)

### 2. Kya PA insurance mein medical test hota hai?
Generally nahi! Most PA policies up to age 65-70 bina medical test ke mil jaati hain. Yeh sabse easily accessible insurance hai. Only high sum insured (₹1 Crore+) ke liye medical test ho sakta hai.

### 3. Kya car/bike accident PA se covered hai?
Haan! Road accidents are the most common PA claims. Chahe car, bike, ya pedestrian — sabhi road accidents covered hain. Yeh PA insurance ka primary use case hai.

### 4. Kya sports injury PA se covered hai?
Professional sports injuries generally excluded hain. Lekh recreational sports injuries covered ho sakti hain — check policy wording. Adventure sports (skydiving, bungee jumping) mostly excluded.

### 5. PA insurance ka claim kaise file karein?
FIR copy (if road accident), medical reports, doctor's certificate for disability, discharge summary, aur photographs (if applicable). Temporary disability ke liye weekly medical certificates chahiye hote hain.

### 6. Kya employer ka PA cover sufficient hai?
Partially! Employer PA typically basic cover deta hai (₹5-10 Lakh). Aapki actual need ₹25-50 Lakh ho sakti hai. Plus, job change pe employer PA khatam. Apna personal PA bhi rakho.
