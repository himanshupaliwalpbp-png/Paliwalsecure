## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for KTM Duke

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! KTM ke premium parts (trellis frame body panels, USD fork covers, TFT display area) bahut expensive hain — zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai
2. **Personal Accident Cover** — ₹15 Lakh coverage (owner-driver) — high-performance bike ke liye must
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹3,500-₹6,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — KTM owners often long rides pe jaate hain, essential add-on
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹1.8-3 Lakh ki premium bike ke liye must

## 📋 KTM Duke Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. KTM authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — KTM Duke Insurance

**Q: KTM Duke ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, KTM Duke 200 (150cc-350cc) ka third-party premium ₹1,700/year hai 2026 mein. Duke 390 (above 350cc) ke liye ₹2,900/year lagega.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Bilkul haan! KTM ke parts Austrian technology ke hain aur bahut expensive hain. Duke 390 ka side fairing ₹6,000-₹10,000, fuel tank ₹5,000-₹8,000, aur headlamp assembly ₹4,000-₹7,000. Zero dep must-have hai!

**Q: KTM Duke ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Duke ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹1,400-₹6,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: KTM Duke ke liye Bajaj Allianz better hai kya?**
A: Bajaj Allianz Bajaj Group ke under hai aur KTM Bajaj ka subsidiary hai, toh unke paas KTM ke parts aur service ka better access ho sakta hai. Lekin premium compare karein — kabhi kabhi doosre insurers better rate dete hain.
