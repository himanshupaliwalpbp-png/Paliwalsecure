## FAQ

### Q: Which has highest CSR?
**A:** TATA AIA: 99.1% — highest among major life insurers. HDFC Life: 98.6%. Both excellent.

### Q: Which is cheaper?
**A:** Comparable (5-10% difference). HDFC Life often slightly cheaper for online term. Get quotes from both.

### Q: Which has better riders?
**A:** HDFC Life: more options including Life Stage Benefit (increase cover at marriage/childbirth without medical). TATA AIA: standard riders.

### Q: Are both safe?
**A:** Yes. TATA AIA backed by TATA + AIA Group (Hong Kong). HDFC Life backed by HDFC Bank. Both solvency above 1.5x.

### Q: Which has better customer service?
**A:** HDFC Life: better digital (app, WhatsApp). TATA AIA: strong branch + phone. Tech-savvy: HDFC. In-person: TATA AIA.
