## Air Pollution Cover — Delhi Ki Sabse Badi Zaroorat

Delhi ki air pollution ki wajah se yeh health issues common hain:

### Pollution-Related Illnesses Covered

- ✅ Asthma attacks aur respiratory distress
- ✅ Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
- ✅ Bronchitis (acute aur chronic)
- ✅ Pneumonia
- ✅ Allergic rhinitis
- ✅ Eye irritation aur conjunctivitis (hospitalization required)
- ✅ Lung infections

### Kya Pollution-Related Treatment Cover Hota Hai?

| Coverage | Yes/No | Condition |
|----------|:------:|-----------|
| Hospitalization for asthma | ✅ | If hospitalized for 24+ hours |
| OPD for respiratory issues | ⚠️ | Only with OPD add-on |

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** OPD add-on leina Delhi mein bahut useful hai kyunki zyada respiratory problems OPD level pe hi treat hoti hain, hospitalization nahi hoti.

## FAQs

### 1. Kya Delhi ki air pollution se hui bimari ka insurance claim hota hai?

Haan, agar hospitalization hui hai (24+ hours) toh respiratory diseases jo pollution se hui hain — asthma, bronchitis, pneumonia — sab cover hote hain. Lekin OPD treatment ke liye OPD add-on chahiye. Preventive measures jaise air purifier insurance se cover nahi hote.

### 2. Dengue ke liye health insurance mein kya cover hota hai?

Dengue ke liye hospitalization expenses — room rent, doctor fees, blood tests, IV fluids, medications, aur platelet transfusion — sab cover hote hain. Cashless treatment available hai Delhi ke most major hospitals mein. Average dengue claim ₹30,000-80,000 tak ka hota hai.

### 3. Delhi mein health insurance ka minimum sum insured kitna hona chahiye?

Delhi ke hospital costs ke hisaab se **minimum ₹10 lakh** ka cover chahiye. AIIMS-type government hospitals ke alawa, private hospitals mein ICU ₹15,000-30,000/night, surgery ₹2-10 lakh tak ka ho sakta hai. Family ke liye ₹15-25 lakh recommended hai.

### 4. Kya NCR (Gurgaon, Noida) ke hospitals bhi Delhi health insurance mein cover hote hain?

Haan! Delhi-NCR ke hospitals ek hi network mein aate hain. Gurgaon ke Medanta, Fortis Memorial aur Noida ke Max, Fortis, Jaypee — sab network hospitals hain most insurers ke. Cashless treatment yahan bhi available hai.

### 5. Corporate health insurance kafi hai ya personal bhi lena chahiye Delhi mein?

Corporate insurance usually ₹3-5 lakh ka hota hai — Delhi mein yeh enough nahi hai. Corporate cover change ho sakta hai job change pe, aur family coverage bhi limited hota hai. Isliye **personal health insurance zaroor leina** — corporate ke upar supplement ke taur pe.

### 6. Delhi mein senior citizens ke liye best health insurance kaunsa hai?

Senior citizens (60+) ke liye Care Health, Star Health, aur Niva Bupa ke plans best hain. Yeh pre-existing diseases ke baad bhi cover karte hain (waiting period ke baad), aur Delhi ke major hospitals mein cashless facility dete hain. Premium ₹15,000-30,000/year hogi.
