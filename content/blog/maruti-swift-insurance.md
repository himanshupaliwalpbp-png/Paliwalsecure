### NCB Protection Tips

- **Small repairs**: ₹3,000 se kam ke repair — out-of-pocket pay karein, NCB bachega
- **NCB certificate**: Insurer change karne par NCB transfer hota hai — certificate zaroor lein
- **NCB protection add-on**: ₹800-₹1,500 mein 2 claims/year allowed without losing NCB
- **Lapsed policy**: Policy lapse hone par NCB zero ho jata hai — time par renew karein

## Swift Insurance Claim Process

### Cashless Claim at Maruti Service Center

1. **Inform insurer**: 24-48 hours mein call karein
2. **Maruti Arena service center**: Nearest cashless network garage
3. **Survey**: Surveyor inspect karega damage
4. **Repair approval**: Estimate approve hone ke baad repair start
5. **Payment**: Aap sirf deductible + non-covered items pay karein
6. **Delivery**: Car ready ho jayegi

### Reimbursement Claim

1. Insurer ko inform karein
2. Any garage mein repair karayein
3. Bills, payment proof, job card submit karein
4. 7-15 days mein reimbursement

### Claim Settlement Tips

- **Maruti genuine parts**: Always insist on genuine parts — insurance mein covered hain
- **Cashless preferred**: Less hassle, faster settlement
- **Photos zaroor lein**: Before, during, after repair
- **FIR**: Third-party injury/property damage mein mandatory
- **Small claims avoid**: NCB bachane ke liiple ₹3,000-₹5,000 se kam ke claims avoid karein

## FAQs

### 1. Maruti Swift ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Swift ka comprehensive insurance ₹6,000-₹15,000 per year hota hai variant aur IDV ke hisaab se. LXi base model ke liye ₹6,000-₹9,000, ZXi+ top model ke liye ₹9,000-₹15,000. Third-party premium ₹4,800-₹5,200 fixed hai. Zero dep add-on ke saalt total ₹8,000-₹15,500 tak jata hai.

### 2. Swift ke liye zero dep add-on lena chahiye?

Haan, bilkul! Swift ke parts (bumper, headlamp, fender) replacement mein zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai. ₹1,500-₹3,000 ka annual add-on ek claim mein ₹5,000-₹15,000 bacha sakta hai. Swift jaise popular car ke liye zero dep must-have hai.

### 3. NCB kaise kaam karta hai aur kitna fayda hota hai?

NCB (No Claim Bonus) claim-free year par milta hai — pehla saal 20%, aur 5 consecutive claim-free years par 50% tak jata hai. ₹10,000 premium par 50% NCB = ₹5,000 saving per year. NCB insurer change karne par bhi transfer hota hai — certificate le kar naye insurer ko dijiye.

### 4. Swift CNG ka insurance alag hota hai?

Swift CNG factory-fitted hai toh insurance premium thoda zyada hoti hai kyunki IDV zyada hai (CNG kit included). Agar aftermarket CNG fit kiya hai toh CNG kit ka separate endorsement insurance mein add karna padega — premium ₹500-₹1,500 extra lagega. Bina endorsement ke CNG-related damage claim nahi hoga.

### 5. Kaunsa insurance company Swift ke liye best hai?

Swift ke liiple **Tata AIG** (best claim ratio 98.2%), **Acko** (fastest settlement), aur **Digit** (lowest premium) best options hain. Maruti ke vast service network ke karan most insurers cashless settlement offer karte hain. Apne city mein cashless network density check karein before choosing.

### 6. Insurance renewal ke time IDV kaise set karein?

IDV = Ex-showroom price minus depreciation. New Swift ke liye 95% of ex-showroom, har saal decreasing. Insurer ka default IDV accept mat karein — negotiate karein. Kam IDV = kam premium lekin kam claim; zyada IDV = zyada premium lekin better coverage. Fair market value par IDV set karein.
