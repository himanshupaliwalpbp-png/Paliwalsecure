## Key Takeaway

Car insurance add-ons (aapoorn rugs) extra benefits hain jo aap apni comprehensive car insurance mein premium pay karke jod sakte hain. Sabse zaroori add-ons hain: Zero Depreciation (₹2,000-5,000/year, saves ₹30,000-50,000/claim), Engine Protect (₹500-1,500/year, covers ₹50,000+ engine damage), aur Return to Invoice (₹1,000-2,000/year, full car value on theft/total loss). Kuch add-ons waste of money bhi hain — jaise key replacement agar aapke paas duplicate key hai. Har add-on ka purpose alag hai, isliye apni need ke hisaab se choose karo, agent ki selling mat suno!

## TL;DR — Key Points

- **Zero Depreciation**: MUST for cars under 5 years — saves ₹30,000-₹50,000 per claim
- **Engine Protect**: MUST for monsoon/flood-prone areas — covers hydrostatic lock, water damage
- **Return to Invoice**: Great for new/expensive cars — get full invoice value on theft/total loss
- **NCB Protector**: Worth it if you have 50% NCB — protects your discount after 1 claim
- **Consumables Cover**: ₹800-1,500/year — covers oil, coolant, nuts & bolts
- **Personal Belongings**: Optional — covers theft of items from car
- **Daily Allowance**: Optional — ₹500-1,000/day while car is in garage

## All Car Insurance Add-Ons Explained

### 1. Zero Depreciation Add-On (Sabse Zaroori)

Zero depreciation (bina ghatav) add-on ensures that during a claim, the insurer does not deduct depreciation on replaced parts. Without this, you lose 5-50% of claim amount.

| Part Type | Normal Depreciation | With Zero Dep |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Rubber/Plastic/Nylon | 50% | 0% |
| Fiber Glass | 30% | 0% |
| Metal (0-6 months) | 5% | 0% |
| Metal (6mo-1yr) | 10% | 0% |
| Metal (1-2yr) | 15% | 0% |
| Metal (2-3yr) | 25% | 0% |
| Metal (3+yr) | 30-40% | 0% |

**Cost:** ₹2,000-5,000/year | **Savings per claim:** ₹30,000-₹50,000
**Verdict:** ✅ MUST BUY for cars under 5 years

### 2. Engine Protect Add-On

Engine damage is NOT covered under standard comprehensive insurance. This add-on covers:

- Hydrostatic lock (engine mein paani ghusna during flooding)
- Oil leakage damage
- Consequential engine damage from accidents
- Gearbox damage
- Connecting rod damage

**Real scenario:** Mumbai floods mein car mein paani ghus gaya. Engine repair cost: ₹80,000. Without engine protect — ZERO coverage. With engine protect — fully covered.

**Cost:** ₹500-1,500/year | **Savings:** ₹30,000-₹1,50,000
**Verdict:** ✅ MUST BUY for flood-prone cities (Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi, Bangalore)

### 3. Return to Invoice (RTI) Add-On

Standard comprehensive pays IDV (which is less than what you paid). RTI pays the full invoice value including registration, road tax, and accessories.

| Scenario | Without RTI | With RTI |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Car stolen (₹10L invoice) | IDV ₹7L (70%) | ₹10L (full invoice) |
| Total loss accident | IDV only | Invoice + registration + road tax |
| Car age limit | N/A | Up to 3 years typically |

**Cost:** ₹1,000-2,500/year | **Savings:** ₹1,00,000-₹3,00,000
**Verdict:** ✅ HIGHLY RECOMMENDED for new cars (0-3 years)

### 4. NCB Protector Add-On

NCB (No Claim Bonus) can reach 50% after 5 claim-free years. One claim and it drops to 0%. NCB Protector allows you to make 1-2 claims without losing your NCB.

| Year | NCB Without Claim | NCB After 1 Claim (No Protector) | NCB After 1 Claim (With Protector) |
|------|-------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Year 1 | 20% | 0% | 20% ✅ |
| Year 2 | 25% | 0% | 25% ✅ |
| Year 3 | 35% | 0% | 35% ✅ |
| Year 4 | 45% | 0% | 45% ✅ |
| Year 5 | 50% | 0% | 50% ✅ |

**Cost:** ₹800-2,000/year | **Savings:** Up to 50% of OD premium
**Verdict:** ✅ WORTH IT if you have 35%+ NCB

### 5. Consumables Cover

Covers items that are used up during repair — engine oil, coolant, brake oil, nuts, bolts, screws, washer, AC gas, grease, and filter.

**Cost:** ₹800-1,500/year | **Typical savings per claim:** ₹3,000-₹8,000
**Verdict:** ✅ GOOD VALUE — small cost, useful benefit

### 6. Personal Belongings Cover

Covers theft of personal items from your locked car — laptop, phone, bags, etc.

**Cost:** ₹500-1,000/year | **Coverage:** ₹10,000-₹25,000
**Verdict:** ⚠️ OPTIONAL — check if your home insurance already covers this

### 7. Daily Allowance / Loss of Use

Pays a daily allowance (₹500-₹1,000/day) while your car is in the garage for repairs after an accident.

**Cost:** ₹500-800/year | **Coverage:** 10-15 days max
**Verdict:** ⚠️ OPTIONAL — only if you heavily depend on your car daily

### 8. Roadside Assistance (RSA)

Covers emergency services: towing, flat tire, battery jumpstart, fuel delivery, key lockout assistance.

**Cost:** ₹500-1,000/year | **Coverage:** 24/7 pan-India
**Verdict:** ✅ RECOMMENDED — very useful for highway travelers

### 9. Personal Accident Cover for Passengers

Covers accidental death/disability for unnamed passengers in your car.

**Cost:** ₹300-600/year | **Coverage:** ₹2,00,000 per passenger
**Verdict:** ⚠️ OPTIONAL — if you regularly carry passengers, consider it

### 10. Key Replacement Cover

Covers cost of replacing lost or stolen car keys including reprogramming.

**Cost:** ₹300-500/year | **Coverage:** ₹10,000-₹20,000
**Verdict:** ⚠️ OPTIONAL — premium cars with smart keys benefit most

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Kaun se add-ons sabse zaroori hain car insurance mein?
Top 3 zaroori add-ons hain: Zero Depreciation (depreciation se bachata hai), Engine Protect (flood/engine damage cover), aur Return to Invoice (theft/total loss pe full value). In teeno ko lane se aapki car nearly fully protected ho jaati hai.

### 2. Kya add-ons mid-policy add kar sakte hain?
Kuch insurers allow karte hain mid-term addition with pro-rata premium. Lekin most insurers renewal pe hi add-ons add karne dete hain. Check with your insurer.

### 3. Kya zero dep aur engine protect same hain?
Nahi! Zero dep depreciation deduction hatata hai accidental damage claims mein. Engine protect engine ke internal damage ko cover karta hai jo standard policy mein excluded hai — jaise hydrostatic lock, oil leakage damage. Dono alag-alag add-ons hain.

### 4. Add-ons ki cost kitni hoti hai?
Total add-ons cost car ki value pe depend karta hai. Maruti Swift ke liye ₹3,500-₹7,000/year, Hyundai Creta ke liye ₹5,000-₹12,000/year, aur luxury cars ke liye ₹10,000-₹25,000/year. Base premium ke upar 30-45% extra aata hai.

### 5. Kya 5 saal purani car mein add-ons mil sakte hain?
Zero dep mostly 5 saal tak milta hai (Go Digit 7 saal tak deta hai). Engine protect, consumables, RSA koi age limit nahi hai. RTI mostly 3 saal tak milta hai. Check with insurer for specific age limits.

### 6. Kya claims pe add-ons ka impact padta hai?
Haan! Zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hota hai. Engine protect ke bina flood damage not covered. RTI ke bina theft pe sirf IDV milta hai. Add-ons significantly increase your claim payout.
