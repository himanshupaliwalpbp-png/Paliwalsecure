## Quick Answer

Pvt Ltd companies need: D&O liability (₹15,000-50,000/year — protects directors), public liability (₹10,000-40,000/year), professional indemnity (₹10,000-30,000/year), fire & burglary (₹10,000-30,000/year), cyber insurance (₹10,000-25,000/year), workers' compensation (₹5,000-20,000/year). Total: ₹60,000-1,95,000/year. One lawsuit without D&O can cost directors personal assets.

## FAQ

### Q: Is D&O insurance mandatory for Pvt Ltd companies?
**A:** Not legally mandatory, but strongly recommended. Directors can be personally liable for company decisions (investor disputes, employee claims, regulatory action). Without D&O, personal assets (home, savings) are at risk. Investors often require D&O before funding rounds.

### Q: What insurance is legally mandatory for Pvt Ltd companies?
**A:** 1) Workers' compensation (if 10+ employees using power, or 20+ without), 2) Third-party motor insurance (for company vehicles), 3) Fire insurance (if required by lender for secured loans). All other insurance is optional but recommended.

### Q: Can Pvt Ltd companies claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All business insurance premiums (D&O, PL, PI, fire, cyber, WC) are deductible under Section 37(1) as business expenses. Reduces taxable company profit. Available under both old and new tax regimes.

### Q: What is the minimum insurance for a new Pvt Ltd company?
**A:** D&O (₹15,000/year) + Public liability (₹10,000/year) + Fire & burglary (₹10,000/year) = ₹35,000/year minimum. Add cyber (₹10,000/year) if you store customer data. Add workers' comp (₹5,000/year) if you have employees. Scale up coverage as the company grows.

### Q: Does Pvt Ltd company insurance cover employees?
**A:** Workers' compensation covers employee injuries on duty. Group health insurance (optional but recommended for retention) covers employee medical expenses. Group term life (optional) provides life cover to employees. These are separate policies — business insurance covers the company, group insurance covers employees.
