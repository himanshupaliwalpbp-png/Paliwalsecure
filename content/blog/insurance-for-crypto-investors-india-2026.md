## Quick Answer

Crypto investors need: health insurance (₹6,000-12,000/year), term life (₹4,000-10,000/year), cyber insurance (₹1,500-5,000/year — exchange hack, wallet breach, phishing). Crypto exchanges may offer limited insurance (e.g., WazirX, CoinDCX have partial coverage), but this does NOT cover personal wallet hacks or exchange insolvency. Buy personal cyber insurance.

## FAQ

### Q: Is my crypto insured if the exchange is hacked?
**A:** Partially. Some Indian exchanges (CoinDCX, WazirX) maintain insurance funds for hot wallet breaches. However, this does NOT cover: cold wallet hacks, exchange insolvency (like FTX), or personal wallet/phishing attacks. Read the exchange's insurance policy carefully. Do NOT assume your crypto is fully insured.

### Q: Can I buy insurance for my crypto wallet?
**A:** Personal cyber insurance covers financial losses from: crypto wallet hack, exchange account breach, phishing, and identity theft. Premium: ₹1,500-5,000/year for ₹10L cover. This covers the FINANCIAL LOSS, not the crypto itself. File FIR + provide transaction evidence when claiming.

### Q: Should crypto investors buy term insurance?
**A:** Yes. Crypto is volatile and illiquid — if you die, your family may not know how to access your wallets (private keys, seed phrases). Term insurance ensures they receive a guaranteed lump sum (₹1Cr+) regardless of crypto market conditions. Include crypto assets in your will + share access details with a trusted family member.

### Q: Can crypto investors claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** If crypto trading is your primary business (intraday, F&O on crypto): cyber insurance may qualify as Section 37(1) business expense. However, crypto taxation in India is complex (30% flat tax + 1% TDS under Section 115BBH). Consult a CA familiar with crypto taxation.

### Q: Does health insurance cover stress-related illnesses from crypto losses?
**A:** Yes. Health insurance covers hospitalization for any reason — including stress-induced cardiac events or mental health (IRDAI mandate 2022). Crypto volatility can cause severe stress — health insurance ensures medical treatment doesn't add to financial burden.
