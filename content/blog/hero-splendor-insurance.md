## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Hero Splendor

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Splendor ke parts replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai — zero dep se poora claim milega
2. **Personal Accident Cover** — ₹15 Lakh coverage (owner-driver) — IRDAI ke according mandatory hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — daily commuter mein chhote accidents common hain
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Splendor daily use bike hai, breakdown risk zyada
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye Splendor ke liye useful

## 📋 Hero Splendor Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Hero MotoCorp authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Hero Splendor Insurance

**Q: Hero Splendor ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Hero Splendor Plus (below 75cc to 150cc) ka third-party premium ₹1,400/year hai 2026 mein. 5-year TP policy ₹7,649 mein bhi available hai.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Splendor ke parts replacement mein zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai. ₹250-₹600/year mein ek claim mein ₹1,000-₹3,000 bacha sakte hain. Value for money hai!

**Q: Hero Splendor ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Splendor ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹640-₹2,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Splendor purani hai — kya insurance lena zaroori hai?**
A: Haan! Third-party insurance legally mandatory hai chahe kitni bhi purani bike ho. Purani Splendor ke liye comprehensive bhi consider karein — theft risk high hai aur repair costs bhi cover hote hain.
