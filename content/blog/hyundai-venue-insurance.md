## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Hyundai Venue

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Venue ke body panels, headlamps, aur bumper replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai bina zero dep ke
2. **Engine Protection** — Turbo engine variants ke liye zaroori — waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover karta hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega, 20-50% discount preserved rahega
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help, towing, flat tire, battery jumpstart — highway drives ke liye useful
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega, naye Venue ke liye smart choice

## 📋 Hyundai Venue Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Hyundai authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Hyundai Venue Insurance

**Q: Hyundai Venue ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Hyundai Venue (1000-1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹6,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Zero dep cover se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Venue ke liye yeh bahut useful hai, especially turbo variants ke expensive parts ke liye. Car 5 saal se purani nahi hai toh must hai.

**Q: Hyundai Venue ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Venue ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹4,800-₹19,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Venue ka insurance renewal ke time kya dhyan rakhein?**
A: IDV verify karein, NCB certificate preserve karein, zero dep add-on continue rakhein, aur kam se kam 3 insurers compare karein. Policy lapse hone se NCB zero ho jata hai!
