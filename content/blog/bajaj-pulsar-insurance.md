## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Bajaj Pulsar

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Pulsar ke sporty body panels, tank shrouds, aur fairing (RS200) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Personal Accident Cover** — ₹15 Lakh coverage (owner-driver) — IRDAI ke according mandatory hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹2,250-₹3,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — performance bike hone ke naate highway rides common hain
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye Pulsar ke liye smart choice

## 📋 Bajaj Pulsar Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Bajaj Pro-biking / KTM authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Bajaj Pulsar Insurance

**Q: Bajaj Pulsar ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Bajaj Pulsar 150-200cc ka third-party premium ₹1,600/year hai 2026 mein. Pulsar N250 (250cc) ke liye ₹2,300/year lagega.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Pulsar ke body panels aur fairing (RS200) expensive hain. Zero dep se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. ₹400-₹900/year mein ek claim mein ₹2,000-₹5,000 bacha sakte hain.

**Q: Bajaj Pulsar ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Pulsar ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹900-₹3,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Pulsar racing/track day use ke liye insurance cover hoga?**
A: Standard insurance racing/track day use cover nahi karta. Agar track pe accident hua toh claim reject ho sakta hai. Track day insurance separate lena padega.
