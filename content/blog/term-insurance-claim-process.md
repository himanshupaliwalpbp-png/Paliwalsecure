## Key Takeaway

Term insurance claim process mein nominee ko insurance company ko death ki intimation deni hai (30 days ke andar), death certificate aur required documents submit karne hain, aur company verify karke sum assured payout karti hai. Natural death mein 15-30 days, accidental death mein 30-60 days, aur early death claim (policy ke 3 years ke andar) mein investigation ke kaaran 90-180 days lagte hain. Key hai — honest disclosure karna policy lete waqt aur complete documents submit karna claim ke time.

## Term Insurance Claim — Kyun Important Hai Samajhna?

Term insurance sabse important financial protection hai — lekin iska fayda tabhi milta hai jab claim smoothly settle ho. Dukhad sach yeh hai ki **India mein approximately 8-10% term insurance claims reject** ho jaate hain, mostly incomplete documents ya wrong information ki wajah se.

Isliye, chahe aap policyholder hon ya nominee — claim process samajhna zaroori hai.

## Term Insurance Death Claim Types

### 1. Natural Death Claim

Jab policyholder ki death bimari ya natural causes se hoti hai:

- Hospital records chahiye
- Doctor ka treatment history
- Death certificate — **cause of death clearly mentioned**
- No investigation (usually) agar policy 3 saal purani hai

### 2. Accidental Death Claim

Jab death accident se hoti hai:

- FIR copy mandatory hai
- Post-mortem report chahiye
- Police investigation report
- Accidental death benefit rider se additional payout mil sakta hai

### 3. Early Death Claim (Less Than 3 Years)

Policy issue hone ke **3 years ke andar** death ho toh:

- Company **investigation** karegi (zaroori hai)
- 90-180 days lag sakte hain
- Har document verify hoga
- Medical history check hogi
- This is standard process — panic mat karein

### Comparison Table: Claim Types

| Feature | Natural Death | Accidental Death | Early Claim (<3 yrs) |
|---------|:------------:|:----------------:|:--------------------:|
| FIR required | ❌ | ✅ | If applicable |
| Post-mortem | ❌ | ✅ | If applicable |
| Investigation | Rare | Yes | Yes (mandatory) |
| Timeline | 15-30 days | 30-60 days | 90-180 days |
| Payout | Sum assured | Sum assured + rider | Sum assured |

## Step-by-Step Term Insurance Claim Process

### Step 1: Death Ki Intimation (30 Days Ke Andar)

Nominee ko insurance company ko **jaldi se jaldi** inform karna chahiye:

**How to intimate:**
- Call helpline number
- Email with policy details
- Visit nearest branch
- Use company's mobile app

**Information needed:**
- Policy number
- Date of death
- Cause of death
- Nominee details
- Contact number

> ⚠️ **Important:** 30 days ke andar intimation nahi ki toh company inquiry kar sakti hai, lekin claim reject nahi hoga sirf late intimation se. But jaldi inform karna better hai.

### Step 2: Claim Form & Documents Prepare Karein

#### Core Documents (Har Case Mein)

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Claim form (duly filled) | Company ka standard form |
| Original death certificate | Municipal corporation se |
| Policy document (original) | Insured ki policy |
| Nominee identity proof | Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID |
| Nominee address proof | Aadhaar, utility bill |
| Bank details + cancelled cheque | Payout ke liye |
| Relationship proof | Birth certificate, marriage certificate |

#### Additional Documents — Natural Death

- ✅ Hospital discharge summary / death summary
- ✅ Doctor's certificate stating cause of death
- ✅ Treatment records (if hospitalized)
- ✅ Cremation/burial certificate (kuch companies maangti hain)

#### Additional Documents — Accidental Death

- ✅ FIR copy (registered)
- ✅ Post-mortem report
- ✅ Police investigation report / final report
- ✅ MLC (Medico-Legal Certificate)
- ✅ Driving license (agar driving accident ho)

### Step 3: Documents Submit Karein

- **Nearest branch** pe personally jaake submit karein (best option)
- **Registered post** se bhejein (tracking rakhein)
- **Online upload** — Kuch companies ab online submission allow karti hain

**Pro Tip:** Har document ki **photocopy** apne paas rakhein. Original submit karne se pehle scan bhi kar lein.

### Step 4: Company Verification & Investigation

Company yeh verify karegi:

1. **Policy status** — Active thi ya lapsed?
2. **Premium payment** — Regular pay kiya tha?
3. **Nominee verification** — Correct nominee hai?
4. **Death cause verification** — Natural ya accidental?
5. **Medical history check** — Kuch chhupaya toh nahi tha? (early claims mein)
6. **Investigation** — External agency se verify (early claims ya suspicious cases mein)

### Step 5: Claim Settlement

| Settlement Type | Details |
|----------------|---------|
| **Lump sum** | Ek hi mein poora sum assured |
| **Monthly income** | Kuch policies mein monthly payout option |
| **Combo** | Part lump sum + part monthly |

Payout generally **NEFT/RTGS** se nominee ke bank account mein aata hai.

## Common Rejection Reasons — Aur Kaise Bachein

| # | Rejection Reason | Kaise Bacha Jaaye |
|---|-----------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Non-disclosure of medical history** | Policy lete waqt HAR medical condition bataayein — bp, diabetes, surgery, sab kuch |
| 2 | **Non-disclosure of habits** | Smoking, drinking, tobacco — honestly bataayein |
| 3 | **Non-disclosure of income** | Genuine income bataayein, over-inflate mat karein |
| 4 | **Lapsed policy** | Premium time pe pay karein, grace period bhi use karein |
| 5 | **Wrong nominee details** | Nominee update rakhein, especially shaadi/birth ke baad |
| 6 | **Suicide (within 1 year)** | First year mein suicide exclude hai most policies mein |
| 7 | **Dangerous activities** | Adventure sports, racing — policy mein mentioned hona chahiye |
| 8 | **Fraud documents** | Original documents use karein, fake mat banwayein |

## How to Ensure Smooth Term Insurance Claim

### For Policyholder (Jab Zinda Ho)

- ✅ **Medical history fully disclose karein** — Choti bhi bimari ho toh bataayein
- ✅ **Habits honestly bataayein** — Smoking/drinking chhupana claim reject karwa sakta hai
- ✅ **Premium regular pay karein** — Auto-debit set karein
- ✅ **Nominee update rakhein** — Life events (shaadi, baccha) ke baad update karein
- ✅ **Policy document safe rakhein** — Nominee ko bhi batayein kahan hai
- ✅ **Company ko inform karein** — Contact number, address change pe update karayein
- ✅ **Medical checkup records preserve karein** — Future mein proof ban sakte hain

### For Nominee (Claim Ke Time)

- ✅ **Jaldi inform karein** — 30 days ke andar
- ✅ **Original documents collect karein** — Death certificate, hospital records
- ✅ **Multiple photocopies banwayein** — Har document ki
- ✅ **Follow up karein** — Regular status check karein
- ✅ **Ombudsman contact karein** — Agar unjustly reject hua toh

## FAQs

### 1. Term insurance claim mein kitna time lagta hai?

Policy ke 3 years baad natural death claim mein **15-30 days**, accidental death mein **30-60 days**, aur early claim (3 years ke andar) mein investigation ki wajah se **90-180 days** lagte hain. Documents complete hone pe faster settlement hota hai.

### 2. Kya nominee ke alawa koi aur claim kar sakta hai?

Nahi, sirf **registered nominee** hi claim file kar sakta hai. Agar nominee nahi hai toh legal heir (court ke through) claim karega. Isliye nominee ka naam policy mein zaroor daalein.

### 3. Agar policy lapsed thi toh claim milega?

Nahi, lapsed policy pe claim nahi milta. Lekin **grace period** (usually 30 days) ke andar premium pay karne pe policy reinstate hoti hai. Grace period ke andar death ho toh claim milta hai (premium deduct hoke).

### 4. Kya suicide case mein term insurance claim milta hai?

Most policies mein **first 12 months** mein suicide exclude hai — claim nahi milta. 12 months ke baad suicide case mein sum assured milta hai (kuch policies mein fund value). Lekin accidental death benefit rider nahi milega.

### 5. Kya overseas death pe term insurance claim hota hai?

Haan, agar death India ke bahar hui hai toh bhi claim hota hai. Additional documents chahiye honge — foreign death certificate (apostilled/attested), Indian embassy ka letter, aur local police report (agar applicable).

### 6. Agar term insurance claim reject hua toh kya karein?

Pehle rejection reason samjhein. Agar unfair lagta hai toh: (1) Company ki grievance cell mein complaint karein, (2) IRDAI portal pe complaint karein, (3) Insurance Ombudsman se contact karein (free service, no lawyer needed), (4) Consumer court jaayein (last resort).
