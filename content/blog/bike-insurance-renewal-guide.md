## Key Takeaway

Bike insurance renewal (do paahiya beema naveekaran) har saal karna padta hai — agar aapne renewal nahi kiya toh police challan kat sakta hai, aur koi accident ho toh poora kharcha aapko khall se dena padega. Online renewal ab 5 minute mein ho jaata hai — bas RC, purani policy, aur payment chahiye. NCB (No Claim Bonus) 20-50% tak ka discount deta hai agar aapne koi claim nahi kiya. Expired policy bhi 90 din tak renew ho sakti hai bina inspection ke. Lekin 90 din ke baad NCB khatam ho jaata hai aur naye sirf se policy leni padti hai. Hamesha time pe renew karo — online karo, compare karo, aur best deal lo!

## TL;DR — Key Points

- **Renew before expiry date** — grace period nahi hota insurance mein
- **NCB can save 20-50%** on own damage premium — don't let it lapse
- **Online renewal is 10-20% cheaper** than offline through agent
- **Expired policy can be renewed within 90 days** without inspection
- **After 90 days, NCB is lost** and fresh policy with inspection required
- **Third-party insurance is mandatory** — riding without it is illegal
- **Compare quotes** from multiple insurers before renewing

## Bike Insurance Renewal Process (Step-by-Step)

### Online Renewal (Recommended — 5 Minutes)

1. **Visit insurer website or comparison portal**
2. **Enter bike details** — registration number, make/model, previous policy number
3. **Choose coverage type** — Third-party only or Comprehensive
4. **Select add-ons** (if comprehensive) — Zero dep, engine protect, etc.
5. **Review IDV** — ensure it's correct as per your bike's age
6. **Apply NCB** — your no-claim bonus discount applies automatically
7. **Make payment** — UPI, card, net banking
8. **Download policy** — instant PDF via email

### Offline Renewal (Through Agent/Branch)

1. **Contact your agent or visit insurer branch**
2. **Submit documents** — RC copy, previous policy, ID proof
3. **Fill renewal form**
4. **Pay premium** — cash, cheque, or card
5. **Receive policy** — may take 2-7 days

## IDV Calculation for Bike Renewal

IDV (Insured Declared Value) = Manufacturer's ex-showroom price × (1 - Depreciation%)

| Bike Age | Depreciation % | IDV % of Ex-Showroom |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| 0-6 months | 5% | 95% |
| 6 months - 1 year | 15% | 85% |
| 1-2 years | 20% | 80% |
| 2-3 years | 30% | 70% |
| 3-4 years | 40% | 60% |
| 4-5 years | 50% | 50% |
| 5+ years | Mutually agreed | Market value |

**Example:** Honda Activa (ex-showroom ₹78,000), 2 years old
- IDV = ₹78,000 × 80% = ₹62,400
- Comprehensive premium ≈ ₹1,800-₹2,200
- TP premium = ₹714

**NCB Example:**

## What If Your Bike Insurance Has Expired?

### Within 90 Days of Expiry
- ✅ Renew online without inspection
- ✅ NCB retained (if no claim in expired period)
- ✅ Same process as normal renewal

### After 90 Days of Expiry
- ❌ NCB is LOST completely
- ❌ Fresh policy required (not renewal)
- ❌ Bike inspection mandatory
- ❌ Process takes 2-5 days
- ❌ Higher premium (no NCB benefit)

### After 1 Year of Expiry
- ❌ All benefits lost
- ❌ Fresh policy with full inspection
- ❌ Some insurers may refuse
- ❌ Starting from scratch with new waiting periods

## Tips to Save on Bike Insurance Renewal

1. **Compare quotes online** — 10-20% savings possible
2. **Maintain NCB** — don't file small claims (under ₹3,000)
3. **Choose correct IDV** — don't over-insure or under-insure
4. **Install anti-theft devices** — get 2-5% discount
5. **Join automobile association** — additional discount
6. **Buy long-term TP** — 3 or 5 year TP avoids yearly TP hikes
7. **Bundle add-ons wisely** — only buy what you need
8. **Avoid lapses** — renewal on time maintains NCB

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Bike insurance renewal kitne din pehle karni chahiye?
Ideally 15-30 din pehle renew karo. Lekin expiry date se pehle koi bhi time pe kar sakte ho. 30 din pehle karne se naye policy mein koi gap nahi aata aur coverage continuous rehta hai. (Bike insurance naveekaran expiry se 15-30 din pehle karna chahiye)

### 2. Kya expired bike insurance online renew ho sakti hai?
Haan, agar expiry 90 din ke andar hai toh online renewal possible hai bina inspection ke. 90 din ke baad inspection lagta hai aur NCB bhi khatam ho jaata hai. Jaldi karo!

### 3. Kya NCB transfer hota hai bike badalne pe?
Haan! Agar aap purani bike bech kar nayi le rahe ho, toh NCB transfer hota hai. NCB aapki hai, bike ki nahi. NCB retention certificate le ke naye insurer pe transfer kara sakte hain.

### 4. Kya comprehensive insurance lena zaroori hai?
Legal requirement sirf third-party insurance hai. Lekin comprehensive lena wise hai kyunki aapki bike ka own damage, theft, aur natural calamity bhi cover hota hai. New bike ke liye comprehensive MUST hai.

### 5. Bike insurance renewal pe kya medical test hota hai?
Nahi, bike insurance mein koi medical test nahi hota. Sirf bike ki inspection lagti hai agar policy 90 din se zyada expired hai ya naye insurer se fresh policy le rahe ho.

### 6. Kya online renewal safe hai?
Bilkul! IRDAI ke registered insurers ki website se renewal 100% safe hai. Digital policy hard copy ke barabar valid hai. Police check pe digital policy dikhana legal hai.

### 7. Long-term bike insurance kya hai?
IRDAI ne mandate kiya hai ki naye bikes ke liye 5-year TP policy lena zaroori hai. Aap 1-year comprehensive + 5-year TP ya 3-year comprehensive + 5-year TP bhi le sakte hain. Long-term policy se yearly TP rate increase se bach jaate hain.
