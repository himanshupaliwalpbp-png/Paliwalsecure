## Term Insurance Buying Guide

### Step 1: Calculate Cover
Cover needed = 15x annual income + outstanding loans - existing cover

### Step 2: Choose Term
- Age 25-30: 40-year term (covers till 65-70)
- Age 30-40: 30-year term
- Age 40+: Whole life (till 70-80)

### Step 3: Compare Plans
Use [our comparison tool](/compare) for instant quotes from 10+ insurers.

### Step 4: Medical Test
Most insurers require medical test for sum insured >₹50L. Free at insurer's network centers.

### Step 5: Buy and Upload Documents
- Identity proof (PAN, Aadhaar)
- Address proof
- Income proof (salary slips/ITR)
- Medical reports (if applicable)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Buying too little cover:** ₹25-50L is insufficient for most families. Buy minimum ₹1Cr.
2. **Choosing short term:** 10-15 year term is too short. Choose 30-40 years.
3. **Hiding medical history:** Non-disclosure = claim rejection. Declare everything.
4. **Not comparing:** Don't buy from first agent you meet. Compare 3+ plans.
5. **Buying offline:** Online plans are 30-40% cheaper for identical cover.
