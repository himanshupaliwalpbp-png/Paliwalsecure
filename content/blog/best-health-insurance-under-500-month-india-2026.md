## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Choosing Lowest Premium Only
A ₹3,000/year plan with 80% CSR is worse than a ₹5,000/year plan with 99% CSR. Always prioritize CSR over premium.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Waiting Periods
Most plans have 4-year waiting for pre-existing diseases. If you have diabetes/BP, look for plans with 1-2 year PED waiting (Star Health, Care Health).

### Mistake 3: Not Checking Room Rent Limit
Some cheap plans cap room rent at 1% of sum insured (₹5,000/day for ₹5L cover). Private rooms in metro cities cost ₹8,000-15,000/day. You'll pay the difference.

### Mistake 4: Buying Without Reading Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery, dental, infertility, and alternative medicine (except AYUSH) are typically excluded. Read the exclusions list before buying.

### Mistake 5: Not Disclosing Medical History
Non-disclosure is the #1 reason for claim rejection. IRDAI's 5-year moratorium rule (after 5 continuous years, no claim can be rejected for non-disclosure except fraud) protects honest customers — but only if you disclosed honestly at purchase time.

**Q: Can I really get health insurance for ₹500/month?**

**Q: Can I upgrade my sum insured later?**
A: Yes, at renewal. But premium will increase based on your age at upgrade. Better to buy higher sum insured at younger age.

**Q: What if my claim is rejected?**
A: File appeal with insurer's grievance officer (15 days response time). If unresolved, approach IRDAI's Bima Bharosa Portal (bimabharosa.irda.gov.in) or Insurance Ombudsman (free, up to ₹50 lakh claims).
