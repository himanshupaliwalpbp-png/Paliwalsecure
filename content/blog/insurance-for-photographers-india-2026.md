## Quick Answer

Photographers need: equipment insurance (₹3,000-10,000/year), professional indemnity (₹3,000-8,000/year), public liability (₹3,000-6,000/year), health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year). One stolen camera body can cost ₹1-3L.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Is camera equipment covered under home insurance?
**A:** Only with photographic equipment add-on. Specify each item with serial numbers. Covers theft, fire, transit. Premium: 1-2% of value.

### Q: Can a photographer be sued by a client?
**A:** Yes. Lost photos, privacy violation, missed wedding. PI covers legal defense + settlement. Premium: ₹3,000-8,000/year.

### Q: Do photographers need public liability for outdoor shoots?
**A:** Yes. Passerby trips on tripod, light stand falls — you are liable. Some venues require PL before permitting photography.

### Q: What if camera is damaged during destination wedding?
**A:** Equipment insurance with transit cover protects during travel. Without transit cover, travel damage excluded.

### Q: Can photographers claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. Equipment, PI, PL under Section 37(1). Health and life under 80D/80C (old regime).
