## Quick Answer

Hobby collectors (art, coins, stamps, antiques, wine) need specialized collectibles insurance. Standard home insurance caps valuable items at ₹10,000-50,000. Collectibles insurance covers full declared value against theft, fire, flood, accidental damage, and transit. Premium: 0.5-2% of collection value per year. Get professional appraisal before buying.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Does home insurance cover my art collection?
**A:** Partially. Standard home contents insurance caps individual items at ₹10,000-50,000. A painting worth ₹5L would only get ₹50,000. You need "valuable articles" or "collectibles" add-on with itemized appraisal. Each item must be declared with value, photo, and provenance.

### Q: How do I insure my coin or stamp collection?
**A:** Get a professional numismatic/philatelic appraisal. Insurer issues a "valuable articles" policy with each item or collection lot listed. Covers theft, fire, flood, accidental damage. Premium: 0.5-1% of declared value. Update appraisal every 3-5 years as collection value appreciates.

### Q: Can I insure antiques inherited from family?
**A:** Yes. Get a professional antique appraisal (from a certified appraiser). Declare each item with: description, age, provenance, appraised value, and photograph. Insurer may require additional documentation for items above ₹5L. Inherited items often have emotional value — ensure sum insured reflects replacement cost.

### Q: What about transit insurance when moving a collection?
**A:** Standard collectibles policy may exclude transit. Add "transit cover" for when you move items (exhibition, relocation, sale delivery). Premium: 0.1-0.5% of item value per transit. For high-value items, use specialized art logistics (climate-controlled, insured transport).

### Q: Can collectors claim insurance premium as tax deduction?
**A:** No. Collectibles insurance is a personal expense, not deductible under 80D, 80C, or 37(1). However, if you sell collectibles as a business (art dealer), the premium becomes a business expense under Section 37(1).
