## Quick Answer

Hospitals need: professional indemnity (₹25,000-2L/year), public liability (₹15,000-50,000/year), equipment insurance (₹20,000-1L/year), cyber insurance (₹10,000-30,000/year). One medical negligence suit can cost ₹10L-1Cr.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Is professional indemnity mandatory for doctors?
**A:** Not legally mandatory but strongly recommended. Consumer Protection Act 2019 makes it easier for patients to sue. PI covers legal defense + compensation.

### Q: What is the typical medical negligence claim amount?
**A:** ₹5L to ₹1Cr+ depending on severity. Death: ₹10L-50L. Permanent disability: ₹15L-1Cr. Supreme Court has awarded up to ₹11Cr.

### Q: Does hospital insurance cover data breaches?
**A:** Only with cyber insurance add-on. DPDP Act 2023 penalties up to ₹250Cr. Cyber insurance covers breach notification, data recovery, legal defense.

### Q: Can hospitals claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All hospital insurance premiums deductible under Section 37(1). Both old and new tax regimes.

### Q: What is business interruption insurance for hospitals?
**A:** Covers lost revenue + fixed costs during temporary closure (fire, flood, equipment failure). Premium: 0.5-1% of annual revenue.
