## Quick Answer

Online marketplace sellers (Flipkart, Amazon, Meesho) need: product liability (₹5,000-15,000/year), inventory insurance (₹5,000-15,000/year), cyber insurance (₹5,000-20,000/year), transit insurance (₹2,000-5,000/year). Consumer Protection Act 2019 holds the seller liable for defective products — not the marketplace. Total: ₹17,000-55,000/year.

## FAQ

### Q: Is the marketplace (Flipkart/Amazon) liable for defective products?
**A:** No. Under Consumer Protection Act 2019, the seller is the liable party — not the marketplace platform. The marketplace facilitates the sale but the seller-of-record is responsible for product quality, safety, and compliance. Product liability insurance protects you.

### Q: Does Flipkart/Amazon seller insurance cover inventory damage?
**A:** Marketplace-provided insurance (e.g., Amazon's Easy Ship damage cover) is LIMITED — typically caps at ₹5,000-10,000 per shipment. For full warehouse inventory coverage, buy separate inventory insurance: fire, theft, flood, accidental damage. Premium: 0.3-0.5% of inventory value.

### Q: What happens if a customer is injured by my product?
**A:** Customer can sue YOU (the seller) for medical costs + compensation. Consumer Protection Act 2019 applies to e-commerce. File with Consumer Forum or District Commission. Product liability insurance covers legal defense + settlement + medical costs. Premium: ₹5,000-15,000/year.

### Q: Can online sellers claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All seller insurance (product liability, inventory, cyber, transit) deductible under Section 37(1). Both old and new tax regimes. Reduces taxable online selling income.

### Q: Do I need separate insurance for each marketplace?
**A:** No. One product liability policy covers all your sales channels (Flipkart, Amazon, Meesho, own website). One inventory policy covers all warehouse stock regardless of which marketplace it's sold through. Buy comprehensive coverage, not per-marketplace.
