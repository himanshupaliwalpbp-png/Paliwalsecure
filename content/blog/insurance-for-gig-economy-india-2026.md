## Quick Answer

Gig economy workers (Ola/Uber drivers, Swiggy/Zomato delivery, Freelancers) have no employer cover. Need: health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year), term life (₹3,000-8,000/year), personal accident (₹500-1,500/year — high accident risk), and commercial vehicle insurance (for drivers). Total: ₹8,500-20,000/year. One accident can cost ₹2-10L without cover.

## FAQ

### Q: Do gig workers get insurance from Ola/Uber/Swiggy?
**A:** Some platforms offer limited accident cover during active trips (₹1-5L). This covers ONLY accidents during a ride/delivery — not health, life, or off-duty accidents. It is NOT a substitute for personal insurance. Always buy individual health + term + PA cover.

### Q: What insurance do Ola/Uber drivers need?
**A:** 1) Commercial vehicle insurance (mandatory, ₹12,000-20,000/year), 2) Personal accident (₹15L mandatory for owner-driver, ₹300-500/year), 3) Health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year — no employer cover), 4) Term life (₹3,000-8,000/year — family protection). Total: ₹20,000-38,000/year.

### Q: Can gig workers claim 80D on health insurance?
**A:** Yes, under old regime. ₹25,000 (under 60) or ₹50,000 (senior). If income is below taxable limit, no tax benefit but insurance is still essential. File ITR if income exceeds ₹2.5L (₹3L new regime) to claim 80D.

### Q: What is the best insurance for delivery executives?
**A:** Health insurance (₹5L, ₹5,000/year) + Personal accident (₹10L, ₹500/year) + Term insurance (₹25L, ₹3,000/year) = ₹8,500/year. Delivery executives have high accident risk (bike riding 8-10 hours/day). PA cover is non-negotiable.

### Q: Can gig workers buy group insurance?
**A:** Some aggregator platforms offer group insurance to gig workers. However, group cover ends if you stop working for that platform. Always buy individual insurance that stays with you regardless of platform.
