## Quick Answer

Startup founders need: health insurance (₹8,000-15,000/year — high-stress lifestyle), term life (₹5,000-12,000/year — family + investor protection), D&O liability (₹15,000-50,000/year — protects personal assets from company lawsuits), key person insurance (₹10,000-30,000/year — protects startup if founder dies), cyber insurance (₹10,000-25,000/year). Total: ₹48,000-1,32,000/year.

## Real Indian Example

A startup founder client died suddenly (cardiac arrest at 39). His family inherited 60% equity in a pre-revenue startup — worthless without him. His personal term insurance (₹1Cr, ₹8,000/year premium) paid out ₹1Cr to his family — ensuring their financial security. The startup's key person insurance (₹50L) paid the company — used to hire a replacement CEO. Without either policy, the family would have been destitute. Founders: your startup equity is NOT a safety net — insurance is.

## FAQ

### Q: Do startup founders need D&O insurance?
**A:** Yes. Directors and Officers liability protects personal assets of founders from lawsuits (investor disputes, employee claims, regulatory action). Without D&O, your personal savings, home, and investments are at risk. Investors often require D&O before funding. Premium: ₹15,000-50,000/year.

### Q: What is key person insurance for startups?
**A:** Term life on the founder, with the startup as nominee. If the founder dies, the company receives the sum insured — used to find replacement, pay off investors, or wind down operations. Essential for startups with founder-dependent revenue. Premium: ₹10,000-30,000/year for ₹1Cr cover.

### Q: Can startups claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. D&O, key person, cyber, and public liability premiums are deductible under Section 37(1). Health and term insurance for the founder qualify for 80D/80C (old regime). Consult your CA — startup tax treatment has specific provisions.

### Q: Should startup founders buy personal term insurance?
**A:** Yes. Startup equity is illiquid — if the founder dies, family inherits equity that may take years to monetize (or may become worthless). Personal term insurance (₹1Cr+) ensures family has immediate cash. Separate from key person (company is nominee for key person, family is nominee for personal term).

### Q: What is the minimum insurance for a bootstrapped startup?
**A:** Founder health insurance (₹8,000/year) + Founder term insurance (₹5,000/year) = ₹13,000/year minimum. Add D&O (₹15,000/year) if you have investors or employees. Add cyber (₹10,000/year) if you store customer data. Start with personal protection, add business protection as you grow.
