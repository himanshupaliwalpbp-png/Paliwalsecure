## Quick Answer

Schools need: public liability (₹10,000-50,000/year), building insurance (₹15,000-1L/year), student accident cover (₹50-200/student/year), professional indemnity (₹10,000-30,000/year), cyber insurance (₹10,000-25,000/year). One student injury lawsuit can cost ₹5-25L.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Is student accident insurance mandatory for schools?
**A:** Some states mandate it. Recommended even if not — covers injuries during school activities. ₹50-200/student/year for ₹1-5L per student.

### Q: Can schools be sued for student injuries?
**A:** Yes. Consumer Protection Act 2019 — schools are service providers. Unsafe premises = liable for medical + compensation.

### Q: What insurance do school buses need?
**A:** Commercial vehicle insurance (mandatory) + passenger cover + GPS tracking. School liable from pickup to drop-off.

### Q: Do schools need cyber insurance?
**A:** Yes. Schools store Aadhaar, photos, records. DPDP Act 2023 applies. One breach can cost ₹5-25L. Premium: ₹10,000-25,000/year.

### Q: Can schools claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All school insurance under Section 37(1). Educational trusts may have additional exemptions under Section 11/12.
