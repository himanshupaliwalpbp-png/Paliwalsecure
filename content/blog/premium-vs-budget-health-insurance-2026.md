## Quick Answer

Premium health insurance plans offer higher sum insured (₹50L-1Cr), more add-ons (zero-dep, restore, protect), larger hospital networks, and lower co-payments. Budget plans (₹3-10L sum insured) cover essential hospitalization at lower premiums. Premium is better for families with health risks, senior citizens, or those in metros with high medical costs.

**At a glance:**

| Parameter | Premium Health Insurance | Budget Health Insurance |
|---|---|---|
| Sum insured range | ₹50L to ₹1Cr+ | ₹3L to ₹10L |
| Premium (30yr, metro) | ₹15,000-35,000/year | ₹5,000-10,000/year |
| Room rent limit | No limit (private room) | 1-2% of sum insured (shared room) |
| Co-payment | Nil | 10-20% (especially for seniors) |
| Restore benefit | Yes (auto-restore on exhaustion) | Limited or not available |
| Add-ons | Zero-dep, protect, restore, global cover | Basic only (zero-dep extra cost) |
| Pre-existing waiting | 2-3 years | 3-4 years |
| Health checkup | Free annual (₹5,000-10,000) | Not available or limited |
| Maternity cover | Included (after waiting) | Add-on (extra premium) |
| Best for | Families, seniors, metro residents | Young, healthy, budget-conscious |

## Exclusions Comparison

Both Premium Health Insurance and Budget Health Insurance have standard health insurance exclusions (per IRDAI guidelines):
- Pre-existing conditions (during waiting period)
- Cosmetic/plastic surgery (unless accident-related)
- Dental treatment (unless due to accident)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- War, nuclear events, terrorism

**Plan-specific exclusions vary.** Always read the policy wording.

## Claim Process

### Cashless Claim (if applicable)
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours
2. Visit network hospital
3. Hospital sends pre-authorization
4. Insurer approves within 4-24 hours
5. Treatment proceeds (no upfront payment)

### Reimbursement Claim
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours (planned) / 7 days (emergency)
2. Get treated at any hospital
3. Submit documents within 15-30 days
4. Insurer reimburses within 7-15 working days
