### 2. Engine Protect Add-on

Monsoon season mein waterlogging se engine damage:
- Hydrostatic lock
- Water entry in engine
- Gearbox damage due to water
- Con-rod bending
- **Cost**: ₹1,500-₹3,000/year
- **Critical for**: Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Delhi NCR

### 3. NCB Protection

Creta ka premium high hai — NCB se 50% discount = ₹6,000-₹14,000/year saving:
- **Cost**: ₹1,200-₹2,500/year
- **Benefit**: 2 small claims without losing NCB
- **Smart math**: ₹2,500 add-on = ₹14,000 NCB protected

### 4. Return to Invoice

New Creta ke liye total loss/theft mein:
- **Cost**: ₹2,000-₹4,000/year
- **Benefit**: Full on-road price (₹12-20 Lakh) instead of depreciated IDV
- **Best for**: First 3 years, financed vehicles

### 5. Personal Accident Cover

- **Cost**: ₹500-₹800/year
- **Coverage**: ₹15 Lakh (owner-driver death/disability)
- **Add**: Passenger cover for family protection (₹300-₹500 extra)

### 6. Consumables Cover

Small items jo normal insurance mein exclude hain:
- Engine oil, coolant, brake oil
- Nuts, bolts, screws
- AC gas, lubricants
- **Cost**: ₹800-₹1,500/year
- **Useful in**: Major accidents where these items are replaced

## FAQs

### 1. Hyundai Creta ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Creta ka comprehensive insurance ₹12,000-₹28,000 per year hota hai variant ke hisaab se. Base E model ke liye ₹12,000-₹16,000, top SX(O) ke liye ₹18,000-₹28,000. Zero dep ke saath ₹15,500-₹32,000. Third-party premium ₹7,800-₹8,500 fixed hai. Premium IDV, location, add-ons, aur NCB par depend karta hai.

### 2. Creta ke liye zero dep add-on zaroori hai?

Haan, 100%! Creta ke parts bahut expensive hain — front bumper ₹15K-₹25K, LED headlamp ₹10K-₹18K each. Zero dep ke bina in parts par 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai. ₹3,000-₹6,000 ka add-on ek hi claim mein recover ho jata hai. Creta jaise premium SUV ke liye zero dep non-negotiable hai.

### 3. Creta diesel ka insurance petrol se zyada hota hai?

Thoda sa zyada hota hai kyunki diesel variant ki IDV zyada hai (₹17.49L vs ₹16.49L) aur engine capacity same hai. Diesel engine ke liye engine protect add-on especially important hai kyunki diesel engines waterlogging mein zyada prone hain hydrostatic lock ke liye. Premium difference lagbhag ₹1,000-₹2,000/year hota hai.

### 4. NCB se kitni saving hoti hai Creta insurance mein?

Creta ka premium high hai toh NCB ka impact bhi significant hai. 50% NCB = ₹6,000-₹14,000/year saving. 5 consecutive claim-free years mein total saving ₹40,000-₹90,000+ ho sakti hai. NCB protection add-on (₹1,200-₹2,500) le kar chhote claims se Nreta bacha sakte hain — bahut smart investment hai.

### 5. Creta insurance renewal ke time kya check karein?

Renewal ke time ye check karein: IDV fair market value par set hai (kam na ho), zero dep add-on included hai, NCB correctly reflected hai, cashless Hyundai service center network available hai, engine protect add-on hai (monsoon cities ke liye), aur kam se kam 3 insurers compare karein. 30 days pehle renewal start karein.

### 6. Kaunsa insurer Creta ke liye best hai?

**Tata AIG** sabse high claim ratio (98.2%) ke saath best hai. **Acko** fastest settlement ke liye, **Digit** lowest premium ke liye. Hyundai ki vast service network ke karan most insurers cashless settlement offer karte hain. Apne city mein cashless garage density check karein — zyada garages = better claim experience.
