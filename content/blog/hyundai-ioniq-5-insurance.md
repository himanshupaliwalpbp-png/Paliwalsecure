## Best Add-ons for Ioniq 5

### 1. Battery Depreciation Cover — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Cost: ₹4,000-₹4,800/yr
- Covers: Battery capacity degradation below 70%
- 72.6 kWh battery replacement: ₹10-14L
- Without this: After 5-7 years, degraded battery replacement is YOUR cost

### 2. Zero Depreciation — MANDATORY for Premium EV
- Cost: ₹5,500-₹6,800/yr
- Saves: ₹80,000-₹1.5L on accident claims
- Ioniq 5 body panels 50%+ costlier than ICE vehicles
- One major claim = zero dep pays for 5+ years of premium

### 3. Charging Port & Cable Cover
- Cost: ₹2,000-₹3,000/yr
- 800V charging port: ₹30K-₹80K repair
- Cable theft/damage: ₹15K-₹25K
- EV-specific add-on, not all insurers offer this

### 4. Return to Invoice — Recommended for first 3 years
- Cost: ₹4,000-₹5,500/yr
- Total loss settlement: ₹46L+ (ex-showroom + reg) vs ₹41L IDV
- Premium EV = higher theft risk
- Difference of ₹5L+ makes this add-on worth it

### 5. Consumables Cover
- Cost: ₹1,500-₹2,000/yr
- EV still needs: Coolant, brake fluid, cabin filter, wiper blades
- Saves ₹3,000-₹5,000 per claim

## EV-Specific Claim Process for Ioniq 5

### Cashless Claim at Hyundai Blue-linked Workshop
1. Call insurer immediately — mention EV-specific damage
2. Take to Hyundai EV-certified workshop only
3. High-voltage system inspection MANDATORY before any repair
4. Battery health check performed separately
5. 800V system repairs require specialized technicians
6. Average repair time: 7-14 days (parts may need import)

### Battery Claim Process
1. Battery physical damage → covered under comprehensive
2. Battery capacity degradation → covered under battery depreciation add-on
3. Hyundai warranty: 8 years/1.6L km on battery (manufacturing defects only)
4. Insurance covers: Accidental damage, fire, flooding, charging incidents

## FAQ

### Q1: Ioniq 5 ka insurance itna mehenga kyun hai?
₹46L ki car hai, IDV ₹41-43L hai — premium IDV pe depend karta hai. Plus battery cover, zero dep, charging port cover — ye sab EV-specific add-ons hain jo ICE car mein nahi chahiye. Premium protection = premium price.

### Q2: Battery cover ke bina chal jayega?
BILKUL NAHI! 72.6 kWh battery replacement ₹10-14L ka kharcha hai. Battery depreciation cover ₹4,500/yr mein ye protection deta hai. 5 saal baad battery 70% neeche giri toh bina cover ke poora kharcha aapka.

### Q3: 800V charging port damage cover hota hai?
Standard policy mein NAHI hota. Charging Port Cover add-on (₹2,000-₹3,000/yr) lena padega. 800V ultra-fast charging system ka repair ₹30K-₹80K tak lagta hai.

### Q4: Hyundai warranty se sab cover ho jayega?
Warranty sirf manufacturing defects cover karti hai. Accident, fire, flooding, charging damage — ye sab INSURANCE se cover honge. Warranty ≠ Insurance, dono chahiye.
