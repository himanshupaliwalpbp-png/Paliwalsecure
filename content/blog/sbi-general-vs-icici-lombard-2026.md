## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is SBI General a government company?
**A:** No. SBI General is a joint venture between State Bank of India (74%) and Insurance Australia Group (26%). It is a private insurer with SBI brand backing, not a government company.

### Q: Which has better claim settlement?
**A:** ICICI Lombard (97.1% CSR) has a higher claim settlement ratio than SBI General (94.5% CSR). This means ICICI Lombard approves a higher percentage of claims. However, both are above the industry average.

### Q: Do SBI account holders get a discount on SBI General insurance?
**A:** Yes. SBI account holders often get 5-10% additional discount on SBI General insurance premiums. Check with your SBI branch or SBI General website for current offers.

### Q: Which has more network hospitals?
**A:** ICICI Lombard: 9,000+. SBI General: 6,000+. If network hospital coverage is important, ICICI Lombard has a clear advantage. However, both have major hospitals in all metro and tier-1 cities.
