## Quick Answer

LIC is government-backed with 64+ years of trust and a 98.5% claim settlement ratio. Private insurers (HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential) offer 30-50% lower premiums and digital-first experience. Both are IRDAI-regulated and equally safe. Choose LIC for trust, private insurers for pricing and convenience.

**TL;DR Summary:**

| Parameter | LIC | Private Insurers (HDFC Life, ICICI Pru, etc.) |
|---|---|---|
| Claim Settlement Ratio | 98.5% (IRDAI 2024-25) | 97-99% (varies by insurer) |
| Government backing | Yes (government-owned) | No (private/foreign JV) |
| Premium for ₹1Cr (30yr) | ₹15,000-18,000/year | ₹10,000-12,000/year |
| Online purchase | Limited (LIC website) | Full (website + app) |

## FAQ

### Q: Is LIC safer than private insurance companies?
**A:** Both are equally safe. LIC is government-backed; private insurers are IRDAI-regulated with mandatory solvency margins. If any insurer fails, IRDAI transfers policies to another insurer (typically LIC). No Indian life insurance company has ever defaulted on death claims.

### Q: Why is LIC term insurance more expensive?
**A:** LIC has higher operating costs (8 lakh+ agents, 2,000+ branches, government salary structure). Private insurers operate digitally with lower overheads. LIC also prices conservatively to maintain its AAA rating. The premium difference is cost efficiency, not safety.

### Q: Can private insurance companies deny claims?
**A:** Yes, but so can LIC. Claim rejection happens for: (1) non-disclosure of pre-existing conditions, (2) suicide in first year, (3) fraud. All insurers (LIC and private) follow the same IRDAI claim guidelines. CSR for both is above 97%.

### Q: What happens if a private insurance company goes bankrupt?
**A:** IRDAI will not let policyholders suffer. The policies are transferred to another insurer (typically LIC). This has happened in India — no policyholder has ever lost money. IRDAI mandates 1.5x solvency margin precisely to prevent this.

### Q: Should I buy LIC or private term insurance?
**A:** If brand trust and physical branches matter → LIC. If you want lower premium, digital experience, and faster claims → private. Both are safe. I recommend private insurers for term insurance (30-50% cheaper) and LIC for endowment plans (government backing matters for long-term savings).
