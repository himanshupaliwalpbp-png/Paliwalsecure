## Quick Answer

E-commerce sellers (Flipkart, Amazon, Meesho) need: (1) Product liability (defective product claim — ₹5,000-15,000/year), (2) Inventory insurance (warehouse stock — ₹5,000-15,000/year), (3) Cyber insurance (account hack, data breach — ₹5,000-20,000/year), (4) Transit insurance (goods in shipping — ₹2,000-5,000/year). One defective product lawsuit can cost ₹2-10L.

## Real Indian Example

An Amazon seller had a customer file a ₹3L claim for a defective electronic product (caused electrical fire). Without product liability insurance, the seller paid ₹3L + ₹50,000 legal fees. ## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Do e-commerce sellers need product liability insurance?
**A:** Yes. Under Consumer Protection Act 2019, the seller is liable for defective products — even if manufactured by someone else. One customer injury from a defective product can cost ₹2-10L. PL covers medical costs + compensation + legal defense.

### Q: Is warehouse inventory covered against fire and theft?
**A:** Yes with inventory insurance. Covers fire, theft, flood, accidental damage to stock. Does NOT cover: spoilage (perishables without cold storage), gradual deterioration, or goods damaged by carrier. Premium: 0.3-0.5% of inventory value.

### Q: What if my Flipkart/Amazon seller account is hacked?
**A:** Cyber insurance covers financial losses from account hack (fraudulent orders, stolen funds), data breach (customer info), and phishing. DPDP Act 2023 imposes penalties for data breaches. Premium: ₹5,000-20,000/year for ₹10L cover.

### Q: Can e-commerce sellers claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All business insurance (product liability, inventory, cyber, transit) deductible under Section 37(1). Both old and new tax regimes. Reduces taxable e-commerce income.

### Q: Do I need transit insurance for shipped products?
**A:** If you ship high-value products (electronics, jewelry, luxury items): yes. Transit insurance covers damage/loss during shipping (from warehouse to customer). Some carriers (BlueDart, Delhivery) offer built-in coverage up to ₹5,000 — above that, buy separate transit cover.
