## Claim Process

### Standard Claim Process
1. **Inform insurer** within 48 hours (varies by product)
2. **Submit claim form** with required documents
3. **Insurer verifies** documents and circumstances
4. **Approval/rejection** within 7-30 days (varies by product and complexity)
5. **Payout** within 7-15 working days of approval

### Documents Required
- Claim form (insurer-provided)
- Policy document
- ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
- Medical reports / FIR / discharge summary (as applicable)
- Bank account details (for payout)

## FAQ

### Q: Is online term insurance safe?
**A:** Yes. The policy is issued by the same IRDAI-licensed insurer. The policy document, T&Cs, claim process, and legal rights are identical to an agent-sold policy. "Online" vs "offline" only refers to how you buy — the insurance contract is the same.

### Q: Will the insurer reject online claims more easily?
**A:** No. IRDAI prohibits different claim treatment based on purchase channel. The claim process is identical whether you bought online or through an agent. CSR is the same for both channels.

### Q: Do I get advisory if I buy online?
**A:** If you buy directly from the insurer's website, you are self-service. If you buy through a POSP like Paliwal Secure, you get the same online price PLUS free personalized advisory, claim assistance, and annual policy review. We are licensed by IRDAI to sell online plans at online prices.

### Q: Can an agent match the online price?
**A:** No. The commission is built into the agent price. If an agent offers a "discount," they are reducing their own commission — this is rare and unsustainable. The online price is the insurer's official price; agents cannot legally match it.

### Q: What documents do I need for online term insurance?
**A:** Aadhaar (e-KYC), PAN, address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITR), bank account details, and nominee details. Medical tests are arranged by the insurer at their cost (home visit or lab visit). Entire process takes 3-7 days.
