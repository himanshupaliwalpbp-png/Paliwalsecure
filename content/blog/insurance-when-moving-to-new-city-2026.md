## Quick Answer

When moving to a new city, update your address on all insurance policies within 30 days. Health insurance: verify network hospitals in your new city. Motor insurance: update registration (RC) within 30 days and inform insurer. Life insurance: update nominee address. Failing to update can lead to claim complications.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Do I need to inform my insurer when I move to a new city?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI requires policyholders to inform insurers of address changes within 30 days. For motor insurance, you must also update your RC with the new RTO. Failure to inform can lead to claim rejection.

### Q: Will my health insurance premium change if I move to a metro city?
**A:** Possibly. Moving from tier-2 to metro may increase premium by 10-15% at renewal because hospital costs are higher in metros. Your existing policy continues at old rate until renewal.

### Q: Can I use my health insurance in a different city?
**A:** Yes. Health insurance is valid across India. Cashless treatment works at any network hospital nationwide.

### Q: Do I need to re-register my car when moving to another state?
**A:** If moving permanently, re-register within 12 months (Section 47, Motor Vehicles Act). Get NOC from old RTO, pay road tax in new state, update RC, inform insurer.

### Q: What insurance should I buy when renting in a new city?
**A:** Renters insurance (contents cover) for ₹1,500-3,000/year. Also verify health insurance network hospitals near your new residence.
