## Quick Answer

Temporary/contract workers have no employer benefits. Need: health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year), term life (₹3,000-8,000/year), personal accident (₹200-500/year). If employer provides group cover during contract: verify it ends when contract ends — buy individual cover BEFORE contract expires. Never have a coverage gap.

## Key Takeaways

- Buy insurance BEFORE you need it — not after
- Declare all information honestly to avoid claim rejection
- Compare 3+ insurers before buying — premium varies 10-20%
- Verify CSR from IRDAI Annual Report (target: 95%+)
- Read policy wording — especially exclusions section

A contract worker client had a 2-month gap between contracts. During this gap, he needed an emergency appendectomy (₹80,000). Contract workers: your insurance must outlast your contract.

## FAQ

### Q: Does contract worker employer provide insurance?
**A:** Some contract employers provide group health/accident cover during the contract period. This ends when the contract ends. Always verify: (1) what is covered, (2) when it ends, (3) sum insured. Buy individual cover BEFORE the contract ends to avoid a gap.

### Q: Can temporary workers buy individual health insurance?
**A:** Yes. Health insurance is available to anyone regardless of employment type. Buy individual policy (₹5L cover, ₹5,000/year). This stays with you across jobs, contracts, and unemployment. Do NOT rely on employer cover alone.

### Q: What insurance do daily wage workers need?
**A:** 1) Personal accident (₹200-500/year for ₹10L — highest priority, no medical test), 2) Health insurance (₹3,000-5,000/year — Ayushman Bharat if eligible, else cheapest private), 3) PMJJBY (₹436/year for ₹2L life cover), 4) PMSBY (₹20/year for ₹2L accident). Total: ₹456-5,500/year.

### Q: Can contract workers claim 80D?
**A:** Yes, if they have taxable income and file ITR (old regime). ₹25,000 on health insurance premium. Many contract workers earn below ₹2.5L (no tax) — 80D is unused but insurance is still essential.

### Q: What happens to insurance between contracts?
**A:** Individual insurance continues regardless of employment status. If you only had employer group cover: you are uninsured between contracts. ALWAYS maintain individual health + PA + term insurance that is independent of any employer.
