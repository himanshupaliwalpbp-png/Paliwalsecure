## Quick Answer

HNIs (₹5Cr+ net worth) need: high-sum health insurance (₹50L-1Cr — covers critical illness without liquidating assets), umbrella liability (₹1-5Cr — protects against lawsuits), term insurance (₹2-5Cr — estate planning), cyber insurance (₹10K-50K — investment account hacks), art/collectibles insurance, and international health coverage. HNI insurance focuses on asset protection, not basic coverage.

## Key Takeaways

- Buy insurance BEFORE you need it — not after
- Declare all information honestly to avoid claim rejection
- Compare 3+ insurers before buying — premium varies 10-20%
- Verify CSR from IRDAI Annual Report (target: 95%+)
- Read policy wording — especially exclusions section

## FAQ

### Q: What insurance does an HNI need that others don't?
**A:** 1) Umbrella liability (₹1-5Cr — covers lawsuits beyond standard limits), 2) High-sum health (₹50L-1Cr — covers critical illness without selling assets), 3) Art/collectibles/wine insurance, 4) Kidnap & ransom (for high-profile HNIs), 5) International health (treatment in US/Europe/Singapore). These are specialized covers not needed by average earners.

### Q: Should HNIs buy term insurance?
**A:** Yes, for estate planning. Term insurance (₹2-5Cr) provides liquidity for heirs to pay inheritance-related costs (capital gains on inherited assets, legal fees, business succession). Without liquidity, heirs may have to sell inherited assets at unfavorable prices. Premium: ₹20,000-50,000/year for ₹5Cr cover.

### Q: Is high-sum health insurance worth it for HNIs?
**A:** Yes. Critical illness (cancer, cardiac) can cost ₹15-50L in top Indian hospitals, and ₹1-5Cr internationally. HNIs should not liquidate investments (mutual funds, stocks) to pay medical bills — that destroys wealth. High-sum health (₹50L-1Cr) ensures medical costs don't eat into investments. Premium: ₹15,000-40,000/year.

### Q: Can HNIs claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes, for business-owned policies: D&O, key person, cyber, PI under Section 37(1). Personal insurance (health, life, umbrella) — 80D/80C (old regime, but HNIs may hit 80C limit quickly with other investments). HNIs in 30% slab: maximum tax saving ₹78,000 on ₹2.5L deductions.

### Q: What is umbrella liability insurance for HNIs?
**A:** Covers lawsuits beyond standard home/auto liability limits. Example: a guest slips at your home → ₹50L lawsuit. Standard home insurance covers ₹10L. Umbrella liability covers the remaining ₹40L. Essential for HNIs with high-profile lifestyle, multiple properties, or public visibility. Premium: ₹10,000-30,000/year for ₹1Cr cover.
