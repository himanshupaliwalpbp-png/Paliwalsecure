## How to Choose the Best Plan for Cancer (Oncology)

### 1. Check PED Waiting Period
Sabse kam waiting period wala plan chuno. 24-60 months common hai, lekin kuch plans me 24 months bhi milta hai.

### 2. Premium Loading Comparison
Different insurers alag loading charge karte hain. 20-50% (dedicated plans lower) average hai. 3-4 quotes compare karo.

### 3. Sum Insured Adequacy
Minimum ₹10 Lakh sum insured chuno — Cancer (Oncology) treatment expensive ho sakta hai. ₹25-50 Lakh super top-up add karo for major expenses.

### 4. Network Hospital Coverage
Aapke city me insurer ke network hospitals check karo. Cashless claim ke liye network hospital zaroori hai.

### 5. Co-payment Clause
Kuch plans me 10-20% co-payment hota hai — matlab har claim me 10-20% aapko dena padega. Co-payment waived plans chuno.

### 6. Restoration Benefit
Sum insured exhausted hone pe automatic restore wale plans best hain — Cancer (Oncology) patients ke liye useful.

### 7. No Room Rent Capping
Single private room allow karne wale plans chuno — capping wale plans me dispute ho sakta hai.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Kya Cancer (Oncology) patient ko health insurance milega?
**A:** Haan, milega. IRDAI ke rules ke under insurers Cancer (Oncology) patient ko cover karte hain, lekin PED waiting period (24-60 months) aur premium loading (20-50% (dedicated plans lower)) ke saath.

### Q: Cancer (Oncology) chhupa ke insurance le sakta hu?
**A:** Bilkul nahi. Hiding pre-existing disease = claim rejection + policy cancellation. IRDAI ke under 3-5 saal tak fraud detected hua to claim reject ho sakta hai. Hamesha honestly declare karo.

### Q: Cancer (Oncology)-related claim PED waiting me hoga to?
**A:** Nahin, PED waiting period ke during Cancer (Oncology)-related claims reject honge. Lekin non-Cancer (Oncology) related hospitalization (accident, etc.) covered hota hai from Day 1.

### Q: Premium kitna zyada lagega?
**A:** Average 20-50% (dedicated plans lower) extra than standard premium. Exact amount insurer + aapki condition pe depend karta hai. 3-4 insurers se quotes lo.

### Q: Kya me existing policy port kar sakta hu?
**A:** Haan. IRDAI portability rules ke under aap existing policy ko better plan me port kar sakte ho — accumulated waiting period credits bhi transfer hote hain.

### Q: Cancer (Oncology) ke baad policy le sakta hu?
**A:** Haan, lekin policy lena jitna jaldi ho utna achha — younger age + shorter waiting period. 24-60 months waiting period jaldi complete hogi.
