## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which is better for health insurance — ICICI Lombard or Bajaj Allianz?
**A:** ICICI Lombard has a higher CSR (97.1% vs 96.2%) and better digital experience. Bajaj Allianz offers more add-ons and lower premiums. For tech-savvy users, ICICI Lombard. For budget-conscious users who want customization, Bajaj Allianz.

### Q: Can I file claims via WhatsApp with ICICI Lombard?
**A:** Yes. ICICI Lombard offers WhatsApp-based claim intimation and tracking. You can also upload documents via WhatsApp. This is a significant advantage for users who prefer messaging over phone calls.

### Q: Which has more network hospitals?
**A:** ICICI Lombard: 9,000+. Bajaj Allianz: 8,000+. Both have pan-India coverage. Check the network hospital list for your city before buying — both have major hospitals in metros and tier-1 cities.

### Q: Do both offer cashless motor insurance?
**A:** Yes. ICICI Lombard has 9,000+ cashless garages. Bajaj Allianz has 6,500+. For motor insurance, ICICI Lombard has a slight edge in garage network.

### Q: Can I port from Bajaj Allianz to ICICI Lombard?
**A:** Yes. Apply for portability at least 45 days before your Bajaj Allianz policy renewal. Your NCB and waiting period credits are transferred. ICICI Lombard will issue a new policy with continuity benefits.
