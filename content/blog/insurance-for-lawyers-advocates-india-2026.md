## FAQ

### Q: Is professional indemnity mandatory for lawyers in India?
**A:** Bar Council of India recommends PI but does not mandate it (yet). However, many corporate clients and government tenders require advocates to have PI cover. One claim of professional negligence (missed deadline, wrong advice, procedural error) can cost ₹5-50L. PI is essential for practicing advocates.

### Q: What does lawyer PI insurance cover?
**A:** Claims of: professional negligence, missed court deadlines, incorrect legal advice, breach of confidentiality, conflict of interest, drafting errors. Covers legal defense + compensation + rectification costs. Does NOT cover: intentional wrongdoing, fraud, or criminal acts.

### Q: How much PI cover should a lawyer buy?
**A:** Minimum ₹25L for individual practice. ₹50L-1Cr for corporate/IP/litigation practice. Government tenders may mandate ₹1Cr+ PI. Premium: 0.3-0.5% of sum insured per year. Higher cover for high-stakes practice areas (M&A, constitutional, criminal defense).

### Q: Can lawyers claim PI as business expense?
**A:** Yes. PI, PL, health, and life insurance deductible: PI + PL under Section 37(1) (both regimes), health under 80D (old regime), life under 80C (old regime). Reduces taxable legal practice income.

### Q: What insurance do law firms need?
**A:** 1) PI for all partners + associates, 2) D&O (protects partners from firm liability), 3) Public liability (client injury in office), 4) Cyber insurance (client data — case files, evidence, settlements), 5) Workers' comp (for staff). Total: ₹50,000-2L/year depending on firm size.
