## Claim Process in Visakhapatnam

### Cashless Claim (Recommended)
1. **Inform insurer** within 48 hours of hospitalization (TOLL-FREE or app)
2. **Visit network hospital** in Visakhapatnam — show health card and ID
3. **Hospital sends pre-authorization** to insurer (TPA)
4. **Insurer approves** within 4-6 hours (for planned) or 24 hours (emergency)
5. **Treatment proceeds** — no upfront payment (except non-medical items)
6. **Discharge** — pay only co-payment (if applicable) + non-medical items

### Reimbursement Claim
1. **Inform insurer** within 48 hours (planned) or 7 days (emergency)
2. **Get treated at any hospital** in Visakhapatnam (cashless not mandatory)
3. **Pay the bill** yourself
4. **Submit documents** within 15-30 days of discharge
5. **Insurer reimburses** within 7-15 working days (after deduction of co-pay/non-payable items)

## Premium Factors

Your health insurance premium in Visakhapatnam depends on:

| Factor | Impact on Premium |
|---|---|
| Age | Higher age = higher premium (₹5,000 at 25yr → ₹25,000 at 60yr for same cover) |
| Sum insured | Higher SI = higher premium (₹5L → ₹10L: ~40% increase) |
| City tier | Tier-2 city rates (lower than metro, higher than tier-3) |
| Family size | Family floater is cheaper than individual policies |
| Pre-existing conditions | May increase premium or add waiting period |
| Add-ons | Zero-dep (+₹2,000), Restore (+₹1,500), Protect (+₹2,500) |
| Co-payment | Higher co-pay = lower premium (10% co-pay saves 15-20%) |
| Policy tenure | 3-year policy saves 10-15% vs annual |
