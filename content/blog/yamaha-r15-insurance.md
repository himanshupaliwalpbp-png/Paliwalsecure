## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Yamaha R15

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! R15 ke full fairing, LED headlamp, aur VVA engine parts bahut expensive hain — zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai
2. **Personal Accident Cover** — ₹15 Lakh coverage (owner-driver) — sports bike ke liye aur bhi zaroori
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹3,000-₹4,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — highway rides aur long tours ke liye essential
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹1.7-2 Lakh ki bike ke liye must

## 📋 Yamaha R15 Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Yamaha Blue Square authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Yamaha R15 Insurance

**Q: Yamaha R15 ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Yamaha R15 (150cc-350cc) ka third-party premium ₹1,700/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Bilkul haan! R15 ka full fairing set ₹8,000-₹12,000 ka aata hai, LED headlamp ₹5,000-₹8,000, aur fuel tank ₹4,000-₹6,000. Zero dep ke bina in sab par 30-50% depreciation deduct hogi. Must-have add-on hai!

**Q: Yamaha R15 ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. R15 ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹1,200-₹4,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: R15 ke liye comprehensive insurance kyun zaroori hai?**
A: R15 premium sports bike hai — iska fairing, LED lights, VVA engine parts sab expensive hain. Plus sports bike theft risk bhi high hai. Sirf TP le rahe ho toh apne bike ka koi damage cover nahi hoga — comprehensive must hai!
