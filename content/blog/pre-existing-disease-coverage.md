## Key Takeaway

Pre-Existing Disease (PED) coverage ke liye **honest declaration zaroori hai** — chhupane se claim 100% reject hoga. Zyaadatar insurance companies 2-4 saal ki waiting period ke baad PED cover karti hain. Diabetes, BP, thyroid jaise common PEDs ke liye specialized plans available hain (Star Diabetes Safe, Care Heart). Kuch plans day-1 PED cover bhi dete hain (with conditions). Premium 25-40% zyada hota hai PED ke saath, lekin coverage milta hai!

## What Is Pre-Existing Disease (PED)?

Pre-Existing Disease wo medical condition hai jo **insurance buy karne se pehle** se aapme exist karti ho — chahe diagnosed ho ya nahi.

### Common Pre-Existing Diseases in India

| Disease | Prevalence in India | Insurance Impact |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Diabetes Type 2 | 7.7 crore Indians | Premium +25-40% |
| Hypertension (BP) | 25-30% adults 40+ | Premium +20-35% |
| Thyroid Disorders | 12% women | Premium +15-25% |
| Asthma | 5-10% population | Premium +15-25% |
| Heart Disease | 3-5% adults | Premium +30-50% |
| Kidney Issues | 1-2% adults | Premium +30-50% |
| PCOD/PCOS | 10-15% women | Premium +10-20% |
| Obesity (BMI > 30) | 20%+ urban adults | Premium +15-30% |

### What Qualifies as PED?

IRDAI ke according, PED wo condition hai jo:
1. **48 months (4 saal)** se pehle diagnosed ya treated
2. **Symptoms exist** the before buying insurance
3. **Medical advice/treatment** le chuke ho before policy start
4. **Ongoing medication** pe ho before policy

> ⚠️ **Important**: Agar aapko pata hai ki aapme koi condition hai (even if not formally diagnosed), aur aapne declare nahi kiya — yeh fraud hai aur claim reject hoga!

## PED Declaration — Why Honesty Is Non-Negotiable

### What Happens If You Hide PED?

1. **Claim rejection**: 100% guaranteed — insurer medical records check karta hai
2. **Policy cancellation**: Entire policy void ho jaayegi
3. **Premium forfeited**: Jo premium pay kiya, wo bhi doobara
4. **Blacklisted**: Future mein kisi insurer se policy nahi milegi
5. **Legal action**: Fraud cases mein legal consequences

### How Insurers Find Out About Hidden PEDs

- Hospital medical records
- Previous insurance claims
- Doctor consultation records
- Pharmacy records (e-prescriptions)
- TPA investigation during claim
- Medical tests at claim time

> ⚠️ **Golden Rule**: Hamesha 100% honest declaration karein. Thoda zyada premium dena claim rejection se kahin better hai!

## FAQs

### 1. Kya pre-existing disease wale log health insurance le sakte hain?

Haan, bilkul le sakte hain! PED declare karein — waiting period (2-4 saal) ke baad full coverage milega. Day-1 PED cover wale specialized plans bhi available hain (diabetes, heart). Kabhi PED chhupana nahi — claim reject hoga.

### 2. Diabetes/BP/Thyroid hai toh premium kitna lagega?

Normal premium se 25-40% zyada lagega. ₹10 lakh cover ke liye normal ₹1,000-1,400/month, PED ke saath ₹1,300-1,900/month. Lekin yeh premium treatment cost se bahut kam hai — diabetes complications mein ₹5-15 lakh tak lag jaata hai.

### 3. Waiting period ke beech mein PED se hospitalization ho gayi toh?

Waiting period mein PED-related hospitalization **covered nahi hogi** — aapko khud pay karna padega. Lekkin accidents aur naye illnesses covered hain. Isliye jaldi insurance lein taaki waiting period jaldi khatam ho.

### 4. Kya PED declare karne pe policy reject ho sakti hai?

Zyaadatar cases mein nahi — insurers PED accept karte hain with loading (extra premium). Lekin severe conditions (stage 4 cancer, organ failure) mein proposal reject ho sakta hai. Moderate PEDs (diabetes, BP, thyroid) almost always accepted.

### 5. Star Diabetes Safe jaise plans kaunse log lein?

Sirf un logon ko lena chahiye jinhe diabetes hai aur unhe immediate diabetes coverage chahiye. Agar aap diabetes-free hain toh normal plan better hai. Star Diabetes Safe mein premium zyada hai aur co-pay bhi hota hai — lekin diabetes Day 1 se covered hai.

### 6. PED waiting period reduce karne ka koi tarika hai?

Directly nahi, lekin: (1) Portability se previous policy ka waiting period carry forward hota hai, (2) Kuch insurers shorter waiting ke saath special plans offer karte hain, (3) Group insurance mein PED Day 1 se covered hota hai — employer cover maintain karein.
