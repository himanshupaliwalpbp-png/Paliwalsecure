## Best Add-ons for XUV 3XO

### 1. Return to Invoice — CRITICAL for New Launch
- Cost: ₹1,500-₹2,500/yr
- Pays: Full ex-showroom + registration on total loss
- New launch car ki market value pehle 2 saal mein tez girti hai
- Without RTI: IDV ₹9L settlement vs ₹12L invoice = ₹3L LOSS

### 2. Zero Depreciation — Must for first 3 years
- Cost: ₹2,200-₹3,000/yr
- Saves: ₹20,000-₹40,000 on accident claims
- New car parts are expensive — first claim mein zero dep pays for itself

### 3. Engine Protect — Recommended for both fuel types
- Cost: ₹1,500-₹2,200/yr
- Covers: Hydrostatic lock, water damage, oil leakage
- Turbo engines = higher repair costs than naturally aspirated
- Diesel variant mein MUST — monsoon water ingress common issue

### 4. NCB Protector
- Cost: ₹600-₹1,000/yr
- New car = careful driving = build 50% NCB in 5 years
- One claim without protector = lose 20-50% NCB discount

### 5. Consumables Cover
- Cost: ₹800-₹1,200/yr
- Saves ₹2,000-₹4,000 per claim on oil, coolant, filters

## Claim Process for XUV 3XO

### Cashless Claim
1. Call insurer helpline within 48 hours
2. Take vehicle to Mahindra authorized service center
3. Surveyor inspects within 24 hours
4. Workshop submits repair estimate
5. Insurer approves — you pay only deductible
6. New model: Parts may take 3-5 extra days initially

### Key Tips for New Launch Vehicle Claims
- **Document everything**: Take photos of all damage immediately
- **OEM parts only**: Insist on Mahindra genuine parts for warranty
- **ADAS calibration**: If sensors damaged, workshop must recalibrate
- **First claims**: May take slightly longer as adjusters learn new model

## FAQ

### Q1: XUV 3XO ka insurance kitna padega?
Base comprehensive ₹16,000-₹28,000/yr. With zero dep + RTI + engine protect: ₹22,000-₹38,000/yr. Third party only: ₹4,923 (petrol) / ₹6,736 (diesel).

### Q2: New launch hai toh insurance mehenga hoga?
Nahi, insurance premium vehicle ki ex-showroom price aur IDV pe depend karta hai, launch date se nahi. Lekin RTI add-on new launch ke liye ZAROORI hai kyunki market value fast girti hai.

### Q3: Diesel ya petrol — kaunsa kam insurance premium?
Petrol variant ka premium thoda kam hoga kyunki third-party rate ₹4,923 hai vs diesel ka ₹6,736. Lekin diesel ka engine protect zyada important hai water damage ke liye.

### Q4: ADAS wale top model ka insurance alag hai?
Insurance premium same hai lekin ADAS parts expensive hain — front sensor ₹25K-₹40K, camera ₹30K-₹60K. Accessory cover ya zero dep zaroori hai top trim ke liye.
