Manufacturing units need: fire insurance (₹15,000-1L/year), machinery breakdown (₹10,000-50,000/year), public liability (₹10,000-40,000/year), workers compensation (₹5,000-20,000/year), business interruption (₹10,000-40,000/year). One fire can destroy ₹50L-5Cr.

## FAQ

### Q: Is fire insurance mandatory for manufacturing units?
**A:** Not legally (unless bank loan). Essential — one fire can destroy machinery worth ₹50L-5Cr. Premium: 0.1-0.3% of asset value.

### Q: What is machinery breakdown insurance?
**A:** Covers sudden damage from internal causes (short circuit, mechanical failure, operator error). NOT wear and tear. Premium: 0.5-1% of machinery value.

### Q: Is workers compensation mandatory for factories?
**A:** Yes. Employees Compensation Act 1923 mandates it. For 10+ employees (power) or 20+ (no power), ESI Act also applies.

### Q: What is business interruption insurance?
**A:** Covers lost profit + fixed costs when manufacturing stops (fire, breakdown, disaster). Premium: 0.5-1% of annual gross profit.

### Q: Can manufacturing units claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All premiums deductible under Section 37(1). Both tax regimes.
