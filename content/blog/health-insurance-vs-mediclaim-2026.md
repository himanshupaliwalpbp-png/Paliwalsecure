## Quick Answer

Mediclaim is a type of health insurance that covers only hospitalization expenses up to a fixed sum insured. Health insurance is broader — it covers hospitalization plus day-care procedures, pre/post-hospitalization, ambulance, maternity, mental health, and more. Mediclaim is the older term (popularized in the 1990s); modern health insurance policies are more comprehensive.

**At a glance:**

| Parameter | Mediclaim | Health Insurance |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage scope | Hospitalization only | Comprehensive (hospitalization + add-ons) |
| Sum insured | Fixed (₹1-5L typical) | Flexible (₹3L to ₹1Cr+) |
| Day-care procedures | Not covered | Covered (300+ procedures) |
| Pre/post-hospitalization | Not covered | Covered (30-60 days pre, 60-180 days post) |
| Maternity cover | Not available | Available (after waiting period) |
| Mental health | Not covered | Covered (mandated by IRDAI since 2022) |
| AYUSH treatment | Not covered | Covered (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) |
| Cashless claims | Limited | Available at 10,000+ hospitals nationwide |
| No Claim Bonus | Not available | Available (10-50% per claim-free year) |
| Restoration benefit | Not available | Available (sum insured restored if exhausted) |

## Exclusions Comparison

Both Mediclaim and Health Insurance have standard health insurance exclusions (per IRDAI guidelines):
- Pre-existing conditions (during waiting period)
- Cosmetic/plastic surgery (unless accident-related)
- Dental treatment (unless due to accident)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- War, nuclear events, terrorism

**Plan-specific exclusions vary.** Always read the policy wording.

## Claim Process

### Cashless Claim (if applicable)
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours
2. Visit network hospital
3. Hospital sends pre-authorization
4. Insurer approves within 4-24 hours
5. Treatment proceeds (no upfront payment)

### Reimbursement Claim
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours (planned) / 7 days (emergency)
2. Get treated at any hospital
3. Submit documents within 15-30 days
4. Insurer reimburses within 7-15 working days
