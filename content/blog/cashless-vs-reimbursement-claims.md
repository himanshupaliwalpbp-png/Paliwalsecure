## Key Takeaway

Cashless claim (bina nagad) mein hospital bill directly insurance company pay karti hai — aapko apni jeb se paisa nahi dena padta (sirf non-payable items ke liye). Reimbursement claim (vaypruti/refund) mein aap pehle poora bill khud se pay karte hain, phir insurance company se refund claim karte hain.

**Cashless** sirf network hospitals mein hoti hai, **reimbursement** kisi bhi hospital mein ho sakti hai. Cashless zyada convenient hai lekin network hospital mein hona zaroori hai. Reimbursement mein flexibility zyada hai lekin pehle kharcha uthana padta hai.

**Best strategy:** Cashless try karo pehle, agar network hospital nahi mil raha toh reimbursement use karo. Dono ke liye **24 ghante ke andar intimation zaroori hai!**

## TL;DR — Key Points Yaad Rakhne Wale

- **Cashless = insurer directly hospital ko pay karta hai** — aap sirf non-covered items pay karo
- **Reimbursement = pehle khud pay karo, baad mein refund lo** — kisi bhi hospital mein kaam karta hai
- **Cashless sirf network hospitals mein** — har insurer ke 5,000-15,000+ network hospitals hain
- **Reimbursement kisi BHI hospital mein kaam karta hai** — non-network bhi chalega
- **Cashless approval: 4-24 ghante** — reimbursement settlement: 15-30 din
- **Dono ke liye 24-48 ghante ke andar intimation zaroori** hai hospitalization ke baad
- **Cashless mein rejection rate kam hai** — pre-approval se pehle hi pata chal jaata hai

## Cashless Claim: Complete Process

### Planned Hospitalization (Pehle Se Plan Ki Hui Treatment)

1. **Network hospital choose karo** — insurer ka hospital list online check karo
2. **Pre-authorization form submit karo** — admission se 3-5 din pehle
3. **Insurer review karega** — approval/rejection 4-24 ghante mein
4. **Admission lo** — ID + health card + approval letter dikhao
5. **Treatment hoti hai** — hospital directly insurer se coordinate karta hai
6. **Enhancement request** (agar zaroorat ho) — agar bill initial approval se zyada ho jaaye
7. **Discharge lo** — sirf non-covered items pay karo (bill ka 5-10%)
8. **Settlement complete!** — insurer directly hospital ko pay karta hai

### Emergency Hospitalization (Achanak Hospital Jana Pad Gaye)

1. **Turant admission lo** — emergency mein approval ka wait mat karo
2. **24 ghante ke andar insurer ko batao** — helpline call karo ya app use karo
3. **48 ghante ke andar pre-auth submit karo** — hospital insurance desk madad karega
4. **Insurer process karega** — emergency approvals tez hote hain (2-4 ghante)
5. **Discharge lo** — same process jaise planned mein

## Reimbursement Claim: Complete Process

1. **Kisi bhi hospital mein treatment karao** — poora bill khud se pay karo
2. **SAARI documents collect karo** — discharge summary, bills, prescriptions, reports
3. **Claim form submit karo** — discharge ke 15-30 din ke andar (insurer pe depend karta hai)
4. **Insurer review karega** — 15-30 din processing time
5. **Query aayi toh** — 7-15 din ke andar jawab do
6. **Settlement hoga** — amount aapke bank account mein credit hoga
7. **Deductions hongi** — non-payable items, co-pay, room rent excess kat jaayega

## Cashless Kab Choose Karein

### ✅ Best for:
- Planned surgeries aur treatments
- Network hospital paas mein available hai
- Aapke paas bada bill pehle pay karne ke paise nahi hain
- Emergency hospitalization (cashless shuru karo, zaroorat pe switch karo)
- Mahangi treatment (₹2L+)

### ❌ Nahi ho payega jab:
- Hospital insurer ke network mein nahi hai
- Insurer ki TPA ne pre-authorization reject kar di
- Aap aise area mein hain jahan network hospital nahi hai
- Emergency mein non-network hospital mein gaye

## Reimbursement Kab Choose Karein

### ✅ Best for:
- Aapka preferred doctor non-network hospital mein hai
- Emergency mein sabse paas wala hospital non-network hai
- Chhoti claims (₹10,000-₹30,000) — easily manage ho jaayega
- Network hospital ne pre-auth deny kar diya
- Daycare procedures non-network facility mein

### ❌ Not ideal jab:
- Bada bill expected hai (₹2L+) — paisa jama karna mushkil
- Aapke paas savings ya credit limit nahi hai
- Documentation complex lag rahi hai
- Paise jaldi chahiye doosre kaam ke liye

## Documents Jo Chahiye Honge

### Cashless Claim Ke Liye
- Health card / policy document
- ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Pre-authorization form (hospital deta hai)
- Doctor ki admission recommendation

### Reimbursement Claim Ke Liye
- Claim form (bharkar sign karna hai)
- Original discharge summary
- Original hospital bills (payment receipts ke saath)
- Original prescriptions (consultation + dawaiyan)
- Diagnostic reports (blood, X-ray, MRI, etc.)
- Pharmacy bills with prescriptions
- ID proof aur policy document ki copy
- Bank details NEFT transfer ke liye
- FIR copy (agar accident case hai)
- Cancelled cheque ya bank passbook copy

## Common Galtiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

### 1. Network Hospital List Check Nahi Karna
Admission se pehle verify karo ki hospital network mein hai ya nahi. Non-network hospital mein cashless nahi hoga — reimbursement karna padega jo zyada troublesome hai.

### 2. Late Intimation (Der Se Batana)
IRDAI rules ke mutaabiq 24 ghante (emergency) ya 48 ghante (planned) mein intimation zaroori hai. Late intimation se claim reject ho sakta hai. Phone call bhi kaafi hai — bas record rakh lo!

### 3. Pre-Authorization Nahi LENA
Cashless claim ke liye pre-auth MUST hai. Bina pre-auth ke treatment shuru karne pe cashless nahi hoga — reimbursement hi file karna padega, jo zyada time leta hai.

### 4. Original Bills Khona
Reimbursement claim ke liye original bills zaroori hain. Duplicate bills se claim reject ho sakta hai. Bills ki photocopies bhi rakh lo backup ke liye. Digital photos bhi le lo har bill ki!

### 5. Non-Payable Items Check Nahi Karna
Cashless mein bhi kuch items non-payable hote hain — attendant charges, telephone, non-medical items. Yeh aapko pay karne honge. Discharge time pe bill carefully check karo aur samjho kya cut hua aur kyun.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Aksar Puche Jaate Sawaal

### 1. Cashless aur reimbursement mein kya farak hai?
Cashless mein insurance company directly hospital ko pay karti hai — aapko paisa nahi dena padta. Reimbursement mein aap pehle khud se pay karte hain, phir insurance company se refund claim karte hain. Cashless network hospitals mein hota hai, reimbursement kisi bhi hospital mein. Seedha samjho: Cashless = direct payment, Reimbursement = pehle aap pay karein, baad mein refund lo.

### 2. Kya non-network hospital mein cashless ho sakta hai?
Aam taur pe nahi. Cashless facility sirf network hospitals mein available hai. Kuch insurers "preferred provider" arrangement dete hain non-network hospitals ke saath, lekin yeh bahut rare hai. Apne insurer se check karo pehle.

### 3. Kya reimbursement claim reject ho sakti hai?
Haan, aur rejection rate reimbursement mein thodi zyada hai kyunki documents mein discrepancies aati hain. Original bills, discharge summary, aur prescriptions complete honi chahiye. Incomplete documentation #1 rejection reason hai — isliye har document save karo!

### 4. Cashless claim mein kitna paisa khud se dena padta hai?
Typically bill ka 5-10% — non-payable items jaise attendant charges, telephone, non-medical items, aur co-pay (agar policy mein hai). Baaki 90-95% insurer directly pay karta hai.

### 5. Kya dono (cashless + reimbursement) ek saath claim kar sakte hain?
Ek hi hospitalization ke liye ek hi method use hota hai. Lekin agar cashless partially approved hua (sirf kuch amount), toh remaining amount reimbursement se claim kar sakte hain. Yeh common practice hai.

### 6. Reimbursement claim kitne din mein file karni chahiye?
Most insurers require reimbursement claim within 15-30 days of discharge. Late filing se claim reject ho sakta hai. Jaldi file karo — saari documents ek saath le kar jaao.

### 7. Cashless reject ho gaya toh kya karein?
Pehle reason samjho — KYC issue, PED non-disclosure, ya policy condition. Phir reimbursement file karo. Aur humse contact karo WhatsApp pe — hum help karenge claim properly file karne mein.
