## Key Takeaway

AYUSH treatment (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) health insurance mein covered hota hai — IRDAI ne 2013 se yeh mandate kiya hai ki sabhi health insurance plans AYUSH treatments cover karein. Lekin conditions hain: treatment kisi recognized AYUSH hospital mein honi chahiye, doctor qualified hona chahiye, aur hospitalization zaroori hai (OPD generally nahi covered). Coverage limit typically sum insured ka 25% ya ₹25,000-₹50,000 tak hota hai. Kuch premium plans mein AYUSH ka separate sub-limit nahi hai — full sum insured applicable hai. Agar aap Ayurvedic ya Homeopathic treatment prefer karte hain, toh AYUSH coverage wali policy choose karein!

## TL;DR — Key Points

- **AYUSH is MANDATORY in all health insurance** — IRDAI rule since 2013
- **Covers: Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy**
- **Hospitalization required** — OPD AYUSH generally not covered
- **Coverage limit: up to sum insured** (some plans have 25% sub-limit)
- **Must be at recognized AYUSH hospital** with qualified practitioner
- **No separate premium** — included in base health plan
- **Useful for: chronic conditions, joint pain, skin diseases, digestive issues**

## What is AYUSH Treatment?

AYUSH stands for:
- **A** — Ayurveda (ancient Indian medicine system)
- **Y** — Yoga & Naturopathy (physical and natural healing)
- **U** — Unani (Greco-Arab medicine)
- **S** — Siddha (Tamil medicine system)
- **H** — Homeopathy (like cures like principle)

### AYUSH Treatment Kya Hai? (Hindi)

AYUSH paanch traditional medicine systems hain jo India mein widely use hoti hain. Ayurveda sabse popular hai — joint pain, skin problems, digestive issues ke liye log Ayurvedic treatment lete hain. IRDAI ne mandate kiya hai ki sabhi health insurance plans in treatments ko cover karein, lekin hospitalization zaroori hai aur recognized facility mein treatment hona chahiye.

## Conditions for AYUSH Claim Approval

### ✅ Must Have:
1. **Hospitalization of 24+ hours** — daycare AYUSH procedures limited
2. **Treatment at recognized AYUSH hospital** — government or accredited
3. **Qualified AYUSH practitioner** — registered with AYUSH ministry
4. **Medical necessity** — doctor's recommendation required
5. **Proper documentation** — discharge summary, treatment records, prescriptions

### ❌ NOT Covered:
1. OPD AYUSH consultations (without hospitalization)
2. Over-the-counter Ayurvedic medicines
3. Self-prescribed Homeopathic remedies
4. Treatment at non-recognized facilities
5. Yoga classes at fitness centers (not hospital-based)
6. Cosmetic/wellness Ayurvedic treatments
7. AYUSH for preventive purposes only

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Kya Ayurvedic treatment health insurance se covered hai?
Haan! IRDAI ne mandate kiya hai ki sabhi health insurance plans AYUSH (Ayurveda included) cover karein. Lekin treatment kisi recognized AYUSH hospital mein honi chahiye aur hospitalization zaroori hai. OPD Ayurvedic treatment generally nahi cover hota. (Ayurvedic upchar bima mein shamil hai)

### 2. Kya homeopathic treatment insurance se claim ho sakti hai?
Haan, agar hospitalization ho aur recognized Homeopathic hospital mein treatment ho. Lekh Homeopathic OPD consultation aur over-the-counter medicines nahi cover hote. Claim file karne ke liye discharge summary aur proper documentation zaroori hai.

### 3. AYUSH ke liye kya premium extra lagta hai?
Nahi! AYUSH coverage base health insurance plan mein included hota hai — koi alag premium nahi lagta. IRDAI ne mandatory kiya hai, toh sabhi plans mein yeh feature available hai. Lekin coverage limits different ho sakti hain.

### 4. Kya Panchakarma insurance se covered hai?
Haan, agar Panchakarma kisi recognized Ayurvedic hospital mein hospitalized patient ke liye ho aur medical necessity ho. Wellness/spa Panchakarma nahi cover hoga. Doctor ki recommendation zaroori hai.

### 5. AYUSH claim ke liye kya documents chahiye?
Discharge summary (AYUSH doctor dwara), treatment details, medicine prescriptions, hospital registration proof (AYUSH ministry recognition), diagnostic reports (if any), aur bills. Cashless ke liye pre-authorization form bhi.

### 6. Kya AYUSH aur Allopathy dono se same condition ka claim ho sakta hai?
Nahi, same condition ke liye ek hi treatment system ka claim ho sakta hai — ya toh Allopathy ya AYUSH. Dono se simultaneously claim nahi hoga. Choose one system and file claim accordingly.
