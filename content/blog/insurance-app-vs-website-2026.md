## Quick Verdict

For buying insurance, a website (mobile-responsive) is better than a dedicated app for most users. Websites offer full feature parity, no storage usage, and work across all devices. Apps are better only if you frequently file claims or need instant policy access offline.

## What is Insurance App?

A dedicated mobile application (iOS/Android) provided by an insurer or aggregator. Examples: ACKO app, Policybazaar app, HDFC ERGO app.

## What is Insurance Website?

A mobile-responsive website accessible via any browser. Examples: paliwalsecure.in, insurer websites, comparison portals.

## Feature Comparison Table

| Feature | Insurance App | Insurance Website |
|---|---|---|
| Storage required | 50-200 MB per app | Zero — runs in browser |
| Works offline | Yes (limited — policy access) | No (requires internet) |
| Push notifications | Yes (renewal reminders, claim updates) | No (unless you enable browser notifications) |
| Biometric login | Yes (fingerprint/face ID) | No (or limited — WebAuthn support varies) |
| Plan comparison | Limited (usually single insurer) | Full (multi-insurer comparison) |
| Document upload | Camera integration (easier) | File picker (slightly more steps) |
| Cross-device access | No (only on installed device) | Yes (any device with browser) |
| Update required | Yes (manual or auto app updates) | No (always latest version) |

## Who Should Choose Insurance App?

You file claims frequently, need offline access to your policy, or prefer push notifications for renewal reminders. Apps also offer biometric login (fingerprint/face) for faster access.

## Who Should Choose Insurance Website?

You want to compare plans across insurers, read detailed policy wording, or buy insurance for the first time. Websites offer larger screens for comparison tables and easier document upload.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is buying insurance through an app cheaper?
**A:** No. The premium is the same whether you buy via app, website, or through a POSP. However, some digital-only insurers (like ACKO) may offer lower premiums regardless of purchase channel because they have no agent commission.

### Q: Which is safer — app or website?
**A:** Both are equally safe if you're on the official insurer's app/website. Always check for HTTPS (lock icon) in the URL. Avoid clicking links in SMS/emails — type the URL directly.

### Q: Can I switch from app to website?
**A:** Yes. Your policy is linked to your PAN/mobile number, not the app. You can access your policy on the insurer's website using the same login credentials.

### Q: Do I need to install multiple insurer apps?
**A:** No. If you buy through a POSP like Paliwal Secure, you manage all your policies in one place — no need for multiple apps. We provide a unified dashboard for all your insurance policies.

### Q: What if I lose my phone with the insurance app?
**A:** Your policy is safe — it's stored on the insurer's servers. Reinstall the app on a new device and log in. Or access your policy via the insurer's website.
