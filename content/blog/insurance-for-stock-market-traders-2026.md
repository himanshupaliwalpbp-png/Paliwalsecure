## Quick Answer

Traders need: health insurance (₹6,000-12,000/year — high-stress profession), term life (₹4,000-10,000/year), cyber insurance (₹1,500-3,000/year — trading account hack), equipment insurance (₹1,500-3,000/year). Trading income is irregular — insurance provides stability.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## Benefits

- Financial protection against specific risks
- Tax benefits under applicable sections (80D, 80C, 37(1))
- Peace of mind for you and your family
- Legal defense coverage where applicable
- Business continuity for business insurance

## Limitations

- Waiting periods for pre-existing conditions (health insurance)
- Specific exclusions vary by policy — read the wording
- Sum insured limits may be insufficient for catastrophic events
- Premium increases with age

## FAQ

### Q: Do stock market traders need special insurance?
**A:** Yes. High-stress, sedentary profession with health risks. Health insurance essential. Term insurance for income replacement. Cyber insurance for trading account hack.

### Q: Can traders claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes if trading is primary business. Health: 80D. Term: 80C. Cyber + equipment: Section 37(1). Both regimes for business expenses.

### Q: What is cyber insurance for traders?
**A:** Covers trading account hack, demat breach, phishing, ransomware. One demat hack can result in ₹5-50L stolen shares. Premium: ₹1,500-3,000/year.

### Q: Is term insurance necessary for traders?
**A:** Absolutely. Irregular income — family needs protection. Term (₹1Cr) costs ₹4,000-10,000/year. Buy before 35.

### Q: Does health insurance cover stress-related illnesses?
**A:** Yes. Covers hospitalization for cardiac, hypertension, mental health (IRDAI mandate 2022). Buy BEFORE health issues develop.
