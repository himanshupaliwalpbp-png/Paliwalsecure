## Key Takeaway

Health insurance portability (health insurance badhna ya badalna) ek IRDAI ka rule hai jo aapko ek insurance company se doosri mein switch karne deta hai bina apne waiting period credits khoye. Yeh facility 2011 se available hai aur iske tahat aapki cumulative bonus, waiting period credits, aur pre-existing disease coverage sab transfer ho jaati hai naye insurer ke paas. Aapko renewal se 45 din pehle portability apply karni hoti hai. Agar aap apni current insurer se不满意 hain — badka premium, poor claim service, ya low coverage — toh port karein, policy lapse mat hone dena!

## TL;DR — Key Points

- **Portability allows switching insurers** without losing waiting period credits
- **Apply 45 days before renewal** — late applications can be rejected
- **All waiting period credits transfer** — initial, PED, specific treatment
- **Cumulative bonus (NCB) also transfers** to the new insurer
- **New insurer CANNOT reject portability** arbitrarily — IRDAI guidelines protect you
- **Port, don't lapse** — lapsing means starting from scratch with waiting periods

## What is Health Insurance Portability?

Health insurance portability is the right given by IRDAI to policyholders to switch from one insurance company to another while carrying forward their accumulated benefits — primarily waiting period credits and no-claim bonus.

### Portability Kya Hai? (Hindi Meaning)

Portability ka matlab hai aapki purani insurance company ke saath jo bhi benefits aapne kamaye hain (waiting period credits, NCB, sum insured enhancements), woh sab naye insurer ke paas transfer ho jaayenge. Aapko nayi policy pe phir se 30 din ka initial waiting period ya 4 saal ka PED waiting period nahi bhugtana padega.

## Why Should You Port Your Health Insurance?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| High premium increase | Current insurer ne 30-40% premium badha diya without extra benefits |
| Poor claim settlement | Claims reject ho rahe hain ya bohot delay ho raha hai |
| Limited coverage | Current plan mein room rent limit hai, co-pay zyada hai, add-ons nahi mil rahe |
| Bad network hospitals | Aapke area mein network hospitals kam hain, cashless facility nahi mil rahi |
| Better plans available | Naye insurers better coverage kam premium pe de rahe hain |
| Poor customer service | Queries ka jawaab nahi milta, renewal reminders nahi aate |

## Step-by-Step Portability Process

### Step 1: Research & Compare (30-45 Days Before Renewal)
- Compare plans from different insurers using our comparison tool
- Check premium, coverage, network hospitals, claim settlement ratio
- Select the plan that suits your needs

### Step 2: Apply to New Insurer (45 Days Before Renewal)
- Fill the portability form with the new insurer
- Provide details of your current policy (insurer name, policy number, claim history)
- Submit KYC documents and medical records if required

### Step 3: New Insurer Contacts Current Insurer
- New insurer sends a portability request on the IRDAI portal
- Current insurer must share your claim history and policy details within 7 working days
- IRDAI's shared portal facilitates this data exchange

### Step 4: New Insurer Evaluates
- Reviews your medical history, claim record, and coverage requirements
- May ask for medical tests (for high sum insured or specific conditions)
- Must communicate decision within 15 days of receiving all data

### Step 5: Policy Issuance
- If approved, new policy is issued with waiting period credits
- If rejected, you can stay with current insurer (policy doesn't lapse automatically)
- Coverage starts from the date your old policy expires

## What Transfers During Portability?

| Benefit | Transfer Status | Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Initial waiting period (30 days) | ✅ Fully transfers | If completed with old insurer |
| PED waiting period credits | ✅ Fully transfers | If 2 out of 4 years completed, only 2 years left |
| Specific treatment waiting | ✅ Fully transfers | Cataract, hernia, etc. |
| Maternity waiting period | ✅ Fully transfers | If partially completed |
| No-Claim Bonus (NCB) | ✅ Transfers as sum insured enhancement | Not as discount — as additional coverage |
| Cumulative bonus | ✅ Transfers | Added to base sum insured |
| Pre-policy check-up | ❌ May be required | New insurer may ask for fresh medical tests |
| Co-payment clause | ❌ New insurer's terms apply | May be different from old insurer |

## Documents Required for Portability

1. **Portability form** (available with new insurer)
2. **Current policy document** (copy)
3. **Renewal notice** from current insurer
4. **Claim history** for last 3-4 years
5. **KYC documents** (Aadhaar, PAN, address proof)
6. **Medical records** (if any pre-existing conditions)
7. **Discharge summaries** of any previous claims

## Common Mistakes During Portability

### 1. Applying Too Late
Agar aap 45 din pehle nahi apply karte, toh new insurer reject kar sakta hai. Last moment pe apply karne se hurry mein galat plan choose karne ka risk bhi badhta hai.

### 2. Not Declaring Medical History
Portability ke time pe agar aapne apni medical history chhupayi aur claim time pe pata chala, toh new insurer claim reject kar sakta hai aur policy fraud ke tahat cancel ho sakti hai.

### 3. Letting Old Policy Lapse
Kabhi bhi purani policy lapse mat hone dena! Portability process ke dauran purani policy active rakhni hai. Agar lapse ho gayi toh waiting period credits khatam.

### 4. Not Checking New Policy Terms
Sirf premium kam dekh ke port mat karo. Room rent limit, co-pay, network hospitals, add-on availability — sab check karo. Kam premium ke chakkar mein worse coverage mil sakti hai.

### 5. Ignoring Claim Settlement Ratio
New insurer ki CSR (Claim Settlement Ratio) zaroor check karo. 85% se kam CSR wali company se door raho.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Kya main apni health insurance kisi bhi insurer pe port kar sakta hoon?
Haan, IRDAI ke tahat aap kisi bhi IRDAI-registered insurer pe port kar sakte hain. Lekin new insurer aapki application medical grounds pe reject kar sakta hai (jaise serious pre-existing conditions). Rejection arbitrary nahi ho sakta.

### 2. Portability ke time pe kya medical test hota hai?
Aapki age aur sum insured pe depend karta hai. 45 years se upar ya ₹10 Lakh se zyada sum insured ke liye medical test ho sakta hai. Kuch insurers 40+ age pe bhi test karte hain. Tests ki cost new insurer bear karta hai.

### 3. Kya NCB transfer hota hai portability pe?
Haan! Aapka no-claim bonus transfer hota hai. Lekin yeh discount ki form mein nahi, sum insured enhancement ki form mein transfer hota hai. Jaise agar aapke paas 50% NCB hai ₹5L policy pe, toh new insurer ₹7.5L coverage dega (₹5L base + ₹2.5L NCB).

### 4. Agar new insurer reject kar de toh kya hoga?
Agar new insurer aapki application reject karta hai, toh aap purane insurer ke saath continue kar sakte hain. Aapki purani policy automatically lapse nahi hoti. Lekin renewal premium time pe pay karna padega.

### 5. Kitni baar port kar sakte hain?
IRDAI ne koi limit nahi lagayi hai. Har renewal pe aap port kar sakte hain. Lekin frequently port karne se claim history complicated ho jaati hai aur insurers cautious ho jaate hain.

### 6. Portability ke time pe premium zyada ho sakta hai?
Haan, ho sakta hai. New insurer aapki age, medical history, aur claim record dekh ke premium decide karta hai. Agar aapne recent mein claim kiya hai, toh premium zyada ho sakti hai. Compare karo before applying.

### 7. Group insurance se individual insurance mein port ho sakta hai?
Haan! IRDAI ne 2020 mein yeh rule banaya hai ki group insurance se individual insurance mein portability possible hai. Waiting period credits transfer hote hain. Yeh bahut useful hai job change ke time pe.
