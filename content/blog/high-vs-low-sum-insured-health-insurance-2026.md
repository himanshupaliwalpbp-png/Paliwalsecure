## Quick Answer

A high sum insured (₹50L-1Cr) protects against major medical events (cancer, cardiac surgery, ICU stays) which can cost ₹10-30L in metros. A low sum insured (₹3-5L) may suffice for minor hospitalization but is insufficient for critical illness. Recommendation: minimum ₹10L for individuals, ₹25L+ for families in metros.

**At a glance:**

| Parameter | High Sum Insured (₹50L-1Cr) | Low Sum Insured (₹3-5L) |
|---|---|---|
| Sum insured | ₹50L to ₹1Cr | ₹3L to ₹5L |
| Premium (30yr) | ₹15,000-30,000/year | ₹4,000-7,000/year |
| Covers major illness | Yes (cancer, cardiac, transplant) | Insufficient (₹3-5L exhausted in 3-5 days ICU) |
| ICU coverage | Full (₹50,000-1L/day) | Partial (2-3 days max) |
| Surgery coverage | Full (incl. transplant) | Partial (minor surgeries only) |
| Restoration benefit | Available (restores if exhausted) | Not available |
| Room rent | No limit (private AC room) | 1% of SI (shared/general ward) |
| Metro adequacy | Sufficient (covers metro costs) | Insufficient (metro costs ₹10L+ for major illness) |
| Best for | Families, seniors, metro residents | Young, single, tier-3 cities |

## Exclusions Comparison

Both High Sum Insured (₹50L-1Cr) and Low Sum Insured (₹3-5L) have standard health insurance exclusions (per IRDAI guidelines):
- Pre-existing conditions (during waiting period)
- Cosmetic/plastic surgery (unless accident-related)
- Dental treatment (unless due to accident)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- War, nuclear events, terrorism

**Plan-specific exclusions vary.** Always read the policy wording.

## Claim Process

### Cashless Claim (if applicable)
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours
2. Visit network hospital
3. Hospital sends pre-authorization
4. Insurer approves within 4-24 hours
5. Treatment proceeds (no upfront payment)

### Reimbursement Claim
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours (planned) / 7 days (emergency)
2. Get treated at any hospital
3. Submit documents within 15-30 days
4. Insurer reimburses within 7-15 working days
