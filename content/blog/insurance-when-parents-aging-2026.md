## Quick Answer

When parents age (60+): buy senior citizen health insurance BEFORE conditions develop (₹15,000-40,000/year for ₹10L), increase sum insured (₹10L minimum), buy personal accident cover (₹500-1,500/year), review life insurance nominees, plan for long-term care.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Can I buy health insurance for parents with pre-existing conditions?
**A:** Yes, with waiting period (2-4 years). Some senior plans have shorter (1-2 years). Declare ALL conditions. Buy early for shorter waiting + lower premium.

### Q: How much health insurance do aging parents need?
**A:** Minimum ₹10L per parent. Senior hospital stays average ₹50,000-2L. One major illness ₹5-15L. ₹20L+ if in metro city.

### Q: Is there tax benefit for parents health insurance?
**A:** Yes. 80D (old regime): ₹50,000 for senior parents. Plus ₹25,000 for self. Total ₹75,000. If both senior: ₹1,00,000. Saves up to ₹31,200 (30% slab).

### Q: Should I buy critical illness cover for parents?
**A:** Yes if affordable. Pays lump sum on diagnosis. Covers non-hospitalized treatment (chemo, dialysis). Valuable for seniors.

### Q: What is long-term care insurance?
**A:** Covers home nursing, assisted living, caregiver expenses — NOT covered by standard health insurance. Essential for mobility/cognitive issues. Premium: ₹10,000-25,000/year.
