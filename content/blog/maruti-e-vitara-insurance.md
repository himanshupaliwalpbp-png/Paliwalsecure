## Best Add-ons for Maruti e Vitara

### 1. Battery Depreciation Cover — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Cost: ₹2,500-₹4,000/yr
- Saves: ₹15-18L on battery replacement after 5+ years
- Without this, battery degradation is YOUR cost

### 2. Zero Depreciation — MUST for first 5 years
- Cost: ₹3,200-₹4,000/yr
- Saves: ₹35,000-₹60,000 on accident claims
- EV body panels are 30-40% costlier than ICE

### 3. Charging Port & Cable Cover
- Cost: ₹1,000-₹1,500/yr
- Covers: Charging port damage, cable theft
- EV-specific: Not available with all insurers

### 4. Return to Invoice — Recommended for Year 1-2
- Cost: ₹2,000-₹3,000/yr
- Pays: Full ex-showroom + registration on total loss
- New EV = higher theft risk

### 5. NCB Protector
- Cost: ₹800-₹1,200/yr
- Protects: 50% NCB discount after a claim
- Maruti's low claim ratio makes this achievable

## EV-Specific Claim Process for e Vitara

### Cashless Claim (Recommended)
1. Call insurer within 48 hours of incident
2. Take vehicle to Maruti ARENA (EV-certified) workshop
3. Surveyor inspects — mention EV-specific damage (battery, charging port)
4. Workshop sends estimate to insurer
5. Approval in 24-48 hours for EV claims
6. You pay only depreciation + compulsory excess

### Battery Damage Claim
1. Battery damage is assessed separately from body damage
2. Battery depreciation cover kicks in for capacity loss
3. If battery capacity <70% after 5 years → covered under battery add-on
4. Physical battery damage (accident) → covered under comprehensive

## FAQ

### Q1: e Vitara ka insurance kitna padega?
First year comprehensive ₹18,000-₹32,000, battery cover extra ₹2,500-₹4,000. Zero dep ₹3,200-₹4,000 extra. Total with all add-ons: ₹25,000-₹42,000/yr.

### Q2: Kya battery cover lena zaroori hai?
BILKUL YES! Battery replacement ₹15-18L ka kharcha hai. Battery depreciation cover ₹2,500-₹4,000/yr mein ₹18L+ ki protection milti hai. Bina iske 5 saal baad battery kharab hui toh poora paisa aapka.

### Q3: Maruti ki EV warranty se battery cover nahi hota?
Warranty covers manufacturing defects for 8 years/1.6L km. Lekin accident damage, charging port damage, aur natural degradation ke liye INSURANCE chahiye. Warranty ≠ Insurance.

### Q4: Charging port kharab ho gaya toh?
Charging port damage ₹15K-₹50K repair. Standard policy mein ye cover nahi hota. Charging port cover add-on ₹1,000-₹1,500/yr mein ye protection milta hai.

### Q5: Third party insurance kitna hai e Vitara ka?
IRDAI fixed rate: ₹6,736/yr for EVs above 1500cc equivalent. Ye legally mandatory hai. Lekin comprehensive lena zyada smart hai — battery, motor, sab cover hota hai.
