## Quick Answer

When changing jobs: group health insurance ends on last working day — buy individual BEFORE resigning. Term life continues if personal (not employer). Never have a coverage gap, even for one day.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: What happens to my employer health insurance when I change jobs?
**A:** It ends on your last working day. No grace period. Buy individual health insurance BEFORE resigning to avoid coverage gap.

### Q: Can I port my employer group health insurance to individual?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI portability allows conversion. Apply 45 days before leaving. Waiting period credits transfer. Premium will be higher.

### Q: Does my term life insurance continue when I change jobs?
**A:** Yes, if personal term policy. Independent of employer. If only group term, that ends — buy personal term before resigning.

### Q: Should I buy health insurance before or after joining new company?
**A:** BEFORE resigning. Buy individual while still employed. Ensures zero coverage gap. New employer group cover may take 30-90 days to activate.

### Q: What insurance should I update when changing jobs?
**A:** Buy individual health + term (if only had group). Update address if relocating. Review sum insured — new salary may warrant higher cover.
