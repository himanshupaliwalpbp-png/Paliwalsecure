## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Maruti Brezza

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Brezza ke body panels, headlamps, aur bumper replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai bina zero dep ke
2. **Engine Protection** — 1.5L engine ke liye waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover — monsoon cities mein must
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹11,000-₹17,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Maruti ka vast service network is add-on ko aur effective banata hai
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye Brezza ke liye smart investment

## 📋 Maruti Brezza Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Maruti Arena service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Maruti Brezza Insurance

**Q: Maruti Brezza ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Maruti Brezza (1000-1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹6,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Zero dep cover se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Brezza ke liye yeh bahut useful hai, especially agar car new hai. Ek claim mein ₹5,000-₹15,000 tak bacha sakta hai.

**Q: Maruti Brezza ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Brezza ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹4,400-₹17,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Brezza CNG ka insurance premium kya hoga?**
A: Factory-fitted CNG variant ka IDV CNG kit ke saath calculate hota hai, toh premium thoda zyada hoti hai. Aftermarket CNG ke liye separate endorsement zaroori hai — ₹500-₹1,500 extra lagega.
