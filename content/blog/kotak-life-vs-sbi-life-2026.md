## FAQ

### Q: Which has better CSR — Kotak Life or SBI Life?
**A:** Kotak Life: 98.0%. SBI Life: 97.8%. 0.2% difference negligible. Both excellent.

### Q: Which is cheaper?
**A:** Generally Kotak Life 5-10% cheaper for online term. Get quotes from both for your profile.

### Q: Are both safe?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI-regulated. SBI Life backed by SBI + BNP Paribas. Kotak Life backed by Kotak Mahindra Bank. No Indian life insurer has defaulted on death claims.

### Q: Can I port between Kotak and SBI Life?
**A:** No. Life insurance portability does not exist. Buy new policy, stop old (lapses). Term has no surrender value.

### Q: Which has better digital experience?
**A:** Kotak Life: superior (app claims, WhatsApp, instant policy). SBI Life: improving but branch-reliant. Tech-savvy: Kotak. In-person: SBI.
