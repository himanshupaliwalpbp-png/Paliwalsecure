## Quick Answer

Truck owners need: (1) Commercial vehicle insurance (mandatory — covers third party + own damage, ₹15,000-40,000/year), (2) Goods in transit insurance (cargo cover — ₹3,000-10,000/year), (3) Public liability (₹5,000-15,000/year), (4) Workers' compensation (driver/helper — ₹3,000-8,000/year). Motor Vehicles Act mandates commercial vehicle insurance — driving without it is illegal.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Is commercial vehicle insurance mandatory for trucks?
**A:** Yes. Motor Vehicles Act 1988 Section 146 mandates third-party insurance for ALL commercial vehicles. Driving without it is a fine of ₹2,000 + 3 months imprisonment (first offense). Commercial vehicle premium is 50-100% higher than private vehicle (higher risk, passenger/goods liability).

### Q: What is goods in transit insurance for trucks?
**A:** Covers damage/loss of cargo during transportation. Essential for: high-value goods (electronics, pharmaceuticals), perishable goods (cold chain), and fragile items. Premium: 0.1-0.3% of cargo value per trip, or annual policy (₹3,000-10,000/year for ₹10L cargo). Some consignors require GIT before booking.

### Q: Does truck insurance cover driver injuries?
**A:** Only with personal accident cover (mandatory for owner-driver under IRDAI: ₹15L). For hired drivers: workers' compensation insurance covers on-duty injuries. Without WC, the truck owner is personally liable for driver medical costs + compensation. Premium: ₹3,000-8,000/year per driver.

### Q: Can truck owners claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All truck insurance (commercial vehicle, GIT, PL, WC) deductible under Section 37(1) as business expense. Both old and new tax regimes. Reduces taxable trucking income. Also claim depreciation on vehicle + fuel + maintenance.

### Q: What is the penalty for driving a truck without insurance?
**A:** First offense: ₹2,000 fine + 3 months imprisonment. Repeat offense: ₹4,000 fine + 3 months imprisonment. Plus: if the uninsured truck causes an accident, the owner is personally liable for ALL third-party damages (unlimited liability). One accident claim can exceed ₹50L — insurance is cheaper than the risk.
