## Quick Answer

Go Digit (95.8% CSR, digital-first, competitive pricing) vs TATA AIG (96.5% CSR, 18+ add-ons, TATA brand trust). Digit is cheaper and app-based. TATA AIG has more add-on options and higher CSR. Choose Digit for pricing + digital, TATA AIG for customization + brand trust.

## Claim Process

1. **Inform insurer** within 48 hours of the event
2. **Submit claim form** with required documents (ID, policy, medical/FIR/bills)
3. **Insurer verifies** documents and circumstances
4. **Approval/rejection** within 7-30 days
5. **Payout** within 7-15 working days of approval

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Which has a higher CSR — Digit or TATA AIG?
**A:** TATA AIG: 96.5% (IRDAI 2024-25). Digit: 95.8%. The 0.7% difference is minor — both are above industry average.

### Q: Which is cheaper — Digit or TATA AIG?
**A:** Digit is typically 10-15% cheaper (digital-only insurer, no agent commission). TATA AIG is competitive but slightly higher. Get quotes from both.

### Q: Which has better add-ons?
**A:** TATA AIG offers 18+ add-ons (more flexible). Digit offers essential add-ons (zero-dep, engine protect, RTI) with simpler plans. For customization: TATA AIG. For simplicity + price: Digit.

### Q: Are both safe?
**A:** Yes. Both IRDAI-regulated. Digit is backed by Fairfax (Canada). TATA AIG is backed by TATA Group + AIG (USA). Both maintain required solvency margins.

### Q: Can I port between Digit and TATA AIG?
**A:** Yes. Apply for portability 45 days before renewal. NCB and waiting period credits transfer. Get help from a POSP for smooth transition.
