## How to Calculate Mahindra Scorpio-N IDV

IDV (Insured Declared Value) = Current market value of your Mahindra Scorpio-N.

**Formula:** Ex-showroom price × (1 - depreciation %)

| Vehicle Age | Depreciation % | IDV Calculation |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| New (0-6 months) | 5% | Ex-showroom × 0.95 |
| 6 months - 1 year | 15% | Ex-showroom × 0.85 |
| 1-2 years | 20% | Ex-showroom × 0.80 |
| 2-3 years | 30% | Ex-showroom × 0.70 |
| 3-4 years | 40% | Ex-showroom × 0.60 |
| 4-5 years | 50% | Ex-showroom × 0.50 |
| 5+ years | Mutual agreement | Market value assessed |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Underinsuring (Low IDV)
**Risk:** At claim time, you get much less than vehicle's actual value
**Fix:** Set IDV to 85% of current market value

### ❌ Mistake 2: Skipping Zero Dep
**Risk:** Claim payout reduced by 25-50% due to depreciation
**Fix:** Pay ₹1,500-3,000 extra for zero dep — saves ₹5,000-15,000 per claim

### ❌ Mistake 3: Not Comparing Insurers
**Risk:** Paying 15-25% more than necessary
**Fix:** Get quotes from 3+ insurers before buying

### ❌ Mistake 4: Forgetting NCB Transfer
**Risk:** Losing 20-50% NCB discount when changing insurer
**Fix:** Transfer NCB certificate from old to new insurer

### ❌ Mistake 5: Missing Renewal Date
**Risk:** Policy lapse — vehicle inspection required + lost NCB
**Fix:** Set auto-reminder 15 days before renewal + auto-debit
