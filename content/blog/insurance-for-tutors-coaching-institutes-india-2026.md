## FAQ

### Q: Can a coaching institute be sued if student is injured?
**A:** Yes. Consumer Protection Act 2019. Faulty chair, slippery floor, unsafe electrical = institute liable. PL covers defense + settlement.

### Q: Do online tutors need insurance?
**A:** Yes. PI covers wrong guidance, data breach, copyright infringement. Even online — parent can sue for educational negligence.

### Q: Is student accident cover needed for coaching centers?
**A:** Yes if students visit premises. ₹50-100/student/year for ₹1-2L per student. One fracture claim can exceed entire year premium.

### Q: Can coaching centers claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All coaching insurance under Section 37(1). Health and life for owner under 80D/80C (old regime).

### Q: What insurance do home tutors need?
**A:** Minimum: health (₹5,000-10,000) + term (₹3,000-6,000). Add PL (₹3,000-5,000) if students visit home. Add PI (₹5,000-8,000) if teaching online.
