## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Honda City

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Honda City ke premium parts (LED headlamps, chrome grille, sedan body panels) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — 1.5L i-VTEC engine repair costs significant hain — waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover karta hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹13,000-₹20,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — sedan owners ke liye highway drives pe useful
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹12-17 Lakh ki investment ke liye must

## 📋 Honda City Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Honda Cars authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Honda City Insurance

**Q: Honda City ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Honda City (above 1500cc, 1498cc) ka third-party premium ₹7,000/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Honda City ke premium parts (LED headlamps, sedan body panels) mahenge hain. Zero dep se ek claim mein ₹10,000-₹30,000 tak bacha sakte hain. 5 saal se purani nahi hai toh must hai.

**Q: Honda City ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Honda City ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹5,200-₹20,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Honda City CVT ka insurance alag hota hai?**
A: CVT automatic variant ka ex-showroom price zyada hai, toh IDV bhi zyada hoti hai aur premium thoda higher hota hai. CVT gearbox repair bhi expensive hai — engine protect add-on consider karein.
