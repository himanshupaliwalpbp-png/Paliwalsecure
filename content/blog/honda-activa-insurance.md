## Activa Insurance Claim Process

### Step-by-Step Claim

1. **Inform Insurer**: 24-48 hours mein call/app se inform karein
2. **Photos Click Karein**: Damage photos 4 angles se
3. **Honda Service Center**: Nearest authorized center par le jaayein
4. **Documents**: RC, DL, policy copy
5. **FIR**: Sirf theft/vandalism/third-party injury mein
6. **Survey**: Surveyor inspect karega
7. **Repair**: Approval ke baad repair start
8. **Payment**: Cashless mein deductible only; reimbursement mein bills submit

### Activa Claim Tips

- **Small dents/scratches**: ₹500-₹1,000 ke repair — out-of-pocket karein, NCB bachega
- **Theft common hai**: Activa ki theft rate high hai — comprehensive zaroor lein
- **Monsoon damage**: Waterlogging mein engine seize — engine protect nahi milta scooters mein, lekin comprehensive mein water damage covered hai
- **Cashless preferred**: Honda ke authorized centers par cashless smooth hota hai

## Online Renewal Guide

### Quick 5-Minute Renewal Steps

1. **Visit**: Insurer website ya aggregator (PolicyBazaar, Acko, etc.)
2. **Enter**: Registration number ya previous policy number
3. **Verify**: Vehicle details aur previous policy info
4. **Choose**: Comprehensive ya TP only
5. **Add-ons**: Zero dep + PA select karein
6. **IDV**: Verify karein — fair value par set ho
7. **Pay**: UPI/Card/Net Banking
8. **Download**: Instant e-policy PDF

### Renewal Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Sirf TP lena — comprehensive mein theft + own damage cover hota hai
- ❌ IDV kam rakhna premium bachane ke liye — theft mein kam mileagega
- ❌ Zero dep skip karna — ₹300-₹700 mein bahut value hai
- ❌ Last day par renewal — lapse hone par NCB zero
- ❌ Compare na karna — kam se kam 3 insurers check karein

## FAQs

### 1. Honda Activa ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Activa 6G ka third-party premium ₹1,414/year (IRDAI fixed for 75-150cc) hai. Comprehensive insurance ₹1,200-₹3,500/year hota hai variant aur add-ons ke hisaab se. Zero dep ke saath ₹1,600-₹3,500. Long-term 5-year TP policy ₹7,649 mein bhi available hai jo renewal hassle khatam karta hai.

### 2. Kya Activa ka sirf third-party insurance kaafi hai?

Nahi, third-party insurance sirf doosron ko hui damage cover karta hai — aapki Activa ka apna damage, theft, ya fire cover nahi hota. Activa ki theft rate India mein bahut high hai — isliye comprehensive insurance strongly recommended hai. ₹500-₹1,500 extra mein full protection milta hai.

### 3. Zero dep add-on Activa ke liye lena chahiye?

Haan, Activa ke liye zero dep ₹300-₹700/year mein milta hai aur ek claim mein ₹1,500-₹4,000 bacha sakta hai. Activa ke plastic parts (mudguard, side panel, headlamp) replacement mein depreciation 50% tak hoti hai bina zero dep ke. Ye add-on value for money hai.

### 4. Activa chori ho jaaye toh kya karein?

FIR file karein (must), insurer ko inform karein 48 hours ke andar, RC copy + policy copy + FIR copy submit karein, aur NCB retention certificate lein. Comprehensive insurance ke under IDV mileagega. Return to Invoice add-on hai toh full ex-showroom price mileagegi.

### 5. 5-year TP policy better hai ya yearly?

5-year TP policy better hai kyunki: no yearly renewal tension, premium inflation se protected (locked rate), aur NCB bhi maintain rehta hai. Cost ₹7,649 for 5 years vs ₹1,414 yearly = marginal upfront saving but huge convenience. New Activa ke saath 5-year TP mandatory bhi hai as per IRDAI.

### 6. Online insurance kharidna safe hai?

Haan, 100% safe! IRDAI regulates all online insurance. Digital policy physical copy ke barabar valid hai. Online kharidne ke fayde: instantly milta hai, compare kar sakte hain, discount milta hai (no agent commission), aur renewal bhi easy hai. Acko, Digit, ICICI Lombard sab reliable hain.
