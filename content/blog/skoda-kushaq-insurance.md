## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Skoda Kushaq

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Skoda ke imported parts bahut mahenge hain — zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — TSI turbo engine repair costs high — waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover essential
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹14,000-₹21,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Skoda ke limited service network ke karan yeh add-on aur bhi zaroori hai
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹11-19 Lakh ki investment ke liye must

## 📋 Skoda Kushaq Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Skoda authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Skoda Kushaq Insurance

**Q: Skoda Kushaq ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Skoda Kushaq (above 1500cc, 1.5L variant) ka third-party premium ₹7,000/year hai 2026 mein. 1.0L variant ke liye ₹6,500/year lagega.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Bilkul haan! Skoda ke imported parts bahut mahenge hain — zero dep ke bina claim mein ₹15,000-₹40,000 tak depreciation deduct ho sakti hai. Must-have add-on hai.

**Q: Skoda Kushaq ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Kushaq ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹5,600-₹21,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Skoda parts expensive hain — kya insurance cover karega?**
A: Comprehensive insurance original Skoda parts cover karta hai. Lekin ensure karein ki cashless claim Skoda authorized center pe ho — local garage mein duplicate parts use ho sakte hain.
