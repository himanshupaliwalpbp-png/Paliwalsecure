## Quick Answer

Standard home insurance covers your residence structure and contents. If you work from home, you may need additional coverage for: (1) Office equipment (laptop, monitor, printer — often excluded from home contents), (2) Professional liability (client data breach, work errors), (3) Cyber insurance (phishing, ransomware). Standard home insurance premium: ₹2,000-5,000/year. WFH add-ons: ₹1,000-3,000/year extra.

## Tax Benefits

Home insurance premium does NOT qualify for 80D or 80C deduction. However:
- **If you are self-employed/freelancer**: home insurance can be claimed as a business expense under Section 37(1)
- **Home loan principal**: qualifies for 80C (up to ₹1.5L, old regime only)
- **Home loan interest**: qualifies for Section 24(b) (up to ₹2L, old regime only; ₹3.5L for affordable housing)

## FAQ

### Q: Does my employer's insurance cover my work laptop at home?
**A:** Often no. Most corporate asset insurance covers equipment "at office premises." When the laptop is at your home, it may not be covered. Check with your HR/IT team. If not covered, buy home contents insurance with "portable electronics" or "office equipment" add-on.

### Q: Do I need cyber insurance if I work from home?
**A:** If you handle client data, financial information, or sensitive company data — yes. Cyber insurance covers: phishing losses, ransomware, data breach notification costs, and legal liability. Premium: ₹500-2,000/year for individuals. One phishing incident can cost ₹50,000+ — cyber insurance is worth it.

### Q: Can I claim tax deduction on WFH insurance?
**A:** Home insurance premium is NOT deductible under 80D or 80C. However, if you are a freelancer/consultant, you can claim it as a business expense under Section 37(1) (deducted from business income). Salaried employees cannot claim home insurance as a tax deduction.

### Q: What is covered under home contents insurance?
**A:** Household items: furniture, appliances, jewelry (up to limit), electronics (if specified). NOT covered: cash, documents, items in open areas. For WFH: specify laptop, monitor, printer as "office equipment" — otherwise they may be excluded as "portable electronics."

### Q: Should I buy home insurance if I rent?
**A:** Yes — for contents (your belongings). Structure insurance is the landlord's responsibility. Contents insurance covers your laptop, TV, furniture, jewelry against theft, fire, and natural disasters. Premium: ₹1,500-3,000/year for ₹5L contents cover. Worth it for anyone with ₹2L+ in household items.
