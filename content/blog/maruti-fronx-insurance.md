## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Maruti Fronx

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Claim poora milega, depreciation cut nahi hoga. Fronx ke turbo engine variant ke parts mahenge hain, toh zero dep must hai
2. **Engine Protection** — Flooding/waterlogging damage cover, especially monsoon-prone cities ke liye
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega, jo aapko har saal 20-50% discount deta hai
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help, towing, flat tire, battery jumpstart
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega, naye Fronx ke liye bahut useful

## 📋 Maruti Fronx Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Network garage/Maruti service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Maruti Fronx Insurance

**Q: Maruti Fronx ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Maruti Fronx (1000-1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹6,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Zero dep cover se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Fronx ke liye yeh bahut useful hai, especially agar car new hai (5 saal se purani nahi). Turbo variant ke expensive parts ke liye toh must hai.

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Fronx ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹4,400-₹17,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.
