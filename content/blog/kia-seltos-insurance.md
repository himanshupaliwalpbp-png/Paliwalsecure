## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Kia Seltos

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Seltos ke premium parts (LED headlamps, ADAS sensors, 10.25-inch screen area) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — Turbo engine repair costs high hain — waterlogging, hydrostatic lock, aur oil leakage damage cover
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹14,000-₹22,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help, towing, flat tire — highway drives ke liye essential
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹11-20 Lakh ki investment ke liye smart choice

## 📋 Kia Seltos Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Kia authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Kia Seltos Insurance

**Q: Kia Seltos ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Kia Seltos (above 1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹7,000/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Zero dep cover se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Seltos premium SUV hai jiske parts mahenge hain — 5 saal se purani nahi hai toh must hai.

**Q: Kia Seltos ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Seltos ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹5,600-₹22,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.
