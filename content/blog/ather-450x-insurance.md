### Pro Pack Warranty Mein Kya NAHI Cover Hota

- ❌ Accidental damage (scooter girna, collision)
- ❌ Theft of scooter or battery
- ❌ Fire damage (short-circuit, charging fire)
- ❌ Natural calamity (flood, waterlogging)
- ❌ Third-party liability (kisi ko injury/damage)
- ❌ Battery damage due to negligence
- ❌ Unauthorized repair/modification
- ❌ Racing or competitive use damage

> **Important**: Pro Pack warranty insurance ki jagah nahi le sakta. Warranty manufacturing defects ke liye hai, insurance accidents aur unforeseen events ke liye. Dono zaroori hain!

## Ather Insurance Claim Process

### Step 1: Report the Incident
- Ather App se roadside assistance book karein
- Insurer ko 24-48 hours mein inform karein
- Damage photos click karein (minimum 4 angles)

### Step 2: Document Collection
- RC copy, driving license, policy document
- FIR (theft/vandalism/third-party injury ke case mein)
- Ather service booking confirmation

### Step 3: Survey & Assessment
- Surveyor Ather service center par inspect karega
- Battery diagnostic report mandatory hai EV claims mein
- Ather's connected features data (ride history) claim proof mein helpful

### Step 4: Repair & Settlement
- Cashless: Direct Ather service center mein repair, insurer pays
- Reimbursement: Aap pay karein, bills submit karein, insurer reimburse karega
- Settlement time: 7-15 working days typically

### Ather-Specific Claim Tips

- Ather's over-the-air data recovery can help prove accident circumstances
- Connected scooter data (telemetry) can be submitted as claim evidence
- Always use Ather authorized service center for repairs
- Dashboard/screen damage common hai — zero dep ke bina 50% depreciation on screen
- Waterlogging damage ke liye immediate action zaroori — battery isolation critical

## FAQs

### 1. Ather 450X ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Ather 450X ka third-party premium ₹1,414 (IRDAI fixed) hai. Comprehensive insurance ₹3,800-₹7,200 per year hota hai variant aur add-ons ke hisaab se. 3.7 kWh variant ka premium thoda zyada hoga kyunki IDV zyada hai. Zero dep add-on ke saath roughly ₹1,000-₹1,500 extra lagta hai.

### 2. Kya Ather Pro Pack warranty ke baad insurance lena zaroori hai?

Pro Pack warranty ke dauraan bhi comprehensive insurance lena zaroori hai kyunki warranty accidental damage, theft, fire, aur third-party liability cover nahi karti. Warranty expire hone ke baad toh insurance aur bhi critical ho jata hai — especially battery degradation cover ke liye.

### 3. Ather ki battery kitne mein replace hoti hai?

Ather 450X ki battery replacement cost ₹40,000-₹60,000 (variant ke hisaab se) hoti hai. Pro Pack warranty ke dauraan (5 years) agar battery capacity 70% se neeche gire toh free replacement hota hai. Warranty ke baad battery cover add-on ye expense cover karta hai.

### 4. Kya Ather insurance online renew kar sakte hain?

Haan, Ather insurance bilkul online renew ho sakta hai. Insurer website ya aggregator par registration number daalein, existing policy details verify karein, IDV check karein, add-ons select karein, aur payment karein. Digital policy instant mil jati hai. Renewal 30 days pehle karein for best rates.

### 5. Ather scooter mein zero dep add-on lena chahiye?

Haan, strongly recommended! Ather 450X mein dashboard display, fiber body panels, aur LED lights — sab depreciation ke subject hain. Zero dep ke bina plastic/rubber parts par 50%, fiber par 30% depreciation deduct hoti hai. ₹1,000-₹1,500 ka investment claim mein ₹10,000-₹20,000 bacha sakta hai.

### 6. Ather service center par cashless claim kaise kaam karta hai?

Cashless claim mein aapko Ather service center par kuch pay nahi karna padta (deductible ke alawa). Insurer directly service center ko pay karta hai. Process: insurer ko inform → surveyor approval → repair → insurer settles bill. Most major insurers Ather service centers ke saath cashless tie-up rakhte hain.
