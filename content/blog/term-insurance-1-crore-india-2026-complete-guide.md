## Why ₹1 Crore Term Insurance?

### Inflation Reality
₹1 crore today = ₹25 lakh in 15 years (at 6% inflation). If your family needs ₹50,000/month today, they'll need ₹1,20,000/month in 15 years. ₹1 crore cover ensures your family can:
- Pay off home loan (₹40-60L average)
- Maintain current lifestyle (₹50,000/month = ₹60L for 10 years)
- Fund children's education (₹20-40L)
- Have emergency corpus (₹10-20L)

### IRDAI Recommendation
IRDAI recommends life cover = 10-15x annual income. If you earn ₹8 lakh/year, you need ₹80L-₹1.2Cr cover. ₹1 crore is the sweet spot for most middle-class Indians.

## How to Buy ₹1 Crore Term Insurance

### Step 1: Calculate Cover Needed
Cover = 15x annual income + outstanding loans - existing life cover
Example: ₹8L income, ₹40L home loan, ₹10L existing cover = ₹1.5Cr needed → Buy ₹1Cr (sufficient for most)

### Step 2: Choose Policy Term
- Age 25-30: Choose 40-year term (covers till age 65-70)
- Age 30-40: Choose 30-year term
- Age 40+: Choose whole life (till age 70-80)
