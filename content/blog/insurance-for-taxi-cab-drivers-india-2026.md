## Quick Answer

Taxi cab drivers need: (1) Commercial vehicle insurance (mandatory — covers passengers + third party), (2) Personal accident cover (₹15L mandatory for owner-driver), (3) Health insurance (often missed — cab drivers have high health risks), (4) Life insurance (income replacement for family). Commercial vehicle premium: ₹8,000-15,000/year (higher than private vehicle).

**TL;DR Summary:**

| Parameter | Mandatory (Vehicle + PA) | Recommended (Health + Life) |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial vehicle insurance | Mandatory (Motor Vehicles Act) | ₹8,000-15,000/year |
| Passenger cover | Included (commercial policy) | Up to ₹2L per passenger |
| Personal accident (owner-driver) | Mandatory (₹15L) | ₹300-500/year |
| Health insurance | Recommended (high-risk profession) | ₹5,000-10,000/year |
| Term life insurance | Recommended (income replacement) | ₹3,000-8,000/year |
| Tax benefit (80D) | On health insurance premium | Up to ₹25,000 deduction |
| Tax benefit (80C) | On life insurance premium | Up to ₹1.5L deduction |

## FAQ

### Q: Is commercial vehicle insurance different from private car insurance?
**A:** Yes. Commercial vehicle insurance costs 50-100% more (higher risk, passenger liability). It includes passenger cover (₹2L per passenger), which private insurance does not. You cannot use private insurance for a commercial vehicle — it is illegal and claims will be rejected.

### Q: Can cab drivers claim 80D on health insurance?
**A:** Yes. Under old tax regime, cab drivers can claim 80D deduction on health insurance premium (₹25,000 under 60, ₹50,000 senior). Under new regime, 80D is not available. Many cab drivers miss this — if your income is taxable, always file ITR and claim 80D.

### Q: What personal accident cover do cab drivers need?
**A:** IRDAI mandates ₹15L personal accident cover for owner-drivers (included in commercial policy). For non-owner drivers (company employees), the employer must provide PA cover. Additional PA cover (₹25L-1Cr) is recommended — driving is a high-risk profession.

### Q: Can Ola/Uber drivers buy group insurance?
**A:** Some aggregator platforms offer group insurance to drivers. However, group insurance ends if you stop driving for that platform. Always buy individual health + term insurance that stays with you regardless of platform. Group insurance is a supplement, not a substitute.

### Q: What is the cheapest insurance combination for cab drivers?
**A:** Commercial vehicle insurance (mandatory): ₹12,000. Health insurance (₹3L cover): ₹4,000. Term insurance (₹25L cover): ₹3,000. Total: ₹19,000/year. Tax saved (old regime, 10% slab): ₹2,100. Net cost: ₹16,900/year. This provides ₹28L+ total protection for less than ₹1,500/month.
