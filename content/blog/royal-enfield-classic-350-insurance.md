## Classic 350 Claim Process

### Cashless Claim at RE Service Center

1. **Accident/Breakdown**: Insurer helpline call karein + RE roadside assistance
2. **RE Service Center**: Nearest authorized workshop par le jaayein
3. **Photos**: Damage photos click karein (RE WhatsApp group tip: always document)
4. **Documents**: RC, DL, policy copy
5. **FIR**: Theft/vandalism/third-party injury ke case mein
6. **Survey**: Surveyor inspection at service center
7. **Engine Assessment** (if engine issue): RE mechanic + surveyor joint assessment
8. **Repair**: Approval ke baad genuine RE parts se repair
9. **Cashless Settlement**: Insurer directly pays (you pay deductible only)

### RE-Specific Claim Tips

- **Always use genuine RE parts**: Aftermarket parts insurance mein covered nahi honge properly
- **Engine damage claim**: Engine protect add-on ke bina reject hoga — standard comprehensive mein engine internal damage nahi cover hota
- **Long ride breakdown**: Roadside assistance add-on se towing free hogi (otherwise ₹2,000-₹5,000)
- **Chrome parts**: Clean photos chahiye honge — pahle chrome polish karein phir photos lein (damage clearly dikhe)
- **Accessory modifications**: Saddle bag, crash guard, custom exhaust — ye standard policy mein nahi aate. Separate accessories cover chahiye
- **RE community tip**: RE owners group mein insurer experience share karte hain — check karein before choosing

## FAQs

### 1. Royal Enfield Classic 350 ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Classic 350 ka third-party premium ₹1,434/year hai (IRDAI fixed for 150-350cc). Comprehensive insurance ₹3,500-₹8,000/year hota hai variant aur add-ons ke hisaab se. Base Redditch model ke liye ₹3,500-₹5,000, Chrome/Custom ke liye ₹4,200-₹8,000. Engine protect + zero dep ke saath total ₹5,200-₹8,500.

### 2. Classic 350 ke liye engine protect add-on kyun zaroori hai?

Classic 350 ki air-cooled engine unique risks rakhti hai — overheating (long rides par), oil leakage (very common), aur monsoon mein water damage. Standard comprehensive policy engine internal damage cover nahi karti. Engine repair ₹8,000-₹30,000 tak ho sakti hai. ₹600-₹1,500/year ka add-on ye massive risk cover karta hai. MUST HAVE for RE owners.

### 3. Classic 350 ke chrome parts insurance mein cover hote hain?

Haan, chrome parts (fender, side panel, exhaust covers) comprehensive insurance mein covered hain — lekin depreciation deduct hoti hai. Metal parts par 30% depreciation, rubber 50%, plastic 50%. Zero depreciation add-on (₹800-₹1,500/year) se full claim mileagega bina depreciation ke. Chrome parts expensive hain — isliye zero dep recommended hai.

### 4. Long ride par breakdown ho toh insurance help karega?

Roadside assistance add-on (₹300-₹600/year) se towing, on-site minor repair, fuel delivery, aur accommodation arrangement (agar stranded ho) milta hai. Bina is add-on ke towing ₹2,000-₹5,000 tak lagta hai. RE owners jo frequently long rides par jaate hain unke liiple ye add-on must hai.

### 5. Classic 350 ke aftermarket accessories insurance mein cover honge?

Nahi, standard policy mein factory-fitted parts hi cover hote hain. Aftermarket accessories jaise crash guard (₹2,000-₹5,000), saddle stay (₹1,500-₹3,000), custom exhaust (₹5,000-₹15,000), LED indicators (₹1,000-₹3,000) — inke liiple **Accessories Cover** add-on lena padega. Cost: accessories value ka 3-5% per year.

### 6. Kaunsa insurer Classic 350 ke liye best hai?

**Tata AIG** sabse high claim ratio (98.2%) ke saath reliable hai. **Bajaj Allianz** ka RE service center network sabse vast hai (1,000+ tie-ups). **Digit** sabse kam premium deta hai. **Acko** fastest settlement ke liye best hai. Agar cashless RE workshop priority hai toh Bajaj Allianz ya ICICI Lombard choose karein. Budget conscious hain toh New India Assurance try karein.
