## How to Choose the Best Plan for HIV/AIDS

### 1. Check PED Waiting Period
Sabse kam waiting period wala plan chuno. 24-48 months common hai, lekin kuch plans me 24 months bhi milta hai.

### 2. Premium Loading Comparison
Different insurers alag loading charge karte hain. 10-30% average hai. 3-4 quotes compare karo.

### 3. Sum Insured Adequacy
Minimum ₹10 Lakh sum insured chuno — HIV/AIDS treatment expensive ho sakta hai. ₹25-50 Lakh super top-up add karo for major expenses.

### 4. Network Hospital Coverage
Aapke city me insurer ke network hospitals check karo. Cashless claim ke liye network hospital zaroori hai.

### 5. Co-payment Clause
Kuch plans me 10-20% co-payment hota hai — matlab har claim me 10-20% aapko dena padega. Co-payment waived plans chuno.

### 6. Restoration Benefit
Sum insured exhausted hone pe automatic restore wale plans best hain — HIV/AIDS patients ke liye useful.

### 7. No Room Rent Capping
Single private room allow karne wale plans chuno — capping wale plans me dispute ho sakta hai.

## Documents Required for Buying Insurance with HIV/AIDS

1. **Identity proof** (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
2. **Address proof** (Aadhaar, utility bill)
3. **Age proof** (Birth certificate, 10th marksheet)
4. **Medical reports** (last 6 months):
   - Doctor's prescription mentioning HIV/AIDS
   - Latest blood test reports
   - Treatment history
   - Hospital discharge summaries (if any)
5. **Photographs** (passport size)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Kya HIV/AIDS patient ko health insurance milega?
**A:** Haan, milega. IRDAI ke rules ke under insurers HIV/AIDS patient ko cover karte hain, lekin PED waiting period (24-48 months) aur premium loading (10-30%) ke saath.

### Q: HIV/AIDS chhupa ke insurance le sakta hu?
**A:** Bilkul nahi. Hiding pre-existing disease = claim rejection + policy cancellation. IRDAI ke under 3-5 saal tak fraud detected hua to claim reject ho sakta hai. Hamesha honestly declare karo.

### Q: HIV/AIDS-related claim PED waiting me hoga to?
**A:** Nahin, PED waiting period ke during HIV/AIDS-related claims reject honge. Lekin non-HIV/AIDS related hospitalization (accident, etc.) covered hota hai from Day 1.

### Q: Premium kitna zyada lagega?
**A:** Average 10-30% extra than standard premium. Exact amount insurer + aapki condition pe depend karta hai. 3-4 insurers se quotes lo.

### Q: Kya me existing policy port kar sakta hu?
**A:** Haan. IRDAI portability rules ke under aap existing policy ko better plan me port kar sakte ho — accumulated waiting period credits bhi transfer hote hain.

### Q: HIV/AIDS ke baad policy le sakta hu?
**A:** Haan, lekin policy lena jitna jaldi ho utna achha — younger age + shorter waiting period. 24-48 months waiting period jaldi complete hogi.
