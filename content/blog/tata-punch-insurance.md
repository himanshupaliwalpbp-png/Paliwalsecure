## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Tata Punch

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Punch ke body parts aur headlamp replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai bina zero dep ke — must-have add-on
2. **Engine Protection** — Monsoon season mein waterlogging damage cover karta hai, especially low-lying areas mein useful
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 20-50% discount preserved rahega renewal pe
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help, towing, flat tire, battery jump — Punch ke highway trips ke liye zaroori
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega, naye Punch ke liye best value

## 📋 Tata Punch Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Tata Motors authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Tata Punch Insurance

**Q: Tata Punch ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Tata Punch (below 1000cc, 1199cc) ka third-party premium ₹5,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Zero dep cover se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Punch ke liye yeh bahut useful hai, especially agar car new hai (5 saal se purani nahi). Ek claim mein ₹5,000-₹15,000 tak bacha sakta hai.

**Q: Tata Punch ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Punch ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹3,600-₹14,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Punch CNG ka insurance premium alag hota hai?**
A: Factory-fitted CNG variant ka IDV CNG kit ke saath calculate hota hai, toh premium thoda zyada hoti hai. Aftermarket CNG ke liye separate endorsement zaroori hai — ₹500-₹1,500 extra.
