## Quick Answer

Differently-abled persons in India can buy health and life insurance. IRDAI guidelines prohibit discrimination based on disability alone — insurers must evaluate on medical grounds. Health insurance: available with possible loading on premium. Life insurance: available, may need medical underwriting. Niramaya Health Insurance (govt scheme) offers ₹10L cover for persons with disabilities.

## Key Takeaways

- Buy insurance BEFORE you need it — not after
- Declare all information honestly to avoid claim rejection
- Compare 3+ insurers before buying — premium varies 10-20%
- Verify CSR from IRDAI Annual Report (target: 95%+)
- Read policy wording — especially exclusions section

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Can a differently-abled person buy health insurance in India?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI guidelines prohibit insurers from rejecting solely on disability. Insurers must evaluate on medical grounds. Premium may have a loading (extra charge) based on health risk assessment. Always declare disability honestly — non-disclosure leads to claim rejection.

### Q: What is Niramaya Health Insurance Scheme?
**A:** Government scheme for persons with autism, cerebral palsy, mental retardation, and multiple disabilities. Provides ₹10L health cover. Premium: ₹500/year (free for BPL families). Covers hospitalization, OPD, and preventive health checkups. Administered under National Trust Act 1999.

### Q: Can a person with disability buy term life insurance?
**A:** Yes. Insurers evaluate based on the type and severity of disability, not disability alone. Mild disabilities (hearing impairment, partial vision) may get standard rates. Severe disabilities may have premium loading or exclusions. Medical underwriting is required. Apply to multiple insurers — acceptance criteria vary.

### Q: Are there tax benefits for differently-abled persons insurance?
**A:** Yes. Section 80U: ₹75,000 deduction (40-80% disability) or ₹1,25,000 (80%+). Section 80DD: deduction for maintenance of disabled dependent (same amounts). Section 80D: health insurance premium deduction. These are under old tax regime only.

### Q: Can a person with mental illness buy insurance?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI 2018 mandate requires all insurers to cover mental illness. Mental Healthcare Act 2017 mandates mental health coverage equal to physical health. Waiting periods may apply (2-4 years for pre-existing mental conditions). Declare all conditions honestly.
