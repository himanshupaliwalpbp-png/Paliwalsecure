## FAQ

### Q: Is public liability insurance mandatory for gyms?
**A:** Not legally mandatory nationwide, but some municipal corporations require it for trade license. Essential — one member injury claim can cost ₹5-15L.

### Q: What does gym equipment insurance cover?
**A:** Damage to treadmills, weights, AC, sound systems from fire, surge, theft, vandalism. NOT wear and tear. Premium: 1-2% of equipment value.

### Q: Can a gym member sue if they get injured during a workout?
**A:** Yes. Consumer Protection Act 2019 applies. Gym owner liable for faulty equipment, inadequate supervision, unsafe premises.

### Q: Do personal trainers need separate insurance?
**A:** Yes. Trainer PI covers negligent instruction claims. If employees, gym PI covers them. If contractors, they need own PI (₹5,000-8,000/year).

### Q: Can gym owners claim insurance premium as business expense?
**A:** Yes. All business insurance premiums deductible under Section 37(1). Reduces taxable gym revenue.
