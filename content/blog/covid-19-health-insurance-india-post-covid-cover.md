## How to Choose the Best Plan for COVID-19 & Post-COVID

### 1. Check PED Waiting Period
Sabse kam waiting period wala plan chuno. 15-30 days (standard policies) common hai, lekin kuch plans me 24 months bhi milta hai.

### 2. Premium Loading Comparison
Different insurers alag loading charge karte hain. Built-in (no separate loading) average hai. 3-4 quotes compare karo.

### 3. Sum Insured Adequacy
Minimum ₹10 Lakh sum insured chuno — COVID-19 & Post-COVID treatment expensive ho sakta hai. ₹25-50 Lakh super top-up add karo for major expenses.

### 4. Network Hospital Coverage
Aapke city me insurer ke network hospitals check karo. Cashless claim ke liye network hospital zaroori hai.

### 5. Co-payment Clause
Kuch plans me 10-20% co-payment hota hai — matlab har claim me 10-20% aapko dena padega. Co-payment waived plans chuno.

### 6. Restoration Benefit
Sum insured exhausted hone pe automatic restore wale plans best hain — COVID-19 & Post-COVID patients ke liye useful.

### 7. No Room Rent Capping
Single private room allow karne wale plans chuno — capping wale plans me dispute ho sakta hai.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Kya COVID-19 & Post-COVID patient ko health insurance milega?
**A:** Haan, milega. IRDAI ke rules ke under insurers COVID-19 & Post-COVID patient ko cover karte hain, lekin PED waiting period (15-30 days (standard policies)) aur premium loading (Built-in (no separate loading)) ke saath.

### Q: COVID-19 & Post-COVID chhupa ke insurance le sakta hu?
**A:** Bilkul nahi. Hiding pre-existing disease = claim rejection + policy cancellation. IRDAI ke under 3-5 saal tak fraud detected hua to claim reject ho sakta hai. Hamesha honestly declare karo.

### Q: COVID-19 & Post-COVID-related claim PED waiting me hoga to?
**A:** Nahin, PED waiting period ke during COVID-19 & Post-COVID-related claims reject honge. Lekin non-COVID-19 & Post-COVID related hospitalization (accident, etc.) covered hota hai from Day 1.

### Q: Premium kitna zyada lagega?
**A:** Average Built-in (no separate loading) extra than standard premium. Exact amount insurer + aapki condition pe depend karta hai. 3-4 insurers se quotes lo.

### Q: Kya me existing policy port kar sakta hu?
**A:** Haan. IRDAI portability rules ke under aap existing policy ko better plan me port kar sakte ho — accumulated waiting period credits bhi transfer hote hain.

### Q: COVID-19 & Post-COVID ke baad policy le sakta hu?
**A:** Haan, lekin policy lena jitna jaldi ho utna achha — younger age + shorter waiting period. 15-30 days (standard policies) waiting period jaldi complete hogi.
