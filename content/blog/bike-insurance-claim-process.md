## Key Takeaway

Bike insurance claim process mein accident ke baad insurance company ko 24-48 hours mein inform karein, FIR file karein (third-party/theft case mein), network garage le jaayein cashless repair ke liye, surveyor inspection karwayein, aur documents submit karein. Accident claim 7-15 days mein settle hota hai, theft claim 30-90 days mein. Common mistakes — late reporting, unauthorized repair, aur incomplete documents — se bachein.

## Bike Insurance Claim Types

Bike insurance mein mainly 3 tarah ke claims hote hain:

| Claim Type | Coverage | FIR Required? |
|-----------|----------|:------------:|
| **Own Damage Claim** | Aapki bike ka nuksan (comprehensive policy) | ❌ Usually no |
| **Third-Party Claim** | Dusre person/vehicle ka nuksan | ✅ Yes |
| **Theft Claim** | Bike chori ho gayi | ✅ Yes |

> 💡 **Note:** Third-party insurance se sirf dusre ka nuksan cover hota hai — apni bike ka nuksan nahi. Apni bike ke liye **comprehensive policy** zaroori hai.

## Bike Accident Claim Process — Step by Step

### Step 1: Accident Scene Pe Kya Karein

Agar aapka bike accident ho gaya hai:

1. **Safety pehle** — Road side pe aayein, traffic block na ho
2. **Injuries check karein** — Agar injury hai toh medical help bulayein
3. **Photos click karein** — Bike damage, accident scene, number plate, third-party vehicle
4. **Third-party details lein** — Us vehicle ka number, driver ka naam, phone number
5. **Witnesses contact karein** — Eyewitness ka number note karein
6. **Police ko inform karein** — Agar third-party involved hai ya injury hai

> ⚠️ **Important:** Accident scene se bike mat hatayein jab tak photos nahi le liye — evidence important hai claim ke liye.

### Step 2: FIR File Karein (Jab Zaroori Hai)

| Situation | FIR Required? |
|-----------|:------------:|
| Self-damage only (apni bike ka nuksan) | ❌ No |
| Third-party damage/injury | ✅ Yes |
| Hit and run case | ✅ Yes |
| Any injury to self or others | ✅ Yes |
| Only property damage (wall, gate, etc.) | Depends on extent |

FIR ke liye nearest police station jaayein. Kuch states mein online FIR bhi ho sakti hai.

### Step 3: Insurance Company Ko Inform Karein

**24-48 hours ke andar** company ko inform karna zaroori hai:

- **Toll-free helpline** pe call karein
- **Mobile app** pe claim register karein (fastest)
- **Email** pe written intimation bhejein
- **WhatsApp** — Kuch companies support karti hain

**Information provide karein:**
- Policy number
- Bike registration number
- Date, time aur location of accident
- Damage description
- FIR number (agar filed ki hai)

### Step 4: Cashless Garage Mein Le Jaayein

Comprehensive policy mein cashless repair available hai:

1. Insurance company ki website/app pe **network garage list** check karein
2. Bike nearest network garage le jaayein
3. Garage wale claim form fill karenge
4. Surveyor aayega damage inspect karne
5. Approval ke baad repair start hoga
6. Aapko sirf **deductible + depreciation** pay karna hai

**Bike Cashless Garage Deductibles:**

| Bike Engine CC | Compulsory Deductible |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Up to 150cc | ₹500 |
| 150cc - 350cc | ₹750 |
| Above 350cc | ₹1,000 |

### Step 5: Surveyor Inspection

Surveyor bike ka damage assess karega:

- **Unauthorized repair mat karwayein** — Pehle surveyor approval leina
- Agar aapko garage change karna hai toh company ko inform karein
- Surveyor ka estimate aayega — aap agree ya disagree kar sakte hain
- Approval ke baad repair shuru ho ga

### Step 6: Documents Submit Karein

| Document | Cashless Claim | Reimbursement |
|----------|:-------------:|:-------------:|
| Claim form | ✅ | ✅ |
| Driving license copy | ✅ | ✅ |
| RC copy | ✅ | ✅ |
| Insurance policy copy | ✅ | ✅ |
| FIR copy (if applicable) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Original repair bills | ❌ | ✅ |
| Payment proof | ❌ | ✅ |
| Surveyor report | ✅ | ✅ |
| Damage photographs | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cancelled cheque | ❌ | ✅ |

## Bike Theft Claim Process

Bike chori ho jaane ke case mein process alag hai:

### Step-by-Step Theft Claim

1. **FIR file karein** — Turant nearest police station mein (mandatory)
2. **Insurance company ko inform karein** — FIR ke saath 24-48 hours mein
3. **Non-traceable certificate** — Police se 30-60 days ke baad milta hai (jab bike nahi milti)
4. **Documents submit karein:**
   - Original FIR copy
   - Non-traceable certificate (from police)
   - Original RC (ya copy with police endorsement)
   - Both bike keys
   - Policy document
   - Claim form (filled)
   - NOC from financer (agar bike loan pe hai)
5. **IDV settlement** — Bike ka Insured Declared Value aapko milega

> ⚠️ **Theft claim mein keys submit karna zaroori hai.** Dono keys nahi mil rahe toh company investigation karegi.

### Theft Claim Timeline

| Stage | Time |
|-------|------|
| FIR filing | Day 0 |
| Company intimation | 1-2 days |
| Non-traceable certificate | 30-60 days |
| Document submission | 1-2 days after NTC |
| Settlement | 15-30 days after submission |
| **Total** | **45-90 days** |

## Common Mistakes Jo Bike Claim Reject Karwa Dete Hain

| # | Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **Late intimation** (48+ hours) | Claim delay ya rejection |
| 2 | **No valid driving license** | 100% claim rejection |
| 3 | **Drink and drive** | Policy excludes this |
| 4 | **Unauthorized repair** | Bina surveyor ke repair karwaya |
| 5 | **Lapsed policy** | Not covered at time of accident |
| 6 | **Wrong information** | Claim form mein galat details |
| 7 | **FIR nahi karwayi** | Jab FIR zaroori thi tab nahi ki |
| 8 | **Keys nahi diye (theft)** | Both keys mandatory for theft claim |
| 9 | **Racing/speeding** | Excluded under dangerous activities |
| 10 | **Commercial use** | Personal policy pe commercial use = rejection |

## Bike Claim Ke Pro Tips

- ✅ **Helmet pehenna** — Accident mein helmet nahi tha toh claim affected ho sakta hai
- ✅ **Dashcam / phone mount** — Video evidence helpful hai
- ✅ **Emergency contacts save** — Insurance helpline, agent number
- ✅ **Digital copies** — Phone pe RC, license, policy ki photos
- ✅ **Zero-dep add-on** — Depreciation se bachein, especially new bike ke liye
- ✅ **Roadside assistance** — Towing, flat tire, fuel delivery — bahut useful
- ✅ **NCB protector** — Claim ke baad bhi No Claim Bonus bacha lein
- ✅ **Return to invoice** — Total loss mein invoice price milega, IDV nahi

## FAQs

### 1. Kya third-party insurance se apni bike ka claim ho sakta hai?

Nahi, third-party insurance se **sirf dusre ka nuksan** cover hota hai — apni bike ka repair nahi. Apni bike ke liye **comprehensive policy** lena zaroori hai.

### 2. Bike accident mein FIR kab zaroori hai?

FIR tab zaroori hai jab: (1) Third-party involved ho, (2) Kisi ki injury hui ho, (3) Hit-and-run case ho, (4) Bike chori ho gayi ho. Self-damage (sirf apni bike ka nuksan, no third-party) mein FIR ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

### 3. Bike theft claim mein kya documents chahiye?

Original FIR, non-traceable certificate (police se 30-60 days baad), RC copy, dono keys, policy document, claim form, aur NOC from financer (agar loan pe hai). Keys submit karna bahut important hai — bina keys ke claim difficult hai.

### 4. Kya pillion rider ko bhi insurance cover milega?

Haan, agar aapke paas **personal accident cover for pillion rider** add-on hai toh pillion rider ko bhi coverage milega. Default PA cover sirf owner-driver ke liye hota hai — pillion ke liye separate add-on lena padta hai.

### 5. Cashless bike repair mein mujhe kitna pay karna padega?

Aapko sirf **compulsory deductible** (₹500-₹1,000 CC ke hisaab se) aur **depreciation amount** pay karna hoga. Zero-dep add-on hai toh depreciation bhi company cover karegi — aapko sirf deductible dena hai.

### 6. Kya racing ya stunting mein bike damage ka claim hota hai?

Nahi, racing, stunting, speed trials, aur dangerous activities policy mein **explicitly exclude** hote hain. Aise cases mein claim reject ho jaata hai. Normal road use hi covered hai.
