## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Mahindra XUV700

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! XUV700 ke premium parts (ADAS sensors, LED headlamps, panoramic sunroof glass) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai bina zero dep ke
2. **Engine Protection** — Turbo petrol aur diesel engine repair costs high hain — waterlogging, hydrostatic lock, aur oil leakage damage cover
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB ka matlab ₹17,500-₹27,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — XUV700 ke highway drives ke liye essential
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹14-28 Lakh ki investment ke liye must

## 📋 Mahindra XUV700 Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Mahindra authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga
