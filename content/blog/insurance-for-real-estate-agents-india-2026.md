## Quick Answer

Real estate agents/brokers need: professional indemnity (₹5,000-15,000/year — misrepresentation, disclosure failures), public liability (₹5,000-10,000/year — property site injuries), health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year), term life (₹3,000-8,000/year). RERA recommends PI for all registered agents. One property dispute lawsuit can cost ₹5-25L.

## FAQ

### Q: Do real estate agents need professional indemnity?
**A:** Yes. PI covers claims of: misrepresentation of property details, failure to disclose defects, wrong documentation, breach of contract. Under RERA (Real Estate Regulation and Development Act 2016), agents are legally liable for misrepresentation. One lawsuit can cost ₹5-25L. Premium: ₹5,000-15,000/year.

### Q: Is RERA registration mandatory for agents?
**A:** Yes. RERA Act 2016 requires all real estate agents to register with their state RERA authority. Registration requires: identity proof, qualifications, and in some states, PI insurance. Operating without RERA registration is illegal (fine: ₹10,000/day + imprisonment up to 1 year).

### Q: What insurance do real estate brokers need for site visits?
**A:** Public liability insurance covers injuries to clients during property site visits (slip, fall, structural collapse). If a client is injured on a property you showed, you may be held liable. PL covers medical + legal + compensation. Premium: ₹5,000-10,000/year.

### Q: Can real estate agents claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes. PI, PL, and office insurance are deductible under Section 37(1). Health and life insurance for the agent qualify for 80D/80C (old regime). Commission income from real estate is taxable as business/professional income.

### Q: What is the minimum insurance for a new RERA agent?
**A:** PI (₹10L, ₹8,000/year) + Health (₹5L, ₹5,000/year) + Term (₹50L, ₹3,000/year) = ₹16,000/year. Add PL (₹5,000/year) if you conduct site visits. Add office insurance (₹3,000/year) if you have an office. Total: ₹19,000-24,000/year for full professional protection.
