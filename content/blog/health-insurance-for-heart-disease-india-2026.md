## Heart Disease and Health Insurance: What You Need to Know

### Is Heart Disease Covered?
Yes, Heart Disease is covered under health insurance in India. However, most insurers impose a **waiting period** of 2-4 years before heart disease-related claims are eligible.

### Types of Waiting Periods

| Waiting Period | What It Means | Typical Duration |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Initial Waiting | No claims except accidents | 30-90 days |
| PED Waiting | Pre-existing diseases (including Heart Disease) | 2-4 years |
| Specific Disease Waiting | Heart Disease-specific treatments | 1-2 years |

### IRDAI Guidelines on Heart Disease
IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) mandates:
- All health insurers must cover Heart Disease after waiting period
- Waiting period cannot exceed 4 years for any disease
- After 5 continuous years of coverage (moratorium), no claim can be rejected for non-disclosure (except fraud)

## How to Buy Heart Disease Health Insurance

### Step 1: Declare Your Heart Disease Status
Honest disclosure is critical. Declare:
- When heart disease was diagnosed
- Current medication/treatment
- Doctor's reports
- Latest test results

### Step 2: Choose Plan with Shortest Waiting Period
Star Health offers 1-year waiting. Care Health offers 2-year. Avoid plans with 4-year waiting.

### Step 3: Get Medical Test Done
Most insurers require medical test for heart disease patients. Tests may include:
- ECG, echo, stress test
- General health checkup

### Step 4: Pay Extra Premium (if applicable)
Heart Disease patients may pay 10-30% higher premium. This is standard loading.

### Step 5: Complete Waiting Period
During waiting period:
- Accidents covered
- Non-heart disease diseases covered
- Heart Disease-related claims NOT covered (unless add-on purchased)

## Heart Disease Coverage: What's Included

### Covered (after waiting period)
- Hospitalization due to heart disease
- heart disease related surgeries/procedures
- Pre and post hospitalization (60/90 days)
- Day care procedures
- Medicines during hospitalization
- Diagnostic tests

### NOT Covered
- Heart Disease during waiting period (unless add-on)
- OPD consultation (unless OPD add-on)
- Alternative medicine (except AYUSH)
- Cosmetic treatment for heart disease (if any)
