## Quick Answer

CAs need: professional indemnity (₹10,000-30,000/year — audit errors, tax filing mistakes), public liability (₹5,000-10,000/year), health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year), term life (₹3,000-8,000/year). ICAI recommends minimum ₹25L PI for all practicing CAs. One audit error lawsuit can cost ₹5-50L. ICAI also offers group PI at discounted rates.

## FAQ

### Q: Is professional indemnity mandatory for CAs in India?
**A:** ICAI recommends minimum ₹25L PI for all practicing CAs. Not legally mandatory, but: (1) ICAI disciplinary action can lead to personal liability, (2) clients increasingly require PI cover, (3) audit firms mandate PI for partners. One audit error or tax filing mistake can cost ₹5-50L.

### Q: Does ICAI offer group PI insurance?
**A:** Yes. ICAI arranges group PI insurance for members at discounted rates (10-20% cheaper than individual policies). Covers: audit negligence, tax advice errors, certification mistakes. Contact your local ICAI chapter for details. Group PI is cheaper but may have lower customization — compare with individual PI.

### Q: What does CA PI insurance cover?
**A:** Claims of: audit negligence, wrong tax advice, filing errors, certification mistakes, breach of confidentiality, conflict of interest. Covers legal defense + compensation + rectification costs. Does NOT cover: intentional fraud, criminal acts, or penalties imposed by ICAI/IT department.

### Q: How much PI should a CA buy?
**A:** ICAI minimum: ₹25L. For audit of listed companies: ₹1Cr+ (SEBI may mandate). For tax practice: ₹25-50L. For international audit: ₹1-5Cr. Premium: 0.3-0.5% of sum insured. Match cover to client size — a ₹100Cr company audit warrants higher PI than a ₹1Cr client.

### Q: Can CAs claim PI as business expense?
**A:** Yes. PI, PL, health, life all deductible: PI + PL under Section 37(1) (both regimes), health under 80D (old regime), life under 80C (old regime). ICAI group PI premium also qualifies. Reduces taxable CA practice income.
