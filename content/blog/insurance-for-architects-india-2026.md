## Quick Answer

Architects need: professional indemnity (₹10,000-30,000/year — design errors, structural failures), public liability (₹5,000-15,000/year — site visitor injuries), health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year), term life (₹3,000-8,000/year). One structural design error lawsuit can cost ₹10L-1Cr. Council of Architecture recommends PI for all practicing architects.

## FAQ

### Q: Is professional indemnity mandatory for architects in India?
**A:** Council of Architecture (COA) recommends PI for all practicing architects. Not legally mandatory, but increasingly required by clients (especially government and corporate projects). One design error causing structural failure can cost ₹10L-1Cr. PI covers legal defense + compensation.

### Q: What does architect PI insurance cover?
**A:** Claims of: design errors, specification mistakes, code violations, project delays due to design, structural failures attributable to design. Covers legal defense + compensation + rectification costs. Does NOT cover: intentional wrongdoing, criminal acts, or contract disputes (unless arising from professional negligence).

### Q: How much PI cover should an architect buy?
**A:** Minimum ₹25L for small projects. ₹50L-1Cr for commercial/institutional projects. Government projects may mandate ₹1Cr+ PI cover. Premium: 0.5-1% of sum insured per year. Higher cover = higher premium but better protection. Match cover to project value — a ₹50Cr project warrants ₹1Cr+ PI.

### Q: Can architects claim PI as business expense?
**A:** Yes. PI, PL, health, and life insurance all deductible: PI + PL under Section 37(1) (both regimes), health under 80D (old regime), life under 80C (old regime). Reduces taxable architecture income.

### Q: What is the minimum insurance for a freelance architect?
**A:** PI (₹25L, ₹12,000/year) + Health (₹5L, ₹5,000/year) + Term (₹50L, ₹3,000/year) = ₹20,000/year. Add PL (₹5,000/year) if clients visit your site. Add equipment insurance (₹2,000/year) for laptop/printer. Total: ₹22,000-27,000/year for full professional protection.
