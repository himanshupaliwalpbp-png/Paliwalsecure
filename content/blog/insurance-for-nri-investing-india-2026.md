## Quick Answer

NRIs can buy Indian insurance: health (for treatment in India), life/term (available with Indian + foreign address), and property insurance (for Indian real estate). Premiums can be paid from NRE/NRO account. 80D/80C tax benefits apply if NRI files Indian ITR. Term insurance for NRIs: 10-20% cheaper than international plans for same cover.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Can NRIs buy health insurance in India?
**A:** Yes. NRIs can buy Indian health insurance for treatment in India. Useful if NRI visits India frequently or plans to return. Premium can be paid from NRE/NRO account. 80D deduction available if NRI files Indian ITR. Cashless at Indian network hospitals.

### Q: Can NRIs buy term life insurance in India?
**A:** Yes. NRIs and OCIs can buy Indian term insurance. Need: Indian passport/OCI card, foreign address proof, income proof, and medical examination (can be done at insurer-designated center abroad). Premium: often 10-20% cheaper than international term plans for same cover. Sum insured paid in USD/INR.

### Q: Can NRIs claim 80D and 80C on Indian insurance?
**A:** Yes, if the NRI files Indian ITR (has Indian taxable income — rental, interest, capital gains). 80D on health insurance premium, 80C on life insurance premium. Deduction reduces taxable Indian income. If NRI has no Indian income (no ITR filed), no tax benefit.

### Q: Should NRIs buy Indian or international health insurance?
**A:** If NRI lives abroad and visits India 1-2 times/year: international plan (covers both countries). If NRI plans to return to India in 2-3 years: buy Indian policy now (locks in lower premium at younger age, waiting periods served before return). If NRI has parents in India: buy Indian policy for parents (80D benefit).

### Q: Can NRIs insure Indian property from abroad?
**A:** Yes. Property insurance for Indian real estate can be bought by NRI owner. Need: property documents, NRI address proof, and payment from NRE/NRO account. Covers fire, theft, natural disaster, tenant damage. Premium: ₹3,000-15,000/year depending on property value and location.
