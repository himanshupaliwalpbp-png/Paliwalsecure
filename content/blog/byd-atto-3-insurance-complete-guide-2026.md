## EV-Specific Insurance Considerations

### 1. Battery Cover (Critical)
BYD Atto 3 battery = 40-50% of vehicle cost. Standard comprehensive covers battery against:
- Accident damage ✅
- Fire ✅
- Theft ✅
- Natural calamity ✅
- **Wear and tear** ❌ (not covered)

**Tip:** Buy "Battery Depreciation Cover" add-on to cover battery wear.

### 2. Charging Cable Cover
BYD Atto 3 charging cable costs ₹68,000-₹113,333. Verify it's included in IDV. Buy "Accessories Cover" if not.

### 3. Charging Station Assistance
Some insurers offer:
- Free towing to nearest charging station
- Emergency charging assistance
- Roadside assistance for EVs

### 4. EV Discount
IRDAI mandates 15% discount on TP premium for all EVs. This is automatically applied.
