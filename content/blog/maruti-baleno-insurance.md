## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Maruti Baleno

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Baleno ke premium parts (LED headlamps, URSA grille, body panels) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — Waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover — monsoon season mein useful
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹9,000-₹14,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Nexa service network ke saath aur effective
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye Baleno ke liye smart choice

## 📋 Maruti Baleno Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Nexa service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga
