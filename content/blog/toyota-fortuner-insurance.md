## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Toyota Fortuner

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Fortuner ke premium parts (LED headlamps, chrome grille, 4WD components) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai — zero dep must hai
2. **Engine Protection** — 2.8L diesel engine repair ₹2-5 Lakh tak ja sakta hai — waterlogging, hydrostatic lock cover essential hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹32,500-₹47,500/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Fortuner off-road drives ke liye bhi useful
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹33-50 Lakh ki investment ke liye must

## 📋 Toyota Fortuner Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Toyota authorized service center (TSM) mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Toyota Fortuner Insurance

**Q: Toyota Fortuner ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Toyota Fortuner (above 1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹12,000/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Bilkul haan! Fortuner ₹33-50 Lakh ki premium SUV hai — uske parts bahut mahenge hain. Zero dep ke bina ek claim mein ₹30,000-₹80,000 tak depreciation deduct ho sakta hai.

**Q: Toyota Fortuner ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Fortuner ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹13,000-₹47,500 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Fortuner 4x4 ka insurance zyada hota hai kya?**
A: Haan, 4x4 variant ka IDV zyada hota hai kyunki ex-showroom price zyada hai. Plus 4WD system components bhi cover hone chahiye — ensure karein ki 4WD-related damage bhi policy mein included hai.
