## What is Universal Sompo?

Universal Sompo General Insurance is a joint venture between Sompo Japan, Allahabad Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, and Dabur. CSR: 95.8% (health). Network: 4,000+ hospitals.

## What is Magma HDI?

Magma HDI General Insurance is a joint venture between Magma Fincorp and HDI Global (Germany). CSR: 92.0% (health). Network: 4,500+ hospitals. Strong in rural/semi-urban areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which has a better CSR?
**A:** Universal Sompo (95.8%) has a significantly higher CSR than Magma HDI (92.0%). The 3.8% difference is notable — Universal Sompo approves a higher percentage of claims.

### Q: Which is cheaper?
**A:** Magma HDI is typically 3-5% cheaper. However, the premium difference is small. Get quotes from both before deciding. Consider the CSR difference when evaluating the premium saving.

### Q: Are both reliable?
**A:** Yes. Both are IRDAI-licensed. Universal Sompo is backed by Sompo Japan (major international insurer) + PSU banks. Magma HDI is backed by HDI Germany + Magma Fincorp.

### Q: Which has better rural presence?
**A:** Magma HDI has stronger rural/semi-urban presence through Magma Fincorp branches. Universal Sompo is stronger in urban areas through Allahabad Bank and IOB branches.
