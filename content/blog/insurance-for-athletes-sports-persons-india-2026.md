## FAQ

### Q: Do standard health policies cover sports injuries?
**A:** Many EXCLUDE professional sports. Need policy with sports injury cover. Declare your sport and level when buying.

### Q: What is loss of income insurance for athletes?
**A:** If injury prevents competition, pays monthly benefit during recovery. Critical — 6-12 months without income. Premium: ₹5,000-15,000/year.

### Q: Can athletes buy personal accident insurance?
**A:** Yes. Pays lump sum for death, disability, temporary total (broken leg, ACL). Ensure covers sports injuries. Premium: ₹2,000-8,000/year.

### Q: Do athletes need critical illness cover?
**A:** Yes. Higher risk: cardiac (endurance), joint deterioration (contact sports), kidney (extreme endurance). Pays lump sum on diagnosis.

### Q: Can professional athletes claim insurance as business expense?
**A:** Yes if sports is profession. Health: 80D. PA + loss of income + CI: Section 37(1). Equipment: 37(1). Both regimes for business.
