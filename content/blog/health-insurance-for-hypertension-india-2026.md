## Hypertension/High BP and Health Insurance: What You Need to Know

### Is Hypertension/High BP Covered?
Yes, Hypertension/High BP is covered under health insurance in India. However, most insurers impose a **waiting period** of 2-4 years before hypertension/high bp-related claims are eligible.

### Types of Waiting Periods

| Waiting Period | What It Means | Typical Duration |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Initial Waiting | No claims except accidents | 30-90 days |
| PED Waiting | Pre-existing diseases (including Hypertension/High BP) | 2-4 years |
| Specific Disease Waiting | Hypertension/High BP-specific treatments | 1-2 years |

### IRDAI Guidelines on Hypertension/High BP
IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) mandates:
- All health insurers must cover Hypertension/High BP after waiting period
- Waiting period cannot exceed 4 years for any disease
- After 5 continuous years of coverage (moratorium), no claim can be rejected for non-disclosure (except fraud)

## How to Buy Hypertension/High BP Health Insurance

### Step 1: Declare Your Hypertension/High BP Status
Honest disclosure is critical. Declare:
- When hypertension/high bp was diagnosed
- Current medication/treatment
- Doctor's reports
- Latest test results

### Step 2: Choose Plan with Shortest Waiting Period
Star Health offers 1-year waiting. Care Health offers 2-year. Avoid plans with 4-year waiting.

### Step 3: Get Medical Test Done
Most insurers require medical test for hypertension/high bp patients. Tests may include:
- BP monitoring, ECG
- General health checkup

### Step 4: Pay Extra Premium (if applicable)
Hypertension/High BP patients may pay 10-30% higher premium. This is standard loading.

### Step 5: Complete Waiting Period
During waiting period:
- Accidents covered
- Non-hypertension/high bp diseases covered
- Hypertension/High BP-related claims NOT covered (unless add-on purchased)
