## Corporate Health Insurance — The Illusion of Safety

Zyaadatar software engineers sochte hain: "Company ka cover hai, kya zaroorat hai personal insurance ki?" Yeh **sabse dangerous misconception** hai.

### What Your Corporate Cover Actually Provides

| Feature | Typical Corporate Cover | What You Actually Need |
|---------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Sum Insured | ₹3-5 lakh (some MNCs ₹7-10L) | ₹15-25 lakh minimum |
| Family Coverage | Spouse + 2 kids (basic) | Full family floater |
| Parents Coverage | Rarely included | Separate parents plan |
| Room Rent | Capped at ₹3,000-5,000/day | No cap |
| Pre-existing Disease | Day 1 cover | Good, but limited |
| Co-payment | 10-20% | No co-pay preferred |
| Restoration | Usually not available | Must have |
| Portability | Lost on job change | Lifetime guaranteed |

### 3 Critical Gaps in Corporate Cover

1. **Job Change = Cover Loss**: Notice period mein bhi cover hai, lekin new job ke probation (3-6 months) mein nahi. Beech mein agar kuch ho gaya toh?
2. **Layoff/Firing**: 2023-24 mein IT layoffs dekhe — 50,000+ employees. Unke corporate cover raat gaya, cover gayab
3. **Inadequate Coverage**: Dengue hospitalization = ₹2-3 lakh. Heart surgery = ₹5-15 lakh. Corporate cover khatam in one serious claim

## IT-Specific Health Insurance Features to Look For

### 1. OPD Coverage

Software engineers zyaadtar OPD visits karte hain — consultation, tests, medicines. OPD cover wala plan choose karein:

- ICICI Lombard Complete Health: OPD cover included
- Niva Bupa Health Companion: OPD expenses covered
- Care Health: Wellness + OPD benefits

### 2. Mental Health Coverage

IT mein anxiety, depression, burnout common hain. Check karein ki plan mental health cover karta hai:
- In-patient psychiatric treatment
- Counseling sessions
- Therapy coverage

### 3. Day Care Procedures

Eye surgery (LASIK complications), carpal tunnel surgery — yeh day care procedures hain jo 24-hour hospitalization nahi chahte. Ensure your plan covers 500+ day care procedures.

### 4. AYUSH Coverage

Yoga, Ayurveda, Homeopathy — IT professionals alternative treatments prefer karte hain. AYUSH coverage wala plan choose karein.

### 5. International Coverage

Onsite assignments ke liye — check karein ki plan international emergency cover karta hai ya nahi.

## FAQs

### 1. Kya corporate health insurance kaafi hai software engineers ke liye?

Nahi, corporate cover ₹3-5 lakh ka hota hai jo serious illness ke liye kaafi nahi hai. Job change ya layoff pe cover khatam ho jaata hai. Personal insurance zaroor lein — corporate cover supplementary hai, replacement nahi.

### 2. IT professionals ko kaunsa health insurance plan best hai?

HDFC ERGO Optima Secure ya Niva Bupa ReAssure best hain IT professionals ke liye — no co-pay, unlimited restoration, aur comprehensive coverage. OPD cover wala plan choose karein kyunki IT mein OPD visits zyada hoti hain.

### 3. Corporate aur personal insurance dono mein claim kaise karein?

Pehle corporate cover se claim karein — remainder personal insurance se. Yeh strategy se personal insurance ka NCB safe rahega. Kuch cases mein corporate insurer direct personal insurer se coordinate kar leta hai (contribution clause).

### 4. Software engineers ko term insurance bhi chahiye?

Haan, zaroor! Term insurance aapke nahi rahne pe family ko financial support deta hai. ₹1-2 crore ka term cover ₹600-1,200/month mein mil jaata hai. Home loan + family expenses ke liye yeh critical hai.

### 5. IT mein mental health insurance cover hota hai?

Zyaadatar comprehensive plans mental health cover karte hain — in-patient psychiatric treatment, counseling sessions. Check karein specifically kyunki basic plans mein yeh coverage nahi hoti. ICICI Lombard aur Niva Bupa achha mental health coverage dete hain.

### 6. Layoff ke baad kya hoga corporate insurance ka?

Layoff ke baad corporate cover typically 30-90 din tak active rehta hai (company policy pe depend karta hai). Uske baad cover khatam. Isliye personal insurance zaroor rakhein — layoff ke time pe yeh lifesaver hoga.
