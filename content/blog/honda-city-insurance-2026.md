## IDV Calculation

IDV = Ex-showroom price × (1 - depreciation %)

| Vehicle Age | Depreciation |
|-------------|-------------|
| New (0-6 mo) | 5% |
| 6 mo - 1 yr | 15% |
| 1-2 yrs | 20% |
| 2-3 yrs | 30% |
| 3-4 yrs | 40% |
| 4-5 yrs | 50% |
| 5+ yrs | Market value |
