## Quick Verdict

Annual premium payment is cheaper than monthly. Insurers charge 5-8% extra for monthly payments due to administrative costs and interest. If you can afford the annual amount, always choose annual. Monthly is suitable only if cash flow is tight.

| Key advantage | 5-8% higher than annual | Lowest total cost |

## What is Monthly Premium?

You pay the premium in 12 monthly installments via auto-debit (NACH/ECS) from your bank account.

## What is Annual Premium?

You pay the full year's premium in one payment. This is the default and most common mode.

## Feature Comparison Table

## Who Should Choose Monthly Premium?

You have a monthly salary and prefer smaller, regular payments. Monthly premium suits freelancers, gig workers, and those who cannot afford a lump sum annual payment.

## Who Should Choose Annual Premium?

You want to save 5-8% on total premium and can afford the annual amount. Annual payment also reduces the risk of policy lapse due to missed monthly payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How much extra does monthly premium cost?
**A:** Typically 5-8% more than annual. For example, if annual premium is ₹15,000, monthly premium would be ₹1,312/month (₹15,744/year) — a 5% surcharge. The exact surcharge varies by insurer.

### Q: What happens if I miss a monthly premium payment?
**A:** You get a 15-day grace period. If you don't pay within 15 days, the policy lapses. You can revive it within 2-5 years (depending on insurer) by paying all pending premiums + interest + revival fee.

### Q: Can I switch from monthly to annual?
**A:** Yes. Contact your insurer at renewal time and switch to annual. Some insurers allow mid-term changes — check your policy document.

### Q: Is monthly premium auto-debited from my bank account?
**A:** Yes. Monthly premium requires NACH (National Automated Clearing House) mandate. The insurer auto-debits the amount on a fixed date each month. Ensure sufficient balance to avoid bounce charges.

### Q: Do all insurers offer monthly premium?
**A:** Most health and life insurers offer monthly. Some motor insurers only offer annual (motor insurance is typically a 1-year policy). Check with your insurer or POSP.
