## Quick Answer

LGBTQ+ persons in India have equal access to insurance. IRDAI guidelines prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity. Health insurance: available to all. Life insurance: can nominate same-sex partner (Supreme Court ruling). Joint policies: some insurers offer same-sex couple floater plans. Always declare honestly — non-disclosure risks claim rejection.

## FAQ

### Q: Can LGBTQ+ persons buy health insurance in India?
**A:** Yes. IRDAI guidelines prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity. Health insurance is available at standard rates. Pre-existing conditions (including HIV/AIDS) are covered after waiting period (2-4 years). Declare all conditions honestly.

### Q: Can I nominate my same-sex partner in life insurance?
**A:** Yes. The Supreme Court (Navtej Singh Johar v. Union of India, 2018) decriminalized homosexuality. IRDAI allows nomination of any person with insurable interest — including same-sex partners. Some insurers may ask for proof of relationship (joint bank account, shared address). Nominee can be changed anytime.

### Q: Can same-sex couples buy joint health insurance (family floater)?
**A:** Some insurers now offer same-sex couple floater plans. Traditionally, family floater was defined as "spouse + children." Progressive insurers (Star Health, Care Health, HDFC ERGO) have expanded definitions. Ask your insurer or POSP about same-sex floater availability. If not available, buy two individual policies.

### Q: Is gender reassignment surgery covered by insurance?
**A:** Some insurers now cover gender reassignment surgery (GRS) as a day-care procedure. IRDAI has not mandated GRS coverage, but progressive insurers include it. Check policy wording for "sex reassignment surgery" or "gender affirmation surgery." Pre-authorization is required. If not covered, consider a specialized policy or rider.

### Q: Can transgender persons buy insurance?
**A:** Yes. Transgender persons have equal insurance rights under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019. Insurers cannot reject based on gender identity. Health and life insurance available at standard rates (unless medical underwriting indicates higher risk). Declare gender as per official ID (Aadhaar/passport).
