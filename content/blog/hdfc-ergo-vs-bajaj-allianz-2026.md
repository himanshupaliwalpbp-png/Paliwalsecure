## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which has better claim settlement — HDFC ERGO or Bajaj Allianz?
**A:** HDFC ERGO has a higher claim settlement ratio (98.85% vs 96.2% per IRDAI 2024-25). This means HDFC ERGO approves a higher percentage of claims. However, both are above the industry average of 90%.

### Q: Which is cheaper — HDFC ERGO or Bajaj Allianz?
**A:** Bajaj Allianz is typically 10-15% cheaper for similar coverage. For a ₹5L health cover for a 30-year-old, Bajaj Allianz charges ₹5,500-7,500/year vs HDFC ERGO's ₹6,500-8,500/year. However, premium varies by age, city, and health condition.

### Q: Do both offer cashless claims?
**A:** Yes. HDFC ERGO has 10,000+ network hospitals and Bajaj Allianz has 8,000+. Check if your preferred hospital is in the network before buying. Both offer cashless settlement across India.

### Q: Can I switch from Bajaj Allianz to HDFC ERGO?
**A:** Yes. At renewal time, you can port your policy to HDFC ERGO using IRDAI's portability guidelines. Your accumulated NCB and waiting period credits (for pre-existing conditions) are transferred. Apply for portability at least 45 days before renewal.

### Q: Which has better customer service?
**A:** HDFC ERGO is generally rated higher for customer service (4.2/5 vs 3.8/5 on consumer forums). HDFC ERGO offers 24/7 helpline, app-based claims, and faster response. Bajaj Allianz has improved recently but still lags slightly.
