## FAQ

### Q: Which has a higher CSR — Bajaj Allianz or TATA AIG?
**A:** TATA AIG: 96.5% (IRDAI 2024-25). Bajaj Allianz: 96.2%. The 0.3% difference is negligible — both are above industry average. Do not choose based on CSR alone.

### Q: Which is cheaper?
**A:** Bajaj Allianz is typically 5-10% cheaper for motor and health insurance. TATA AIG is competitive but slightly higher. Get quotes from both for your specific profile.

### Q: Which has better add-ons?
**A:** Both offer 18+ add-ons. TATA AIG is known for flexible motor add-ons (engine protect, RTI, tyre secure). Bajaj Allianz offers good health add-ons (restore, protect, health checkup). Compare add-on lists for your specific product.

### Q: Are both safe?
**A:** Yes. Bajaj Allianz: backed by Bajaj Finserv + Allianz SE (Germany). TATA AIG: backed by TATA Group + AIG (USA). Both maintain required solvency margins above 1.5x IRDAI minimum.

### Q: Can I port between Bajaj Allianz and TATA AIG?
**A:** Yes. Apply for portability 45 days before renewal. NCB and waiting period credits transfer. Get help from a POSP for smooth transition.
