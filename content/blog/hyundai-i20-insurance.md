## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Hyundai i20

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! i20 ke premium parts (LED headlamps, parametric grille, Bose speaker area body panels) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — Turbo DCT variant ke liye zaroori — waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹10,000-₹15,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Hyundai ka good service network hai, is add-on ko effective banata hai
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye i20 ke liye best value

## 📋 Hyundai i20 Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Hyundai authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Hyundai i20 Insurance

**Q: Hyundai i20 ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Hyundai i20 (1000-1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹6,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! i20 ke premium parts mahenge hain — zero dep se claim mein depreciation deduction nahi hota. Turbo variant ke liye toh must hai. Ek claim mein ₹8,000-₹20,000 bacha sakta hai.

**Q: Hyundai i20 ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. i20 ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹4,000-₹15,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: i20 Turbo DCT ka insurance zyada hota hai?**
A: Haan, Turbo DCT variant ka ex-showroom price zyada hai aur turbo engine ke parts bhi expensive hain, toh IDV aur premium dono thoda higher hota hai. Engine protection add-on bhi consider karein.
