## Quick Answer

Freelance writers need: health insurance (₹5,000-10,000/year — no employer cover), term life insurance (₹3,000-8,000/year — income replacement), professional indemnity (₹3,000-8,000/year — plagiarism/copyright claims), equipment insurance (₹1,500-3,000/year — laptop). Total: ₹12,500-29,000/year. All premiums deductible as business expenses (PI + equipment) or 80D/80C (health + life).

## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Do freelance writers need professional indemnity insurance?
**A:** Yes. PI covers: plagiarism claims, copyright infringement, defamation, negligent advice in content. One lawsuit can cost ₹2-5L. If you write for clients (agencies, brands, publications), PI is essential. Premium: ₹3,000-8,000/year for ₹10L cover.

### Q: Is my laptop covered under home insurance?
**A:** Only with "portable electronics" or "business equipment" add-on. Standard home contents exclude business laptops. Specify make, model, value. Covers theft, fire, accidental damage. Premium: ₹1,500-3,000/year for ₹1L cover.

### Q: Can freelance writers claim 80D on health insurance?
**A:** Yes, under old regime. ₹25,000 (under 60) or ₹50,000 (senior). PI and equipment insurance are business expenses under Section 37(1) — deductible from freelance income under both regimes.

### Q: Should freelancers buy term insurance?
**A:** Yes. Freelancers have no employer life cover. Term insurance (₹50L-1Cr) costs ₹3,000-8,000/year. Buy before 35 for lowest premium. 80C benefit under old regime. Your family needs income replacement regardless of employment type.

### Q: What is the minimum insurance for a freelance writer?
**A:** Health insurance (₹5L, ₹5,000/year) + Term insurance (₹50L, ₹3,000/year) = ₹8,000/year minimum. Add PI (₹4,000/year) if writing for clients. Add equipment (₹2,000/year) if laptop is essential. Total: ₹10,000-14,000/year for full protection.
