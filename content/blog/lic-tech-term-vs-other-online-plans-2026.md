## Quick Answer

LIC Tech Term is LIC's online-only term plan at 20-30% lower premium than agent-sold LIC policies. However, it is still 30-40% more expensive than private online term plans (HDFC Life, ICICI Pru, TATA AIA) for the same coverage. Choose LIC Tech Term for government trust, private plans for value pricing.

**TL;DR Summary:**

| Parameter | LIC Tech Term | Private Online Term Plans |
|---|---|---|
| Premium ₹1Cr (30yr) | ₹13,000-15,000/year | ₹9,000-12,000/year |
| Claim Settlement Ratio | 98.5% (LIC) | 97-99% (varies) |
| Online purchase | Yes (LIC website) | Yes (insurer website + aggregators) |
| Government backing | Yes | No (IRDAI-regulated) |
| Riders | Accident benefit, critical illness | More options (waiver of premium, etc.) |
| Digital claim process | Limited | Full (app + WhatsApp) |
| Solvency margin | 1.55x | 1.5-2.5x |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Choosing based on premium alone** — lower premium may mean lower coverage or higher deductibles
2. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
3. **Under-declaring information** — non-disclosure can lead to claim rejection
4. **Not comparing options** — buying from the first insurer you encounter
5. **Ignoring claim settlement record** — cheap premium from a low-CSR insurer means claim struggle

## FAQ

### Q: Is LIC Tech Term cheaper than regular LIC term insurance?
**A:** Yes, 20-30% cheaper. LIC Tech Term is the online-only version — no agent commission means lower premium. But it is still 30-40% more expensive than private online term plans.

### Q: Can I buy LIC Tech Term through an agent?
**A:** No. LIC Tech Term is online-only — you must buy it directly from LIC's website. A POSP can guide you through the process but the purchase must be online.

### Q: Which online term plan is cheapest in India?
**A:** Among reputable insurers: Aegon Life iTerm, TATA AIA Sampoorna Raksha, and HDFC Life Click 2 Protect are among the cheapest. Premium varies by age, smoking status, and sum insured. Get quotes from 3+ insurers before buying.

### Q: Is LIC Tech Term available for NRIs?
**A:** Yes. LIC Tech Term is available for NRIs (with Indian passport/OCI). Medical tests can be arranged in the NRI's country of residence (LIC has tie-ups with international diagnostic centers). Premium may be higher for NRIs in certain countries.

### Q: What is the maximum sum insured for LIC Tech Term?
**A:** No upper limit (subject to underwriting). Minimum: ₹50 lakh. Most buyers choose ₹1-2 crore. For sum insured above ₹1 crore, additional medical tests may be required.
