### Step 4: NCB Transfer Karein

**NCB = No Claim Bonus** — claim-free year pe discount milta hai. Yeh aapka RIGHT hai, insurer ka nahi!

#### NCB Slab

| Claim-Free Years | NCB Discount |
|-------------------|-------------|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 years | 25% |

#### NCB Transfer Process

1. Previous insurer se **NCB Certificate** lein
2. Naye insurer ko renewal pe NCB certificate submit karein
3. NCB discount automatically apply ho jaayega

> 💡 **Critical**: NCB aapki hai, car ki nahi! Car bechne pe bhi NCB retain hota hai — 3 saal tak valid rehta hai naye car pe use ke liye.

## Expired Policy Renewal — What To Do

Car insurance expire ho gayi? **Panic mat karein!** 90 din ke andar renewal possible hai.

### Process for Expired Policy Renewal

1. **Within 30 days**: Online renewal with same NCB — no inspection needed (most insurers)
2. **30-90 days**: Online renewal possible lekin NCB zero ho jaata hai + inspection may be required
3. **After 90 days**: Fresh policy buy karni padegi — vehicle inspection mandatory

### Risks of Lapsed Insurance

- ❌ Driving without insurance = ₹2,000-4,000 fine + imprisonment
- ❌ Accident mein koi cover nahi — sab khud pay
- ❌ Third-party liability — unlimited financial risk
- ❌ NCB lost — 50% discount gayab
- ❌ Fresh inspection required — time-consuming

## Documents Needed for Online Renewal

### For Active Policy Renewal

- Registration Certificate (RC)

### For Expired Policy Renewal

- RC copy

- Previous insurer's NCB certificate

## Money-Saving Tips for Car Insurance Renewal

1. **Compare before renewing**: Har saal same insurer se mat renew — compare karein
2. **Correct IDV insist karein**: Low IDV se mat raazi hon
3. **Voluntary deductible lein**: ₹2,500-5,000 deductible = 10-15% premium discount
4. **Anti-theft devices**: ARAI-approved device = 2-5% discount
5. **AAA membership**: Automobile association member = 5% discount
6. **NCB Protection add-on**: Ek chhota claim NCB safe rakhega
7. **Long-term policy**: 3-year third-party = premium lock, no yearly hike
8. **Bundled plans**: Some insurers offer car + health combo at discount

## FAQs

### 1. Car insurance renewal kitne din pehle karni chahiye?

**30-45 din pehle** compare karna shuru karein. Actual renewal expiry se 15-30 din pehle kar sakte hain — coverage continuously rahegi. Early renewal se NCB aur terms better negotiate ho sakte hain.

### 2. NCB kaise transfer hota hai naye insurer ko?

Previous insurer se NCB certificate lein (free hai). Naye insurer ko renewal ke time pe submit karein — discount automatically lag jaayega. NCB aapka hai, car ka nahi — car bechne pe bhi retain ho sakta hai 3 saal tak.

### 3. Expired policy online renew ho sakti hai?

Haan! 30-90 din ke andar online renewal possible hai. 30 din ke andar NCB bhi bach jaata hai. 90 din ke baad fresh policy buy karni padegi with mandatory vehicle inspection. Jaldi karein — bina insurance driving illegal hai!

### 4. IDV kya hai aur kyun important hai?

IDV = Insured Declared Value = Car ki current market value. Total theft ya total loss mein IDV ke hisaab se payment milta hai. Low IDV = low premium lekin total loss mein kam payment. Always correct IDV ensure karein.

### 5. Online renewal pe claim kaise karein?

Online renewal ke baad claim process bilkul same hai — call insurer's toll-free, ya app se intimation, cashless garage mein repair, aur reimbursement. Online policy aur offline policy mein claim process mein koi farq nahi.

### 6. Kya har saal insurer change karna chahiye?

Compare zaroor karein har saal! Agar better deal mile toh switch karein — NCB transfer ho jaayega. Lekin sirf premium ke liye mat badlein — claim settlement ratio, network garages, aur service quality bhi dekhein.
