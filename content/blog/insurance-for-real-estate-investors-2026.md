## Quick Answer

Real estate investors need: property insurance (₹3,000-15,000/year per property), tenant damage cover (₹1,000-3,000/year), loss of rent cover (₹1,500-3,000/year), public liability (₹2,000-5,000/year). One fire can destroy ₹20L-2Cr.

## Who Should Buy This?

This insurance is essential for anyone who identifies with the situations described. If you are in the relevant category, you should have the recommended coverage.

## Who Should Avoid?

If you already have comprehensive coverage that adequately covers all risks mentioned, additional coverage may not be needed. Always verify your existing cover is sufficient.

## FAQ

### Q: Do I need separate insurance for each rental property?
**A:** Yes. Each property needs own fire/structure policy. Some insurers offer portfolio policies for 5+ properties at 10-15% discount.

### Q: Does property insurance cover tenant damage?
**A:** Standard fire insurance does NOT cover tenant damage. Need tenant damage cover add-on. Excludes normal wear and tear. Premium: ₹1,000-3,000/year.

### Q: What is loss of rent insurance?
**A:** If property becomes uninhabitable (fire, flood), pays monthly rent during repairs. Premium: ₹1,500-3,000/year for ₹25,000/month cover.

### Q: Is home loan insurance mandatory?
**A:** No. Banks push it but it is NOT mandatory. Regular term insurance is cheaper and more flexible.

### Q: Can investors claim property insurance as tax deduction?
**A:** Yes. Property insurance, property tax, repairs deductible from rental income under Section 24. Reduces taxable rental income.
