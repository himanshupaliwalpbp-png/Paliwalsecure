## Quick Answer

Group health insurance (provided by employers) covers you while employed but ends when you leave the job. Individual health insurance is yours for life — you control coverage, sum insured, and insurer. Relying only on employer cover is risky: job loss = no coverage, especially dangerous if you develop a condition during employment.

**At a glance:**

| Parameter | Group Health (Employer) | Individual Health Insurance |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the policy | Employer | You (individual) |
| Coverage duration | Only while employed | Lifetime (if renewed) |
| Sum insured | ₹2-5L typical (employer decides) | ₹3L to ₹1Cr+ (you decide) |
| Waiting periods | Waived (day-one coverage) | Apply (2-4 years for pre-existing) |
| Pre-existing conditions | Covered from day one | Covered after waiting period |
| Maternity cover | Often included | Available after 2-4 year waiting period |
| Parents cover | Sometimes (extra cost) | Separate policy needed |
| Premium payment | Employer pays | You pay (80D tax benefit) |
| Customization | None (employer decides) | Full (add-ons, insurer, sum insured) |
| Portability | Cannot port | Can port to another insurer |

## Exclusions Comparison

Both Group Health (Employer) and Individual Health Insurance have standard health insurance exclusions (per IRDAI guidelines):
- Pre-existing conditions (during waiting period)
- Cosmetic/plastic surgery (unless accident-related)
- Dental treatment (unless due to accident)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- War, nuclear events, terrorism

**Plan-specific exclusions vary.** Always read the policy wording.

## Claim Process

### Cashless Claim (if applicable)
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours
2. Visit network hospital
3. Hospital sends pre-authorization
4. Insurer approves within 4-24 hours
5. Treatment proceeds (no upfront payment)

### Reimbursement Claim
1. Inform insurer within 48 hours (planned) / 7 days (emergency)
2. Get treated at any hospital
3. Submit documents within 15-30 days
4. Insurer reimburses within 7-15 working days
