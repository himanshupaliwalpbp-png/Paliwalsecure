## Essential Insurance Covers

### 1. Health Insurance
- Sum insured: ₹10-50 Lakh (depends on family size)
- Family floater for couples + children
- Individual for seniors
- Premium: ₹8,000-30,000/yr

### 2. Term Insurance
- Sum insured: 15-20× annual income
- Policy term: Until retirement (60-65 years)
- Premium: ₹15,000-50,000/yr
- Tax benefit: Section 80C

### 3. Critical Illness
- Sum insured: ₹10-50 Lakh
- Lump sum on diagnosis
- Covers cancer, heart attack, stroke
- Premium: ₹5,000-20,000/yr

### 4. Personal Accident
- Sum insured: ₹25 Lakh-1 Cr
- 24/7 worldwide cover
- Premium: ₹3,000-15,000/yr

### 5. Specialty Cover (situation-specific)
- Maternity (for expecting parents)
- Senior citizen (for elderly parents)
- Professional indemnity (for professionals)
- Travel (for students abroad)

## Situation-Specific Advice

Single parent? High term cover (₹2-5Cr) + health + child plan + will. Total ₹50K/yr. Critical — child's future depends on you alone.

### Key Actions:
1. **Review existing policies** — update nominees
2. **Increase sum insured** if responsibilities increased
3. **Add new covers** specific to situation
4. **Update will** — ensure assets distributed correctly
5. **Inform family** about all policies + documents

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not updating nominees** — ex-spouse/old nominees still listed
2. **Underinsuring** — coverage doesn't match new responsibilities
3. **Missing premium payments** — policy lapse
4. **Not informing family** — they don't know about policies
5. **Buying wrong products** — ULIP/endowment instead of term

## Tax Planning

- **Section 80D**: Health insurance premium (₹25k-50k deduction)
- **Section 80C**: Term insurance + PPF + ELSS (₹1.5L deduction)
- **Total savings**: Up to ₹67,500/year (30% bracket)
- **GST input credit**: If self-employed/business owner

## FAQ

### Q: Existing policy update karna zaroori hai?
**A**: Haan! Nominee + sum insured + contact details update karna critical hai.

### Q: Kitna term cover chahiye?
**A**: Annual income × 15-20. E.g., ₹10L income → ₹1.5-2 Cr term.

### Q: Multiple policies le sakte hain?
**A**: Haan, multiple policies allowed. Health + term + CI + accident — sab alag le sakte hain.

### Q: Tax kitna bachega?
