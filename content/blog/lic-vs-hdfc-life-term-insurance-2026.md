## Quick Answer

LIC has 98.5% CSR and government trust; HDFC Life has 98.6% CSR, 30-40% lower premiums, and superior digital experience. For term insurance specifically, HDFC Life is better value. For endowment/traditional plans, LIC's government backing is an advantage.

**TL;DR Summary:**

| Parameter | LIC | HDFC Life |
|---|---|---|
| Claim Settlement Ratio | 98.5% | 98.6% (marginally higher) |
| Premium ₹1Cr (30yr male) | ₹15,000-18,000/year | ₹10,000-12,000/year |
| Solvency margin | 1.55x | 1.8x |
| Digital experience | Limited | Excellent (app + WhatsApp claims) |
| Branch network | 2,000+ (largest in India) | 500+ (growing) |
| Claim processing | 15-30 days | 7-15 days |
| Riders available | Accident, critical illness | Accident, critical illness, waiver of premium |
| Online term plan | LIC e-Term | Click 2 Protect Super |

## FAQ

### Q: Which has a higher claim settlement ratio — LIC or HDFC Life?
**A:** HDFC Life (98.6%) is marginally higher than LIC (98.5%) per IRDAI 2024-25. The difference is 0.1% — both are excellent. Do not choose based on CSR alone; consider premium, riders, and digital experience.

### Q: Is HDFC Life as safe as LIC?
**A:** Yes. Both are IRDAI-regulated. HDFC Life has a higher solvency margin (1.8x vs 1.55x) — meaning it has more capital buffer relative to its risks. No life insurer in India has ever defaulted on valid death claims.

### Q: Which is cheaper for term insurance?
**A:** HDFC Life is 30-40% cheaper. For ₹1Cr cover (30yr male, non-smoker, 30yr term): LIC ≈ ₹17,000/year, HDFC Life ≈ ₹11,000/year. Over 30 years, HDFC Life saves ₹1.8L+.

### Q: Can I switch from LIC to HDFC Life?
**A:** Yes. Buy HDFC Life term policy first, then stop paying LIC premium (it will lapse). Do not cancel LIC before HDFC Life policy is active — you need continuous coverage. Term insurance has no surrender value.

### Q: Which is better for endowment plans — LIC or HDFC Life?
**A:** LIC. For traditional/endowment plans, LIC's government backing and 64-year track record provide comfort for 20-year commitments. HDFC Life is better for term insurance (lower premium, better digital).
