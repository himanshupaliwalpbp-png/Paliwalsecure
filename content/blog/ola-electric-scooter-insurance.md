## Best Insurance Add-ons for Ola Electric Scooter

### 1. Zero Depreciation Cover (Zaroori)

Ola scooter mein plastic parts, fiber panels, aur battery casing — sab depreciation ke subject hain. Zero dep ke bina claim mein 30-50% deduction hota hai.

- **Cost**: ₹800-₹1,500 per year
- **Benefit**: Full claim amount without depreciation deduction
- **Best for**: S1 Pro owners (higher parts cost)

### 2. Battery Cover Add-on

Warranty ke baad battery degradation cover karta hai:
- **Cost**: ₹1,000-₹2,000 per year
- **Benefit**: Battery replacement if capacity drops below 70%
- **Best for**: Scooters older than 2 years

### 3. Charging Damage Protection

Home aur public charging dono ke liye:
- **Cost**: ₹300-₹600 per year
- **Benefit**: Fire, surge, short-circuit during charging

### 4. Personal Accident Cover

- **Cost**: ₹300-₹500 per year
- **Benefit**: ₹15 Lakh coverage for death/disability
- **Mandatory**: IRDAI ke according PA cover lena mandatory hai

### 5. Return to Invoice

Scooter theft ke case mein:
- **Cost**: ₹400-₹800 per year
- **Benefit**: Full invoice value (not depreciated IDV)

## Ola Scooter Insurance Claim Process

### Step-by-Step Claim Guide

1. **Accident/Incident Hone Par**: Ola App se roadside assistance book karein + insurer ko call karein
2. **Photos Click Karein**: Damage photos 4 angles se, number plate visible
3. **FIR (Agar Chahiye)**: Theft, vandalism, ya third-party injury mein FIR mandatory hai
4. **Surveyor Visit**: Insurer surveyor bhejega — Ola service center par inspection hogi
5. **Repair Authorization**: Surveyor approval ke baad repair start hogi
6. **Battery Assessment**: Alag se battery diagnostic report chahiye hogi
7. **Claim Settlement**: Cashless (Ola authorized center) ya reimbursement

### Ola-Specific Claim Tips

- Ola service centers mein **cashless settlement** available hai most insurers ke saath
- Battery damage claim ke liye **Ola diagnostics report** zaroori hai
- Software update issue ke liye directly Ola service center contact karein — ye warranty mein aata hai
- Accident ke baad scooter ko ON mat karein — battery damage aur badh sakti hai
- Charging fire ke case mein fire department report aur electricity board report bhi chahiye hogi

## FAQs

### 1. Ola S1 Pro ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

Ola S1 Pro ka third-party premium ₹1,414 fixed hai IRDAI ki taraf se. Comprehensive insurance premium ₹3,500-₹6,500 per year hota hai IDV, add-ons, aur insurer ke hisaab se. Zero dep add-on ke saath total cost ₹4,500-₹6,500 tak jata hai.

### 2. Kya Ola ki battery warranty insurance ki jagah le sakti hai?

Nahi, battery warranty aur insurance dono alag cheezein hain. Warranty sirf manufacturing defects cover karti hai — accidental damage, theft, fire, aur natural calamity ke liye insurance zaroori hai. Warranty expire hone ke baad battery degradation bhi insurance add-on se hi cover hoti hai.

### 3. Ola scooter ka zero dep add-on lena chahiye?

Haan, bilkul! Ola scooter mein plastic parts, fiber body panels, aur display screen — sab depreciation ke subject hain. Zero dep ke bina claim settlement mein 30-50% ka deduction hota hai. ₹800-₹1,500 extra investment worth it hai S1 Pro jaise premium scooter ke liye.

### 4. Charging ke dauraan aag lage toh claim hoga?

Standard comprehensive policy mein charging fire cover nahi hota. Iske liye aapko **Charging Damage Protection** add-on lena hoga jo ₹300-₹600 per year mein milta hai. Ye add-on home charging, public charging station — dono jagah fire, surge, aur short-circuit cover karta hai.

### 5. Ola scooter chori ho jaaye toh kya milega?

Comprehensive insurance ke under theft cover hai. Aapko IDV (Insured Declared Value) mileagegi jo depreciation ke hisaab se calculate hoti hai. Agar aapke paas **Return to Invoice** add-on hai toh full invoice value mileagegi — ye add-on especially useful hai new Ola owners ke liye.

### 6. Insurance renewal ke time IDV kaise calculate hota hai?

IDV = Ex-showroom price minus depreciation. New scooter (0-6 months) ke liye 95% of ex-showroom, 6 months-1 year ke liye 85%, 1-2 years ke liye 70%, 2-3 years ke liye 55%. Ola EVs mein battery ki value separately assess hoti hai — 2+ year old scooter ke liye battery health report se IDV adjust hota hai.
