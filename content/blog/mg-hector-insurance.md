## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for MG Hector

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Hector ke premium parts (large touchscreen, panoramic sunroof, LED headlamps) replacement mein depreciation 30-50% hoti hai
2. **Engine Protection** — Turbo petrol aur diesel engine repair costs high — waterlogging aur hydrostatic lock damage cover essential
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — 50% NCB = ₹16,000-₹25,000/yr ki bachat
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — Hector ke long drives ke liye useful
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — ₹14-22 Lakh ki investment ke liye must

## 📋 MG Hector Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. MG authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — MG Hector Insurance

**Q: MG Hector ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, MG Hector (above 1500cc) ka third-party premium ₹7,500/year hai 2026 mein.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Hector ke premium parts (sunroof, large screen, sensors) mahenge hain. Zero dep se ek claim mein ₹20,000-₹50,000 tak bacha sakte hain. 5 saal se purani car nahi hai toh must hai.

**Q: MG Hector ka insurance online kaise karein?**
A: Paliwal Secure AI website pe visit karein, vehicle details dalein, plans compare karein, aur online buy karein — 5 minute mein ho jayega!

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Hector ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹6,400-₹25,000 tak bachat kar sakta hai.

**Q: Hector ka connected car feature insurance mein covered hai?**
A: Hector ka i-SMART connected car tech software-related hai, jo generally insurance mein nahi aata. Lekin physical damage (screen break, sensor damage) comprehensive policy mein covered hai.
