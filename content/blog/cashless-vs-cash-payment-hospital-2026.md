## Quick Answer

Cashless = insurer pays hospital directly, you pay only deductibles. Cash/reimbursement = you pay first, submit bills, get reimbursed in 7-15 days. Cashless is always better — no upfront payment, no cash flow stress. Verify hospital is in network before admission.

## FAQ

### Q: Is cashless hospitalization always available?
**A:** No. Only at network hospitals. If hospital not in network, pay cash and file reimbursement. Verify network BEFORE admission.

### Q: What if insurer rejects cashless authorization?
**A:** You can still get treated (pay cash) and file reimbursement. If claim is valid, reimbursement will be approved.

### Q: How long does cashless approval take?
**A:** Planned: 4-6 hours. Emergency: 24 hours. If not approved in 24 hours, pay cash and file reimbursement.

### Q: Do I pay anything in cashless hospitalization?
**A:** Yes: compulsory deductible (₹1,000-5,000), co-payment (10-20% for seniors), non-medical items, expenses beyond sum insured.

### Q: Can I switch from cashless to reimbursement?
**A:** Yes. If cashless denied, pay hospital and file reimbursement. Keep ALL original bills, discharge summary, prescriptions.
