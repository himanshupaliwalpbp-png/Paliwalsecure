## Quick Answer

When expecting a baby: (1) Check if your health insurance covers maternity (2-4 year waiting period), (2) Buy maternity cover add-on if not included, (3) Add newborn to policy within 90 days of birth (day-one coverage), (4) Buy term life insurance (income replacement for child's future), (5) Start child insurance planning (education fund). Maternity costs: ₹50,000-2L (normal delivery), ₹1-3L (C-section).

A couple bought health insurance with maternity cover 3 years BEFORE planning pregnancy (₹15L family floater, ₹12,000/year). ## Common Mistakes

1. **Buying insurance only after a loss** — insurance is prevention, not recovery
2. **Under-insuring** — buying minimum cover to save ₹2,000/year premium
3. **Not declaring pre-existing conditions** — claim rejection + policy void
4. **Choosing lowest premium only** — lower CSR = claim struggle
5. **Not reading exclusions** — assuming everything is covered
6. **Not comparing insurers** — buying from first quote received
7. **Letting policy lapse** — gap in coverage = no protection
8. **Not updating nominee/address** — claim complications
9. **Buying investment-linked insurance** — term + mutual funds is better
10. **Not consulting a certified advisor** — DIY insurance can be costly

## FAQ

### Q: Does health insurance cover maternity and delivery costs?
**A:** Yes, if your policy has maternity cover. Most comprehensive plans include maternity after a 2-4 year waiting period. Covers: normal delivery, C-section, pre/post-natal expenses, newborn baby coverage (first 90 days). If maternity is not included, buy maternity add-on or a plan with maternity built-in. Waiting period applies — buy BEFORE planning pregnancy.

### Q: How do I add my newborn to my health insurance?
**A:** Inform insurer within 90 days of birth. Newborn is added to the family floater policy from day one (no waiting period for newborn). If you miss the 90-day window, the child may need a separate policy with waiting periods. Documents needed: birth certificate, hospital discharge summary, parent's policy details.

### Q: Should I buy life insurance when having a baby?
**A:** Absolutely. A child means 18+ years of financial dependency. If you die, your child needs: living expenses, education (₹10-50L), and lifestyle maintenance. Term insurance (₹1-2Cr) costs ₹8,000-15,000/year. Update nominee to include child (or create a trust). This is the MOST important insurance decision when becoming a parent.

### Q: What is the ideal sum insured for a family with a newborn?
**A:** Minimum ₹15L family floater (covers maternity + newborn + parents). If in metro city: ₹25L+ (hospital costs higher). Consider: delivery costs (₹50,000-3L), newborn ICU (₹10,000-50,000/day if premature), vaccinations (₹15,000-25,000 first year), pediatric emergencies. Buy higher cover BEFORE pregnancy (waiting period).

### Q: Can I claim 80D on maternity expenses?
**A:** Health insurance premium (including maternity cover): yes, 80D applies. Out-of-pocket maternity expenses (hospital bills not covered by insurance): NO, 80D does not apply to medical expenses — only to insurance premium. However, some employers allow maternity expenses as medical reimbursement (tax-free up to ₹15,000 under old regime Section 17).
