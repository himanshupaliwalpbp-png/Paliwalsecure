## Key Takeaway

Travel insurance claim file karna easy hai **agar documents sahi hon** — medical emergency claim 15-30 days mein, trip cancellation 30-60 days mein, aur baggage loss 30-45 days mein process hota hai. Key steps: insurer ko 24-48 hours mein inform karein, sab documents collect karein, claim form bharein, aur follow-up karein. Cashless treatment available hai network hospitals mein — India aur abroad dono!

## Types of Travel Insurance Claims

Travel insurance ke 5 main claim types hain — har ek ka process thoda alag hai:

### Claim Types Overview

| Claim Type | Processing Time | Approval Rate | Complexity |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|-----------|
| Medical Emergency | 15-30 days | 85-90% | Medium |
| Trip Cancellation | 30-60 days | 75-85% | High |
| Baggage Loss/Delay | 30-45 days | 80-85% | Medium |
| Flight Delay | 15-30 days | 90-95% | Low |
| Personal Liability | 45-90 days | 60-70% | High |

## 1. Medical Emergency Claim Abroad

Medical emergency abroad — yeh sabse critical claim type hai. India se abroad jaane wale yeh claim sabse zyada file karte hain.

### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Immediate Action (Within 24-48 Hours)**
- Insurer ki 24/7 helpline call karein (policy document mein number hai)
- Insurance details provide karein: Policy number, passport number, emergency details
- Pre-authorization lein hospital admission se pehle (if possible)

**Step 2: During Treatment**
- Hospital ko insurance details dein for cashless treatment
- Agar cashless available nahi hai toh bills khud pay karein — reimbursement later
- Sab medical reports, bills, discharge summary collect karein

**Step 3: Document Collection**
- Original hospital bills with itemized breakdown
- Discharge summary / medical report
- Doctor's prescription and reports
- Pharmacy bills with prescriptions
- Passport copy (with entry/exit stamps)
- Visa copy
- Boarding pass / flight tickets
- FIR / police report (if accident)

**Step 4: Claim Filing**
- Claim form bharein (insurer ki website se download)
- Sab documents attach karein
- 30-90 days ke andar submit karein (check your policy timeline)

### Medical Emergency Claim Documents Checklist

| Document | Mandatory? | Source |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Claim Form | ✅ Yes | Insurer website |
| Original Bills | ✅ Yes | Hospital |
| Discharge Summary | ✅ Yes | Hospital |
| Doctor's Report | ✅ Yes | Treating doctor |
| Prescription | ✅ Yes | Doctor |
| Passport Copy | ✅ Yes | Your passport |
| Visa Copy | ✅ Yes | Your visa |
| Boarding Pass | ✅ Yes | Airline |
| FIR (if accident) | ⚠️ If applicable | Local police |
| Currency Conversion | ✅ Yes | Bank/Forex |

### Common Medical Emergency Scenarios

| Scenario | Average Claim | Tips |
|----------|-------------|------|
| Heart Attack (USA) | ₹15-50 lakh | Call helpline immediately |
| Accident (Europe) | ₹5-15 lakh | Get police report |
| Dengue/Fever (SE Asia) | ₹1-3 lakh | Keep all OPD bills |
| Food Poisoning | ₹50,000-2 lakh | Doctor's report must |
| Dental Emergency | ₹30,000-1 lakh | Check if dental covered |

> ⚠️ **USA Warning**: USA mein medical treatment bahut expensive hai — emergency room visit ₹2-5 lakh, surgery ₹15-50 lakh. Always take adequate coverage for USA travel!

## 2. Trip Cancellation Claim

Trip cancel karni padi? Insurance se claim le sakte hain — lekin reason valid hona chahiye.

### Valid Reasons for Trip Cancellation

| Reason | Covered? | Documentation Needed |
|--------|---------|---------------------|
| Death in family | ✅ Yes | Death certificate |
| Your medical emergency | ✅ Yes | Doctor's certificate |
| Family member hospitalization | ✅ Yes | Hospital records |
| Natural disaster at destination | ✅ Yes | News reports, advisory |
| Terrorist incident | ✅ Yes | Government advisory |
| Job loss (within 90 days) | ⚠️ Check policy | Termination letter |
| Visa rejection | ⚠️ Check policy | Rejection letter |
| Change of mind | ❌ No | N/A |
| Business meeting cancelled | ❌ No | N/A |
| Friend cancelled trip | ❌ No | N/A |

### Trip Cancellation Claim Process

1. **Trip cancel karte hi insurer ko inform karein** — 24-48 hours mein
2. **Reason ki documentation collect karein** — doctor's certificate, death certificate, etc.
3. **Original booking receipts** — flight, hotel, tour bookings
4. **Refund evidence** — airline/hotel ne kitna refund kiya, kitna loss hua
5. **Claim form submit karein** with all documents

### Trip Cancellation Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Cancellation claim form | Insurer's standard form |
| Original booking invoices | Prove booking amount |
| Cancellation invoices | Prove cancellation charges |
| Doctor's certificate / Death certificate | Prove cancellation reason |
| Airline refund confirmation | Prove how much refunded |
| Hotel cancellation policy | Prove non-refundable amount |

## 3. Baggage Loss / Delay Claim

### Baggage Lost vs Delayed — Difference Important Hai

| Type | Definition | Claim Timeline |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Baggage Delay | Baggage late aaya (usually 12+ hours) | 24 hours of delay |
| Baggage Loss | Baggage completely lost | 21-30 days after airline confirms loss |

### Baggage Loss Claim Process

1. **Airport pe hi report karein** — airline counter pe Property Irregularity Report (PIR) file karein
2. **Insurer ko inform karein** — 24-48 hours mein
3. **Airline se written confirmation** lein — loss/delay acceptance letter
4. **Contents ki list banayein** — kya kya tha baggage mein with approximate values
5. **Original bills** — expensive items ke (if available)
6. **Claim form submit karein**

### Baggage Claim Documents

| Document | Source |
|----------|--------|
| PIR (Property Irregularity Report) | Airline at airport |
| Airline's loss/delay confirmation | Airline (written) |
| Baggage tag copy | Your boarding pass/luggage tag |
| Contents list with values | You (self-declaration) |
| Purchase bills (if available) | Original receipts |
| Boarding pass | Airline |
| Claim form | Insurer |

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Baggage delay claim mein immediate essentials ke liye reimbursement milta hai — clothes, toiletries. ₹5,000-15,000 tak claim kar sakte hain. Bills zaroor rakhein!

## Common Reasons for Claim Rejection

1. ❌ **Late intimation** — 24-48 hours mein inform nahi kiya
2. ❌ **Incomplete documents** — important bills/reports missing
3. ❌ **Pre-existing condition** — medical emergency PED se related hai
4. ❌ **Excluded activity** — adventure sports, DUI, etc.
5. ❌ **False claims** — inflated bills, fake documents
6. ❌ **Policy conditions not met** — mandatory deductibles, sub-limits

## Pro Tips for Successful Travel Insurance Claims

1. **24/7 helpline number save karein** phone mein before travel
2. **Policy document carry karein** — physical + digital copy
3. **Har expense ka bill rakhein** — even ₹500 ka bhi
4. **Photos lein** — damaged baggage, accident scene, etc.
5. **Police report zaroor file karein** — theft, accident, baggage loss
6. **Airline documentation** — PIR, delay certificate, cancellation proof
7. **Email trail maintain karein** — insurer se sab communication save karein
8. **Original bills submit karein** — photocopies not accepted usually
9. **Currency conversion record** — bank statement for exchange rate
10. **Follow-up karein** — 7-10 days mein ek baar status check karein

## FAQs

### 1. Travel insurance claim kitne din mein process hoti hai?

Medical emergency claims 15-30 days mein, trip cancellation 30-60 days mein, aur baggage loss 30-45 days mein process hoti hai. Complete documents hone pe faster processing hoti hai. Incomplete documents se delay hota hai.

### 2. Medical emergency abroad mein cashless treatment milega?

Haan, zyaadatar insurers ke global network hain jahan cashless treatment available hai. Insurer ki 24/7 helpline call karein — wo nearest network hospital bata denge. USA, Europe, SE Asia mein achha network hai. Agar cashless available nahi hai toh reimbursement claim file karein.

### 3. Trip cancellation ke liye kaunse reasons valid hain?

Death in family, aapki medical emergency, family member ki hospitalization, natural disaster destination pe, terrorist incident — yeh sab valid reasons hain. "Change of mind" ya "friend ne cancel kiya" — NOT valid. Doctor's certificate ya death certificate zaroor chahiye hoga.

### 4. Baggage loss claim ke liye kya karna padega?

Airport pe hi airline counter pe Property Irregularity Report (PIR) file karein. 21-30 days mein airline confirm karegi loss. Insurer ko 24-48 hours mein inform karein. Baggage contents ki list aur original bills (if available) submit karein.

### 5. Agar claim reject ho gayi toh kya karu?

Rejection ki reason samjhein — insurer se detailed letter maangein. Agar reason incomplete documents hai toh additional documents submit karein. Agar unfair rejection hai toh insurer ki grievance cell mein complaint karein, phir IRDAI ombudsman approach karein. Har rejection ki written reason leni hai aapka RIGHT.

### 6. Travel insurance claim ke liye India mein kahan submit karein?

Zyaadatar insurers online claim submission allow karte hain — website ya app pe. Some require physical documents courier se bhejne. Check karein aapke insurer ki process. Pehle online intimation, phir documents submit — yeh standard process hai.
