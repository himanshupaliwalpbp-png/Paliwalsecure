## COVID-19 and Health Insurance: What You Need to Know

### Is COVID-19 Covered?
Yes, COVID-19 is covered under health insurance in India. However, most insurers impose a **waiting period** of 2-4 years before covid-19-related claims are eligible.

### Types of Waiting Periods

| Waiting Period | What It Means | Typical Duration |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Initial Waiting | No claims except accidents | 30-90 days |
| PED Waiting | Pre-existing diseases (including COVID-19) | 2-4 years |
| Specific Disease Waiting | COVID-19-specific treatments | 1-2 years |

### IRDAI Guidelines on COVID-19
IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) mandates:
- All health insurers must cover COVID-19 after waiting period
- Waiting period cannot exceed 4 years for any disease
- After 5 continuous years of coverage (moratorium), no claim can be rejected for non-disclosure (except fraud)

## How to Buy COVID-19 Health Insurance

### Step 1: Declare Your COVID-19 Status
Honest disclosure is critical. Declare:
- When covid-19 was diagnosed
- Current medication/treatment
- Doctor's reports
- Latest test results

### Step 2: Choose Plan with Shortest Waiting Period
Star Health offers 1-year waiting. Care Health offers 2-year. Avoid plans with 4-year waiting.

### Step 3: Get Medical Test Done
Most insurers require medical test for covid-19 patients. Tests may include:
- Relevant diagnostic tests
- General health checkup

### Step 4: Pay Extra Premium (if applicable)
COVID-19 patients may pay 10-30% higher premium. This is standard loading.

### Step 5: Complete Waiting Period
During waiting period:
- Accidents covered
- Non-covid-19 diseases covered
- COVID-19-related claims NOT covered (unless add-on purchased)

## COVID-19 Coverage: What's Included

### Covered (after waiting period)
- Hospitalization due to covid-19
- covid-19 related surgeries/procedures
- Pre and post hospitalization (60/90 days)
- Day care procedures
- Medicines during hospitalization
- Diagnostic tests

### NOT Covered
- COVID-19 during waiting period (unless add-on)
- OPD consultation (unless OPD add-on)
- Alternative medicine (except AYUSH)
- Cosmetic treatment for covid-19 (if any)
