## EV-Specific Insurance Considerations

### 1. Battery Cover (Critical)
Ather 450X battery = 40-50% of vehicle cost. Standard comprehensive covers battery against:
- Accident damage ✅
- Fire ✅
- Theft ✅
- Natural calamity ✅
- **Wear and tear** ❌ (not covered)

**Tip:** Buy "Battery Depreciation Cover" add-on to cover battery wear.

### 2. Charging Cable Cover
Ather 450X charging cable costs ₹2,400-₹4,000. Verify it's included in IDV. Buy "Accessories Cover" if not.
