## What is Insurance Agent?

An insurance agent represents ONE insurance company. They can only sell products from that insurer. Agents are licensed by IRDAI and earn commission from their insurer.

## What is Insurance Broker?

An insurance broker represents the CLIENT, not the insurer. Brokers are licensed by IRDAI to sell products from multiple insurers. They can compare plans objectively and help with claims across insurers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is an insurance broker more expensive than an agent?
**A:** No. Brokers and POSPs are paid by the insurer you choose, not by you. The premium is the same whether you buy from an agent, broker, or directly from the insurer. The difference is that a broker can show you multiple options.

### Q: Can a broker help with claims?
**A:** Yes. Brokers and POSPs can assist with claims across all insurers they represent. A captive agent can only help with their one insurer. At Paliwal Secure, we assist with claims for ALL 51+ insurers we work with.
