## FAQs

### 1. Tata Curvv ka insurance premium kitna lagta hai?

ICE variants ke liye ₹18,000-35,000/year (comprehensive with zero dep) aur EV variants ke liye ₹22,000-42,000/year. Premium variant, IDV, add-ons, aur city pe depend karta hai. Metro cities mein thoda zyada, Tier 2 mein kam.

### 2. EV Curvv ka insurance ICE se zyada kyun hai?

EV ka ex-showroom price zyada hai → IDV zyada → premium zyada. Lekin EV ka third-party premium 15-20% KAM hai. Overall comprehensive premium 15-20% zyada hai EV mein. Battery cover add-on extra lein toh aur 10-15% badh jaata hai.

### 3. Tata Curvv ke liye kaunsa insurance company best hai?

**Tata AIG** best choice hai — Tata group company, Tata service centers se direct integration, aur Tata-specific parts ke liye smooth claim experience. Alternative: HDFC ERGO (fastest claims) ya Digit (lowest premium).

### 4. Zero depreciation add-on zaroori hai Curvv ke liye?

Haan, bilkul zaroori! Curvv ki parts premium hain — LED headlamps ₹30,000-50,000, sensors ₹15,000-25,000, body panels ₹20,000-40,000. Bina zero dep ke depreciation lagane ke baad aapko 40-60% khud pay karna padega.

### 5. EV battery insurance mein covered hai?

Comprehensive insurance mein battery accidental damage covered hai. Lekin normal degradation/wear & tear covered nahi. Battery-specific add-on lein — yeh degradation bhi cover karta hai (terms apply). Tata ka battery warranty 8 years/1.6 lakh km hai, lekin insurance warranty se alag hai.

### 6. Curvv Accomplished+ ke liye kya special add-ons chahiye?

Accomplished+ mein ADAS sensors, 360° camera, premium smart key, aur ventilated seats hain. Sensor cover, key replacement, aur tyre protect add-ons zaroor lein — yeh parts bahut expensive replace karne hain. Return to Invoice bhi lein kyunki yeh premium variant hai.
