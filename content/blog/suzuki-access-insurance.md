## ⭐ Best Add-Ons for Suzuki Access 125

1. **Zero Depreciation Cover** — Sabse important! Access 125 ke plastic parts (mudguard, side panel, headlamp) replacement mein depreciation 50% tak hoti hai — zero dep se poora claim milega
2. **Personal Accident Cover** — ₹15 Lakh coverage (owner-driver) — IRDAI ke according mandatory hai
3. **NCB Protector** — Ek claim ke baad bhi NCB safe rahega — claim-free years ka discount preserved rahega
4. **Roadside Assistance** — 24/7 emergency help — towing, flat tire, battery jumpstart
5. **Return to Invoice** — Total loss/theft mein poora invoice value milega — naye Access ke liye useful

## 📋 Suzuki Access 125 Claim Process

### Cashless Claim:
1. Suzuki authorized service center mein le jaayein
2. Insurance company ko inform karein (24-48 hours)
3. Surveyor assess karega damage
4. Repair start — bill directly insurer pay karega
5. Sirf depreciation/excess ka payment karein

### Reimbursement Claim:
1. Kisi bhi authorized workshop mein repair karwaein
2. Original bills aur documents save karein
3. Claim form bharein aur documents submit karein
4. 15-30 din mein amount bank mein transfer hoga

## ❓ FAQ — Suzuki Access 125 Insurance

**Q: Suzuki Access 125 ka third-party insurance kitna hai?**
A: IRDAI fixed rates ke according, Suzuki Access 125 (75cc-150cc) ka third-party premium ₹1,400/year hai 2026 mein. 5-year long-term TP policy ₹7,649 mein bhi available hai.

**Q: Kya zero depreciation add-on lena chahiye?**
A: Haan! Access 125 ke plastic parts replacement mein zero dep ke bina 30-50% depreciation deduct hoti hai. ₹300-₹700/year mein ek claim mein ₹1,500-₹3,500 tak bacha sakte hain.

**Q: Suzuki Access 125 ka insurance online kaise karein?**

**Q: NCB kya hai aur kaise kaam karta hai?**
A: No Claim Bonus (NCB) ek discount hai jo claim-free year pe milta hai — 20% se 50% tak. Access 125 ke comprehensive insurance mein NCB lagbhag ₹700-₹2,750 tak bachat kar sakta hai.
