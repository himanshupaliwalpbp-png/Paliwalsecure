## FAQ

### Q: Are IDBI Federal and Aviva Life reliable?
**A:** Yes. Both are IRDAI-licensed and maintain required solvency margins (1.62x and 1.58x respectively, above the 1.5x minimum). IDBI Federal is backed by Ageas (Belgium's largest insurer). Aviva is now majority-owned by the Burman Group (Dabur family).

### Q: Which has a better CSR?
**A:** IDBI Federal (98.3%) has a higher CSR than Aviva (97.2%) per IRDAI 2024-25. Both are above the industry average. The 1.1% difference is not significant enough to be the sole deciding factor.

### Q: Which is cheaper?
**A:** Premiums are comparable — Aviva is typically 3-5% cheaper for the same profile. However, the difference varies by age, smoking status, and sum insured. Get quotes from both before deciding.

### Q: Can I port between IDBI Federal and Aviva?
**A:** No. Life insurance portability does not exist in India (unlike health insurance). You can buy a new policy from the other insurer and stop paying the old one (it will lapse). Term insurance has no surrender value.

### Q: Should I buy from a smaller insurer to save premium?
**A:** Yes, if the insurer has: (1) CSR above 95%, (2) solvency margin above 1.5x, (3) adequate claim processing infrastructure. Both IDBI Federal and Aviva meet these criteria. Smaller insurers often offer 5-15% lower premiums.
