## FAQ

### Q: What is Ayushman Bharat and who is eligible?
**A:** Free health insurance up to ₹5L per family. Check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in or call 14555. Cashless at empaneled hospitals. Cost: ₹0 (government-funded).

### Q: How do I enroll in PMJJBY?
**A:** Visit bank branch, fill consent form. Premium ₹436/year auto-debited June 1st. Cover ₹2L on death. Age 18-50. Need savings account + Aadhaar.

### Q: What is PMSBY?
**A:** Accident insurance: ₹2L death/permanent disability, ₹1L partial. Premium ₹20/year. Age 18-70. Enroll at bank. Cheapest insurance in India.

### Q: Can low-income families get free health insurance?
**A:** Yes. Ayushman Bharat provides ₹5L free. Check eligibility. If not eligible, state government schemes or cheapest private (₹3L for ₹3,000-5,000/year).

### Q: What if I cannot afford any insurance?
**A:** Enroll in PMSBY (₹20) + PMJJBY (₹436) = ₹456/year for ₹9L coverage. If eligible, add Ayushman Bharat (free ₹5L). Total ₹9L+ coverage for ₹456/year.
