### Cashless Claim Steps

1. **Accident/Incident**: Insurer helpline call karein within 24-48 hours

### Key Claim Tips for BE 6

## FAQs

### 1. Mahindra BE 6 ka insurance kitna lagta hai?

BE 6 ka comprehensive insurance ₹18,000-₹35,000 per year hota hai variant ke hisaab se. Pack One (₹18.9L) ke liye ₹18,000-₹24,000, Pack Three (₹24.9L) ke liye ₹24,000-₹35,000. Zero dep aur battery cover ke saath total ₹22,000-₹40,000 tak jata hai. Third-party premium ₹6,400 fixed hai with EV discount.

### 2. BE 6 ki battery insurance se cover hoti hai?

Standard comprehensive policy mein battery accidental damage se covered hai. Lekin battery degradation (normal wear) cover nahi hota — iske liye **Battery Cover Add-on** lena padta hai. BE 6 ki battery replacement ₹3-5 Lakh ki hai, isliye ye add-on ₹2,000-₹4,000/year mein lena very wise decision hai.

### 3. Kya Section 80EEB ka tax benefit BE 6 par apply hota hai?

Haan! Agar aapne BE 6 ke liye loan liya hai, toh Section 80EEB ke under loan interest par ₹1.5 Lakh tak deduction claim kar sakte hain. Ye deduction old tax regime mein available hai. BE 6 jaise expensive EV ke liye ye significant tax saving hai — roughly ₹45,000 tax saved ho sakta hai.

### 4. BE 6 ke liye kaunsa add-on sabse zaroori hai?

**Battery Cover Add-on** aur **Zero Depreciation** — ye dono sabse critical hain. Battery cover kyunki battery replacement ₹3-5 Lakh ki hai, aur zero dep kyunki BE 6 ke parts bahut expensive hain (bumper ₹25K-₹40K, headlamp ₹15K-₹25K). Bina inke claim mein heavy loss ho sakta hai.

### 5. Mahindra warranty aur insurance mein kya difference hai?

Mahindra warranty (8 years/1.6 lakh km battery) manufacturing defects cover karta hai. Insurance accidental damage, theft, fire, flood, third-party liability cover karta hai. Warranty free mein aata hai, insurance premium pay karni padti hai. Dono alag hain aur dono zaroori hain — warranty insurance ki jagah nahi le sakta.

### 6. BE 6 insurance renewal ke time kya dhyan rakhna chahiye?

Renewal ke time: IDV properly verify karein (battery depreciation alag hai), battery cover add-on zaroor add karein, NCB preserve karein, 3-4 insurers compare karein, aur Mahindra service center cashless tie-up wali company choose karein. 30 days pehle renewal start karein for best rates.
